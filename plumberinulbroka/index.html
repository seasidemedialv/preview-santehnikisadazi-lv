Plumber in Ulbroka - flats, private houses and the new parts of the village

A plumber in Ulbroka works in two different settings. The core of the village has blocks of flats built in Soviet times, for example on Institūta Street, where the work is inside the flat: blockages, toilets, radiators and heated towel rails.

Since 2023 Ulbroka also includes Vālodze, Jaunbrunava and Biķeri, and there houses typically need a connection to the street network or work on the septic tank outlet.

Blocks of flats: what is the flat's part and what is the building's

In Soviet times Ulbroka's population grew from 929 (1970) to 3,060 (1989), which coincides with the building of the block-of-flats village. On Institūta Street there are buildings with several dozen flats each, for example 13 and 29 Institūta Street. In such buildings the pipes, risers and heating system are shared by everyone.

Job What is done in the flat What must be agreed
A blocked kitchen sink cleaning the trap and the outlet up to the riser with a drain snake if the blockage is in the riser, report it to the manager
A toilet leaks or must be replaced cistern mechanism or a new toilet usually nothing, if you have your own shut-off valve
Replacing a radiator or a heated towel rail removal, fixing the new one, connecting it shutting off and refilling the riser with the manager

In a block of flats, the price for a radiator or a heated towel rail does not include shutting off the riser: the manager does that, and you agree the time beforehand. If a kitchen sink blocks repeatedly, the reason is often grease and coffee grounds on the pipe walls; it is better to put them in the rubbish, and a strainer in the sink helps.

Vālodze, Jaunbrunava and Biķeri after 2023

In September 2023, Ulbroka's boundaries were extended by adding Vālodze, Jaunbrunava and the northern part of Dreiliņi village, or Biķeri. The population grew from 3,035 (2022) to 6,084 (2026), and by this figure Ulbroka is the largest village in Latvia. This means that under one village name there are now both flats and streets of private houses.

In a private house, you first need to find out where the water and wastewater go: to the street network, or to your own borehole and septic tank. In the drinking water reports of “Garkalnes inženiertīkli” for Stopiņi Parish, Vālodze is also mentioned. If a house still uses a septic tank, the regulations of Ropaži Municipality require it to be registered and emptied at least once a year.

Water and sewerage after 2024

Earlier, water supply and sewerage in Stopiņi Parish were provided by the municipal agency “Saimnieks”, and since autumn 2024 this has been done by the municipal company SIA “Garkalnes inženiertīkli”. The company publishes drinking water reports for Ulbroka (sampling point at 36A Institūta Street) and states that the water meets Cabinet Regulation No. 547. In Stopiņi Parish the water tariff is €1.38/m³ and the sewerage tariff €1.85/m³ excluding VAT (municipal decision No. 3380, 10.07.2024).

The reports have no water hardness figures, so judge limescale in the water heater and in tap aerators by what you see. If the flow from a tap has become uneven, the aerator is usually clogged with limescale; you can unscrew and clean it yourself. If limescale builds up quickly in the water heater, water heater cleaning helps.

A radiator in a flat does not heat evenly

If the flat is connected to the building's heating, the radiator's performance is affected both by the valves in your flat and by the whole building's system.

The radiator can show the reason. If the top is cooler than the bottom, it usually has air in it. A radiator is bled when the heating is off and the radiator has cooled: the key is turned until the hissing stops and water starts to flow evenly. In a flat with the building's heating, this is done in agreement with the manager. If the top is warm but the bottom is cold, the cause is usually sludge, and bleeding will not help. If several flats on one riser are cold, report it to the manager. More: radiator not heating.

Connection of a private house and municipal support

If the “Garkalnes inženiertīkli” main network is at your property, Ropaži Municipality may co-finance half of the cost of connecting a dwelling house. The support is given for the external part, not for the internal networks, and the work must be finished within 6 months after the contract. So the inside of the house must be prepared in good time: the drain outlet must be redirected to the connection point and the water supply switched from the hydrophore to the network.

Binding regulations No. 34/23 of Ropaži Municipality, in force from 01.01.2024; binding regulations No. 45/22 on decentralised sewerage. Checked 13.11.2026. In more detail: connecting to the central sewer.

Roads to Ulbroka

Ulbroka lies at the junction of the P4 (Riga–Ērgļi) and P5 (Ulbroka–Ogre) roads, about 11 km from the centre of Riga, and is the administrative centre of Ropaži Municipality. Bus routes 51 and 6 run from Riga to Ulbroka.

Frequently asked questions

My neighbour below says water is running from their ceiling. What should I do?

Close the water valve in your flat and, if the water does not stop running, report it to the building manager so the riser can be shut off. Then describe in the form where the dampness is, and the plumber will look for the leak in your supply pipes or appliances.

Why do I have to contact the manager to replace a radiator?

In a block of flats the radiator is connected to the shared riser. To replace it, the riser must be shut off and refilled after the work, and the manager does that. This part is not included in the price of replacing a radiator.

I live in Biķeri. Is that Ulbroka or Dreiliņi?

Since September 2023 the northern part of Dreiliņi village, or Biķeri, has been included in Ulbroka. In the form, enter your address as it appears in your documents.

Do I need to check anything after bleeding a radiator?

In a private house with its own boiler, the pressure in the system usually drops after bleeding, so look at the boiler's pressure gauge and, if needed, top up the system. In a flat with the building's heating, the manager checks this.
