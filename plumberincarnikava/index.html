Plumber in Carnikava - house connection and cellars by the Gauja

A plumber in Carnikava is most often needed in private houses, because detached houses make up most of the built-up area here, although there are also multi-storey blocks.

Several streets have only recently received a central water main and sewer, so the job is often switching the house’s indoor system from a well and septic tank to the street network. The second typical reason is the mouth of the Gauja: in the lowest places in spring you have to think about the cellar, pumps and a backflow valve.

The new street networks: what moving from a well and septic tank means

In the project “Development of water management services in Carnikava, phase III”, a water main and sewer were built, for example, on Mazā Gaujas, Līņu, Vimbu, Mežrožu, Ceriņu, Draudzības, Cīrulīšu, Kokgaujas and Dambja streets. If your house is by such a new network, the Ādaži Municipality regulations require you to connect within one year after the main networks are put into operation, if the territory is planned for the central system.

The applicant builds the connection and the meter assembly at their own expense with a contractor entitled to do this work, under the operator’s supervision, and SIA “Ādažu ūdens” issues the technical conditions free of charge within 20 working days. The former Carnikava Municipality co-funding for connections was cancelled from 10 May 2022 because the budget had no money for it, so count on financing the connection yourself.

Inside the house, the move means several jobs. The water inlet after the meter is joined to the house’s existing system, the hydrophore (a pump with a pressure tank) is disconnected or left only for watering the garden, and the drain outlet is moved from the septic tank to the new connection chamber. A plumber can do this house side; the street connection and the inspection chamber are built to the operator’s technical conditions.

Ādaži Municipality binding regulations No. 72/2022 (amended by No. 11/2026): connect within one year, technical conditions free of charge within 20 working days; co-funding cancelled by No. 24/2022. Checked 13.11.2026.

Floods, ice jams and water in the cellar

On 2 March 2021, an ice jam in the Gauja by the railway and pedestrian bridge raised the water by about a metre, and six houses on the bank without a dyke were flooded. The municipality maintains polders and drainage systems, but heavy rainfall and storms sometimes cause local floods. If your house has a cellar or a utility room below ground level, the system has to be planned for such cases.

The building code LBN 221-15 requires that if a sanitary appliance is below the cover of the nearest inspection chamber, it must be connected with a separate outlet. It also needs a device that does not let the external sewer flood the cellar, that is, a backflow valve. If groundwater collects in the cellar, a drainage pump in a pit with a float switch and a non-return valve in the discharge pipe helps.

  • The backflow valve manufacturer KESSEL advises the user to check it once a month by opening and closing the emergency lid.
  • After a flood, do not use electrical appliances until an electrician has checked that it is safe; this applies to the water heater and the pump as well.
  • If the boiler room was flooded, the water heater’s safety valve and the heating circuit pressure are checked before starting up.

Old summer cottages that people now live in all year round

At the start of the 20th century Carnikava became a summer retreat for Riga residents, and after independence it became one of the fastest-growing villages in the Riga suburban area. If your house was originally built for summer, the water pipe tends to be laid shallow, without insulation, and the pipes run through an unheated veranda or the floor void.

In such a house the plumber checks where the pipe crosses cold places and offers either to move the pipes into a warm zone or to insulate them with no gaps at bends and valves. If the house will stand empty in winter, the incoming supply valve is closed and the system drained; how to do this properly is described on the page about winterising a summer house.

The meter assembly and the incoming supply after the change of operator

By 31 October 2024, SIA “Ādažu ūdens” took over Carnikava’s water management from the agency “Carnikavas komunālserviss”, and residents had to switch their contracts. It is not only the sender of the bill that changes: it can be a reason to check the meter assembly and its valves.

The municipality’s regulations allow a meter to be placed in a heated room no further than 2 m from the outer wall if it cannot go in the standard place. If the meter turns even when all the taps are closed, the leak can be in the underground incoming supply: the signs are damp patches, a sunken lawn or unusually green grass in dry weather.

The solid-fuel boiler and the heating circuit

In the village development plan, residents cite solid-fuel smoke in the cold months as a problem. If your house has a wood-fired boiler, what matters for its system is the circulation pump, the safety valve and clean heating water. If a radiator is warm at the top but cold at the bottom, the cause is usually sludge, and then you need heating system flushing, not bleeding.

Frequently asked questions

Carnikava has been a town since 2026. Do I have to write anything differently in the request?

Since 22.08.2026 Carnikava has been a town in Ādaži Municipality, and addresses are written “Carnikava, Ādažu novads” (Ādaži Municipality), not “Carnikavas pagasts” (Carnikava Parish). Street names and house numbers stay the same, so in the form simply enter the street and house number. Kalngale, Garciems and Lilaste are separate villages of Carnikava Parish; enter them under their own names too.

A sewer was built in our street. Does the old septic tank have to be dug up?

The plumber does not take that decision: it is set by the technical conditions and the construction procedure. Our part is the inside of the house and the outlet to the connection point. While the septic tank is still in use, the Ādaži Municipality regulations require it to be emptied at least once a year and the system to be registered with the agency “Carnikavas komunālserviss”.

After a flood, can I switch the water heater and the pump on straight away?

No. First an electrician has to check whether the equipment affected by water may be used. After that the plumber checks the water heater’s safety valve, the pump’s non-return valve and whether any mud has been left in the drains.

Do you also work in flats in multi-storey blocks?

Yes, in a flat it is usually a blocked sink or bath, a mixer tap, a toilet or a water heater. If a riser has to be shut off, the building manager does that, and you arrange it beforehand.
