Plumber in Dreiliņi - blocks of flats and private houses

A plumber in Dreiliņi works in both flats and private houses. Riga’s Dreiliņi neighbourhood has blocks of flats and mixed-use areas, while beyond the city boundary, in the village of Dreiliņi in Stopiņi Parish, districts of private houses grew up in the 2000s.

In flats the drains more often need cleaning and taps or water heaters replacing, while in private houses the water system needs maintenance and the connections need sorting out.

A city neighbourhood and a Stopiņi Parish village

Riga’s Dreiliņi neighbourhood covers 415.5 ha, and in 2025 it had 7,325 residents. Detached-house areas take up 14.9 ha, mainly in the north-east between Kaivas and Biķernieku streets, while mixed-use areas take up 177.4 ha. The biggest traffic junction is the Kaivas Street roundabout.

The village of Dreiliņi in Stopiņi Parish, Ropaži Municipality, lies by the eastern boundary of Riga, south of the P4. It grew fast in the 2000s with private-house development: in 1989 it had 250 residents, in 2011 1,766. In 2023 the northern part (Biķeri) and Jaunbrunava were joined to Ulbroka, and in 2025 the village had 2,101 residents. The village includes the Saulīši private-house estate.

  • On the Riga side, water supply and sewerage are served by SIA “Rīgas ūdens”.
  • In Saulīši the service was formerly provided by SIA “Ciemats Saulīši” (tariffs approved in 2013); see your bill for the current operator.
  • The rest of Stopiņi Parish has been served by SIA “Garkalnes inženiertīkli” since autumn 2024.

If you do not know who your water supplier is, look at the bill and write it in the form.

Series 602 blocks and plumbing in a flat

In 1990 construction of series 602 blocks began in Dreiliņi, but in 1993 construction stopped, leaving an unfinished nine-storey complex. Flat owners usually call a plumber for things inside their own flat.

  • The kitchen sink drains slowly: fat, oil and coffee grounds settle on the pipe walls; they should go in the rubbish, and a food-waste strainer in the sink helps.
  • A tap drips even when closed: usually the fault is a damaged or limescaled ceramic cartridge, which can be replaced.
  • The washing machine spills water on the floor: the manufacturer (Bosch) specifies for the drain hose a height of 60–100 cm from the floor to the drain, and the end of the hose must not be submerged in water.
  • A water heater drips from the safety valve: during heating this is normal, but if no water is being used and the water heater is not heating, dripping points to too high a pressure or a faulty valve.

If the blockage is in the common riser, it affects the neighbours too, and then the building manager has to be told. The plumber clears the flat’s part up to the riser with a drain snake; if that does not help, the line is flushed with a high-pressure unit.

When the tap suddenly runs cloudy

From 29 September to 2 October 2026, SIA “Rīgas ūdens” flushed 8.5 km of water main from Cēsu Street to the outlet at the Dreiliņi roundabout, and in Dreiliņi the water could be cloudy. During such flushing, sediment also gets into the house pipes.

Afterwards it is usually enough to run the cold water until it is clear and to rinse the tap aerators. If after a few days the flow is still weak at only one tap, the fault is usually in its strainer or cartridge. If only the hot water is cloudy, the sediment can be at the bottom of the water heater, and it is removed during water heater servicing.

Groundwater, the Piķurga and the former Hausmaņa purvs

Groundwater in the neighbourhood is usually at a depth of 1.5–3 m. In the corner of the former Hausmaņa purvs bog (the former landfill by Augusta Deglava Street) there is peat 3–8 m thick and poor building conditions. The neighbourhood is crossed by the Piķurga and the Dreiliņupīte, which is piped under Hipokrāta and S. Eizenšteina streets.

For a house with a cellar, this means groundwater can be close to the cellar floor. The second question for a private house is the yard sewer: in an external sewer, manholes are placed where the direction, fall or diameter changes, and on straight sections every 35 m if the pipe is smaller than 200 mm. Through a manhole the line can be cleaned without digging it up.

Mechanical cleaning with a rotating spiral breaks up fat deposits and cuts roots, while high-pressure jetting removes deposits from the pipe walls. Which method to choose depends on the diameter of the line and the blockage.

On the Stopiņi side: newer private houses and Ropaži rules

Many houses in the village were built in the 2000s, when the village grew fast, and a water heater needs servicing over time. The manufacturer (Dražice) advises checking the magnesium anode and, if needed, replacing it after 2 years of operation, and checking the safety valve by turning its test knob at least once a month and after every idle period longer than 5 days.

For houses not connected to the central sewer, the Ropaži Municipality regulations on decentralised sewerage apply. The plumber works on the house side: the indoor drains, the outlet and the yard line to the tank or the connection point.

Ropaži Municipality binding regulations No. 45/22 (amended by No. 43/24 and No. 15/25): septic tanks and holding tanks must be emptied at least once a year, and for a holding tank the frequency is calculated from consumption (1.5 m³ per person per month or meter data). The system is registered with SIA “Garkalnes inženiertīkli”; for new builds before acceptance for use, changes must be reported within a month, and by 1 March each year the previous year’s water meter readings must be submitted. For a connection to the central network the municipality has co-funding under regulations No. 34/23. Checked 13.11.2026.

Frequently asked questions

The kitchen sink in my flat drains slowly. Is the fault in the trap or the riser?

If only the sink drains slowly, the blockage is usually in the trap or in the line to the riser, and it is cleared inside the flat. If water also rises in the bath or at the neighbours’, the common line may be blocked. Clearing a kitchen sink blockage costs from €50.

Does the water heater need cleaning after the mains flushing?

Not always. If the hot water is clear after a few days, rinsing the aerators is enough. If only the hot water stays cloudy, the water heater is drained and cleaned; water heater cleaning costs from €75.

My house is in Dreiliņi in Stopiņi Parish. Where do I register a septic tank or holding tank?

With SIA “Garkalnes inženiertīkli”, as set by Ropaži Municipality regulations No. 45/22. For a new build this is done before acceptance for use, and changes must be reported within a month.

How often should I check the water heater’s safety valve?

The manufacturer (Dražice) advises doing it at least once a month and after every idle period longer than 5 days, by turning the valve’s test knob. If it drips constantly even when the water heater is not heating and no water is being used, the valve or the pressure needs checking.
