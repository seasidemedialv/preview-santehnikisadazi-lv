Plumber in Atari - your own water and the networks to come

A plumber in Atari most often works in detached houses: in the village by Lake Atari, most of the territory is low-density detached housing, with no industrial or public buildings.

Water supply and sewerage exist only in part of the village, so many houses get water from their own borehole and send wastewater to a septic tank. That brings the most common jobs: the hydrophore (a pump with a pressure tank) and water pressure, the drain line to the tank, and preparing the house for a connection when the networks arrive.

Water from your own borehole: the hydrophore unit in the utility room

The Atari village development plan says that residents mostly use their own water supply systems. That means the water pressure in the house is created not by the street network but by your own pump with a hydrophore. A hydrophore set usually consists of a pump, a diaphragm pressure tank, a pressure switch and fittings: a non-return valve, hoses and a pressure gauge. If one of these parts wears out, the way water flows from the tap changes.

A typical complaint is a pump that switches on and off every few moments. The cause is usually lost or wrongly set tank pre-charge pressure, a leak in the pipes, a damaged diaphragm or a tank that is too small. A simple sign: if water comes out when you press the tank’s air valve, the diaphragm is damaged.

The pre-charge pressure is measured and adjusted only when the tank is disconnected from the system and drained, otherwise the water pressure spoils the reading. The manufacturers (Zilmet, Grundfos) advise checking the tank pre-charge pressure at least once a year. In the form, write how often the pump switches on, what the pressure gauge shows and how many years old the unit is. More about this work on the page hydrophores and water pressure.

Water quality is different for every borehole, and there are no published analyses for Atari wells. If sediment builds up in the filter or the water has changed colour, describe it in the form: it helps to tell whether the filter cartridge needs changing or the cause should be sought in the pump and pipes.

If a water main and sewer reach your street

At village plan meetings, residents have expressed a wish to connect to the Ādaži sewer and water main. The meeting noted that one kilometre of both utilities costs 120–130 thousand euros and that residents’ confirmations are needed. In the village action plan, extending the networks is written into the responsibility of SIA “Ādažu ūdens”. The date when the network will reach a particular house cannot be read from the plan.

When that happens, Ādaži Municipality binding regulations No. 72/2022 apply. The applicant builds the connection and the commercial metering assembly at their own expense, with a qualified contractor under the operator’s supervision. The operator issues the technical conditions free of charge within 20 working days, and they are valid for at least a year. After new main networks are put into operation, an owner in a territory planned for the central system must connect within one year.

The plumber’s part is the inside of the house. The drain outlet from the house must have a fall of at least 2 cm per metre, and its diameter must not be smaller than the largest connected riser. If the water will come from the network, the existing hydrophore unit must be disconnected or switched over so that borehole water and network water do not mix. A meter that cannot go in the standard place may be located in a heated room no further than 2 m from the outer wall. How the whole process works is described in the advice on connecting to the central sewer.

Ādaži Municipality binding regulations No. 72/2022 (amended by No. 11/2026) and No. 24/2022, which cancelled the former Carnikava Municipality co-funding for connections. Checked 13.11.2026 on likumi.lv.

The drain line to the septic tank

While there is no network, wastewater from the house flows to a septic tank. Emptying and building the tank is not plumber’s work: it is done by septic tank emptying contractors and treatment unit installers. Our work is everything between the sanitary appliances and the tank inlet: the indoor pipes, the outlet through the foundation and the line in the yard.

Two values matter for the yard line. The first is the fall, because without it fat and dirt settle in the line. The second is the depth: an external gravity sewer pipe is laid 0.3 m shallower than the standard soil frost depth, but no shallower than 0.7 m to the top of the pipe, and shallower pipes must be protected from frost. If the line freezes in winter or water drains slowly from the sink in spring, the cause can be a line that was laid too shallow or with the wrong fall at the time.

A blockage in the yard line is cleared with a motorised drain snake. Read about it and about rebuilding on the pages sewer connection to a septic tank in Atari and drain cleaning.

Ditches, the canal and rainwater

Atari lies in the Coastal Lowland, and the Gauja–Baltezers canal bounds the village to the north-west. The municipality maintains roads, ditches and drainage systems to reduce flood and rainfall damage, and the village plan provides for cleaning drainage ditches and culverts on Bukultu Road near the Atari border. That does not mean the houses flood here. It means that in low terrain rainwater has to have somewhere to go.

If water appears in a cellar or technical pit only in spring and late autumn, that is a pattern. According to LVĢMC data, the groundwater level in Latvia is usually highest in the second half of March and in April, and again in October and November. A drainage pump with a float switch suits such places. If the pump is installed permanently, the manufacturer (Grundfos) states that the discharge pipe must have a non-return valve. Drainage water is not sent to a septic tank, because it overfills the tank. More: drainage and basement pumps.

New houses in a growing village

At the start of 2025, 155 people lived in Atari, and from 2020 to 2025 the number grew by an average of 4% a year. In a growing village some of the work is in new builds: water supply, drains and heating in an empty house.

In a new build, the key moment is before the walls and floors are closed. A finished water supply system is pressure-tested, flushed with drinking-quality water and, if needed, disinfected; the test pressure and method are stated in the building design. For example, the manufacturer (Uponor) specifies a water test for its pipes: 11 bar for 30 minutes with no pressure drop, then 5.5 bar for 120 minutes. If the house will have its own borehole, provide space for the hydrophore unit straight away, and for a later switch-over to the network. More on the page plumbing in a new build.

Frequently asked questions

Can a house be prepared for a connection while there is no network in Atari yet?

The inside can be prepared: the outlet point in the foundation with the correct fall and diameter, a place for the meter and for disconnecting the hydrophore unit. The connection itself is built to the operator’s technical conditions once the network is at the property.

How long do I have to connect once the new network is in operation?

Under Ādaži Municipality binding regulations No. 72/2022, an owner in a territory planned for the central system must connect within one year after the new main networks are put into operation.

Does Ādaži Municipality co-fund the connection?

The former Carnikava Municipality co-funding for connections was cancelled by binding regulations No. 24/2022 because the budget had no funds for it. Before the work, ask the municipality whether a new programme has been adopted.

My hydrophore switches on every few seconds. Does it have to be replaced?

Not always. More often it is enough to restore the tank pre-charge pressure or fix a leak. If water comes out of the tank’s air valve, the diaphragm is damaged, and then the diaphragm or the tank has to be replaced.
