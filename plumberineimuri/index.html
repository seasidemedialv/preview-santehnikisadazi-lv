Plumber in Eimuri - in both Eimuri villages

A plumber in Eimuri works in two villages with one name: in Ādaži Municipality there are Eimuri in Ādaži Parish and Eimuri in Carnikava Parish.

Both are in the south of the municipality, about 10 km from the centre of Ādaži. Forests and farmland with small detached-house zones dominate, and most houses have a borehole well and a local sewage system. More often than in village centres, the work here is outside the house walls: the service pipe from the borehole, the yard line and water in a pit.

Two villages with one name: which to enter in the form

At the start of 2025, Eimuri in Ādaži Parish had 36 residents and Eimuri in Carnikava Parish had 28. The municipality has one shared development plan for both villages, and both are in Ādaži Municipality, so the same municipal rules apply to both on plumbing matters.

The road differs. Eimuri in Ādaži Parish is crossed by Laveru Road and Garciems Road, and can also be reached by Ataru Road, Briljantu Road and Briljantu Street. Eimuri in Carnikava Parish can be reached only by Garciems Road. It is about 28 km to Riga. In the form, enter the name of the road or street and the house name, if it has one, because in a rural address that helps find the right house.

The service pipe from the borehole to the house

The village plan says that central water supply and sewerage exist only in a small part of Eimuri in Ādaži Parish, and not at all in Eimuri in Carnikava Parish. The other houses use borehole wells. That means that between the borehole and the house a water pipe runs under the yard, and the owner is responsible for it.

Two things can damage this pipe: frost and leaks. The laying depth of external water pipes (to the bottom of the pipe) must be at least 0.5 m greater than the depth at which 0 °C is possible in the ground (LBN 222-15). A leak in an underground service pipe cannot always be seen, but the signs are damp patches, a sunken lawn or paving, unusually green grass in dry weather and pressure changes. If the hydrophore (a pump with a pressure tank) starts switching on even when nobody is using water, the cause can be in the service pipe.

Before any work it is important to know where the main shut-off valve is, so that the water can be shut off quickly in a leak. On replacing a service pipe: pipe replacement in a house.

The yard sewer line to the local system

If the house stands on a larger plot, the line to the local treatment system tends to be long. In an external sewer, manholes are placed at connection points where the direction, fall or diameter changes, and on straight sections every 35 m if the pipe is smaller than 200 mm. The length of the outlet from the riser or inspection point to the axis of the inspection chamber must not exceed 12 m for a 100 mm pipe. If a long line has no manholes, a blockage in it is hard to reach.

A blockage between the house and the chamber is cleared with a motorised drain snake: a rotating spiral breaks up fat deposits and cuts roots. If the line clogs again and again, it is flushed with a high-pressure water jet, which removes deposits from the pipe walls. After cleaning it is worth checking whether the line has a sag where water stands.

A local system in Ādaži Municipality is registered by the agency “Carnikavas komunālserviss” under binding regulations No. 3/2025, and changes, such as a rebuild, must be reported within a month. This applies to both Eimuri. More on cleaning lines: drain jetting.

Ādaži Municipality binding regulations No. 3/2025 and No. 72/2022. Checked 13.11.2026 on likumi.lv.

The polder, ditches and water in the cellar

On the Carnikava Parish side the Eimuri–Mangaļi polder operates. Reconstruction of its pumping station “Mangaļi” is planned for 2025–2027, and renewal of the bridge over the Eimuri canal is also planned. In a polder, the water level in the ground is kept by a pumping station and a network of ditches. In the village plan, residents complain that the ditches are not cleaned.

At house level this means a drainage pump may be useful for a cellar or a technical pit. A float switch switches the pump on and off automatically according to the water level, and if the pump is installed permanently, the manufacturer (Grundfos) requires a non-return valve in the discharge pipe. The discharge line is protected from frost in winter. The discharge point is chosen in advance: in Ādaži Municipality rainwater may be discharged into the central sewer only with the operator’s permission, and it is not sent into a local sewage system, because that overfills.

We describe choosing and installing pumps on the page drainage pumps, and repair on the page drainage pump repair.

Frequently asked questions

Does Eimuri in Carnikava Parish have different rules from Eimuri in Ādaži Parish?

No. Both villages are in Ādaži Municipality, so binding regulations No. 3/2025 apply to local sewerage in both, and No. 72/2022 to connections to the central system.

How can I tell that the service pipe from the borehole is leaking?

The signs are a damp or sunken lawn over the pipe, greener grass in dry weather and a hydrophore that switches on when nobody is using water. Close all the taps and watch the pressure gauge: if the pressure falls, there is a leak somewhere.

Why does a long yard line need manholes?

On straight sections manholes are placed every 35 m if the pipe is smaller than 200 mm, and where the line changes direction or fall. Without them a blockage in a long line is hard to reach with a drain snake.

Where should I discharge the water from a drainage pump?

Not into a local sewage system, and not into the central sewer without the operator’s permission. Usually a place on the plot is chosen where the water can drain away, and the discharge line is protected from frost.
