Plumber in Kadaga - the block-of-flats centre and summer houses by the Gauja

A plumber in Kadaga works in two very different settings: in blocks of flats in the village centre and in summer houses in Upmalas, between Lake Kadaga and the Gauja.

In flats with district heating, typical jobs are replacing radiators, towel rails and taps, while for summer houses the most important things are winterising and spring start-up. In both places you have to allow for fairly hard water.

The largest village in Ādaži Parish, with a block-of-flats centre

Kadaga, formerly Ādaži-2, is the largest village in Ādaži Parish: in 2025 it had 2,518 residents. It lies on the right bank of the Gauja, about 3 km from Ādaži. The village grew up after the Second World War as a military town, and its centre is built up with blocks of flats.

Usually the flat's part starts beyond the flat's shut-off valves, while the risers and the shared pipes in the basement are the building manager's responsibility; the building manager sets the exact boundary. If a riser leaks, report it to the manager first. If a mixer tap, a trap or a toilet supply leaks, it can be fixed within the flat.

Radiators and towel rails with district heating

Kadaga has district heating, provided by SIA “Ādažu namsaimnieks”. This changes how a radiator is replaced in a flat: you cannot simply shut off the heating riser yourself. The manager arranges shutting off the riser and refilling the system, and this is charged separately from our work.

  1. Agree the time with the manager The work is planned for a moment when the riser can be shut off and drained. Outside the heating season this is easier to do.
  2. Replacing the radiator or towel rail The old one is removed, and the new one is fixed and connected to the existing supply pipes.
  3. Refilling and bleeding After connection, the system is refilled, the radiator is bled and the connections are checked.

If you are only replacing a mixer tap or a toilet, the district heating does not affect the work, and it is enough to shut off the water in the flat.

Upmalas and Cits Mežaparks: summer houses between the lake and the Gauja

Kadaga also includes Upmalas, a summer-house village between Lake Kadaga and the Gauja, and Cits Mežaparks by Upmalu road. If a summer house's plumbing stands unused for months, this brings its own problems.

  • When water freezes it expands by about 9%, so a pipe or a pump body full of water can burst.
  • The maker Grundfos states that a pump not used during a frost period must have all the water drained from its chambers.
  • In an unheated house, the water heater is drained before the cold weather and disconnected from the electricity on all poles (Dražice).
  • If an appliance is not used for a long time, the water in the trap evaporates, and a sewer smell appears in the room.

In spring the system is flushed: remove the aerators and shower hoses, run the cold water first and then the hot, from the tap nearest the incoming supply to the farthest one. If the summer house takes water from its own well or borehole, it is also worth checking the pre-charge pressure of the hydrophore (a pump with a pressure tank) before start-up.

Who owns the pipes in the village

In 2011, because of a dispute over the internal networks in Cits Mežaparks, water was supplied for only an hour a day, and the municipality took the view that the pipes belonged to the residents. It is an old case, but it shows well why you need to know where ownership ends.

The Water Management Services Law (Ūdenssaimniecības pakalpojumu likums) sets the boundary between the service provider's networks and the owner's. Everything on your side is your responsibility, including an underground leak on the way to the house. If it is not clear where the boundary runs, ask the operator before the repair; Kadaga has its own water supply system, managed by SIA “Ādažu ūdens”.

Water hardness in Kadaga and water heaters

In the Kadaga system in April 2026, the hardness was 2.23 mmol/l, labelled “fairly hard”, while iron was 0.052 mg/l against a limit of 0.20 mg/l. So in the village water main the main problem is limescale, not iron.

Limescale builds up in the water heater tank and on the heating element, and the maker Dražice advises a lower set temperature so that deposits form more slowly. In time a water heater needs descaling: it is drained, opened, the limescale is removed and the seal is checked. If the house takes water from its own borehole, these figures do not apply to it, because borehole water can have a different composition.

How much does a plumber in Kadaga cost?

Prices are for labour; materials are extra. Jobs in Kadaga are outside Ādaži town, so the travel charge depends on the distance; we tell you when we contact you, before the work.

  • Installing or replacing a radiator: from €75 each; shutting off the riser (arranged with the building manager) is not included.
  • Installing or replacing a heated towel rail: from €80.
  • Water heater cleaning (descaling) and servicing: from €75.
  • Installing or replacing a mixer tap: from €25.

For winterising a summer house there is no “from” price. It depends on whether only the water supply is drained or also the heating and the traps, whether the system is blown through with a compressor, and whether the water heater and the hydrophore must be drained.

Frequently asked questions

Can a radiator be replaced in Kadaga during the heating season?

Only if the manager can shut off and drain the heating riser. In Kadaga heat is supplied by SIA “Ādažu namsaimnieks”, and the riser work is arranged by the building manager. Replacing a radiator costs from €75.

Does Kadaga water need an iron filter?

In the village water main in April 2026, iron was 0.052 mg/l against a limit of 0.20 mg/l, so usually not. The problem is hardness, 2.23 mmol/l. If you take water from your own borehole, you first need to know its analysis.

How do you prepare an Upmalas summer house for winter?

Close the incoming supply, drain the pipes, the water heater and the pump, and disconnect the water heater from the electricity. If the house has water-filled heating and will not be heated in winter, it must be drained too. If you want a plumber to do it, describe in the form what the house has: a water heater, a hydrophore, heating.

Who is responsible for the pipe from the street to my house?

The boundary between the operator's and the owner's networks is set by the Water Management Services Law (Ūdenssaimniecības pakalpojumu likums). What is on your side is your responsibility. Ask the operator for the exact boundary for your address.
