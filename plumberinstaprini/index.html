The village has grown very fast over the last 25 years: in 2000 it had 128 residents, and in 2025 already 590. If a house was built in these years, its plumbing is no longer new but not old either. The most common jobs are maintenance: the water heater, the heating circuit, underfloor heating and, if the house has its own borehole, the hydrophore (a pump with a pressure tank).

A village that grew in the 2000s

According to CSP (the Central Statistical Bureau) data, Stapriņi had 128 residents in 2000, 494 in 2011, 529 in 2021 and 590 in 2025. The biggest jump was in the first decade, when the population almost quadrupled. The village grew out of the Stapriņi farmsteads mentioned in 1866, which in time merged with other farmsteads.

If in your house the water heater, the heating pump and the expansion vessel date from the time of construction, that does not yet mean anything is faulty. It means that the maintenance jobs the makers advise every two years or once a year are worth keeping up with, rather than waiting for a leak.

Water heater: anode, limescale and safety valve

A magnesium anode protects the water heater tank from corrosion. The maker (Dražice) advises checking it and, if needed, replacing it after 2 years of operation, because the anode's life varies with the hardness and composition of the water. The maker (Ariston) advises descaling the heating element about every 2 years, and more often in very hard water. An enamelled tank must not be cleaned with acids.

If water drips from the safety valve while heating up, that is normal: water expands when heated. If it also drips when the water heater is not heating and nobody is using water, then usually either the pressure in the water main is too high or the valve is faulty. The maker (Dražice) advises checking the valve's operation with its test button at least once a month. In more detail: water heater cleaning in Stapriņi and water heater repair.

The hydrophore tank in a house with its own borehole

If the house has its own borehole, the diaphragm of the pressure tank loses air pressure over the years. The maker (Grundfos, JP pumps) advises setting the tank pre-charge pressure 0.2 bar below the pump's cut-in pressure, while for Grundfos GT-U+ tanks it states 0.9 times the cut-in pressure for constant-speed pumps. The two rules give a similar result, but it can only be measured with the tank drained and disconnected.

If someone changed the pressure switch settings at some point, the tank pre-charge pressure must be readjusted now too. This mismatch can be the reason why the pump starts to switch on more often. Job prices and signs: hydrophores.

Underfloor heating and heating circuit pressure

In a closed heating system the pressure when cold should usually be 1–2 bar. The maker (Vaillant) states that below 0.8 bar the boiler shows a low-pressure warning, and below 0.5 bar it switches off. If the pressure falls repeatedly, the reason can be the expansion vessel; the maker (Flamco) advises checking it and its pre-charge pressure every year, when the system has cooled down and is without pressure.

In water underfloor heating, the flow of each circuit is set according to the design. The maker (Danfoss) states that the presetting of each manifold valve is set from a diagram. If one room is cold while others are warm, the fault is often in this adjustment or in the actuator, not in the pipe in the floor. The standard EN 1264 limits the floor surface temperature in the occupied zone to 29 °C, so the floor is not run hotter to compensate for an unbalanced system. More: underfloor heating repair.

Buying or selling a house in the village

Before a deal it is worth checking the plumbing that you will not see afterwards. Signs of a hidden leak in a wall are bubbling or peeling paint or wallpaper, yellow or brown stains, mould or a musty smell, and a faint sound of running water. A simple check: close all the taps and watch the pressure gauge; if the pressure falls within a few hours, there may be a leak.

Also ask where the water in the house comes from and where the drains go. If the house has a local system, in Ādaži Municipality it must be registered with the agency “Carnikavas komunālserviss”, and after a sale the changes must be reported within one month. We describe what the check covers on the page pre-sale plumbing inspection.

Frequently asked questions

Is Stapriņi in Stopiņi Parish?

No. Stapriņi is a large village in Ādaži Parish, Ādaži Municipality, by the A1 road. So the binding regulations of Ādaži Municipality apply to it, not the regulations of Ropaži Municipality, which operate in Stopiņi Parish.

How often should a water heater anode be replaced?

The maker (Dražice) advises checking the anode and, if needed, replacing it after 2 years of operation; Ariston states replacement every 2 years, and a check every year in corrosive water.

One room with underfloor heating does not warm up. Does the floor have to be opened?

Usually not at once. First the manifold is checked: this room's circuit valve, flow meter and actuator. The floor is opened only if there are signs that the fault is in the pipe.

The heating pressure drops every few weeks. Why?

The most common reasons are a seep at some connection or an expansion vessel that has lost its pre-charge pressure. The maker (Vaillant) states that if water comes from the vessel's valve, the vessel must be replaced.
