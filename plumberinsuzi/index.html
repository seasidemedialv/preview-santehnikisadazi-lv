There are few houses here, because the lake takes up more than half of the neighbourhood, and each house can get its water differently. So in your request first say whether you have a city water main or your own borehole: this decides whether the job involves a hydrophore (a pump with a pressure tank), the incoming supply or a drain outlet.

Dunes, the lake and the houses between them

The Suži neighbourhood covers 402.3 ha, and 52% of it is taken up by Lake Ķīšezers. Detached-house development occupies only 12.7 ha, or 3.2% of the area. The residential development sits on the Jaunciems dunes, and the Suži village is 9–11 m above sea level; nearby are the Sužu dune (up to 18 m) and Sužu ūziņa in the Jaunciems Nature Reserve.

In the Ķīšezers shore zone (50–200 m), groundwater is shallower than 1.5 m and the flood risk exceeds 10%, but the existing development on the dunes is practically not floodable. So a basement pump is not the typical first job here. It becomes relevant if a house or an outbuilding stands lower, on the shore-zone side.

City water main or your own borehole

In the list of network development projects of SIA “Rīgas ūdens” are Jugla, Berģi, Bukulti, Čiekurkalns and Mangaļsala, but Suži is not mentioned there. So plumbing work depends on what exactly your house has: a city water main and sewer, or your own borehole and a tank.

The hydrophore and the pressure tank in a house with a borehole

A hydrophore set usually consists of a pump, a diaphragm pressure tank, a pressure switch and fittings: a non-return valve, hoses and a pressure gauge. If one part does not work, you feel it at the tap. The pressure jumps, the pump switches on after every glass of water or cannot switch off.

  • The makers (Zilmet, Grundfos) advise checking the tank pre-charge pressure at least once a year; a large pressure drop can indicate a worn diaphragm.
  • The pre-charge pressure is measured and adjusted only when the tank is disconnected from the system and drained of water.
  • The maker (Grundfos, JP pumps) advises setting the pre-charge pressure 0.2 bar below the pump's cut-in pressure.
  • If the pressure switch settings are changed, the tank pre-charge pressure must be readjusted too.

The price of a diaphragm tank check and hydrophore repair depends on whether an air pressure correction is enough or the diaphragm must be replaced. It also depends on what the fault is (the switch, the diaphragm, the non-return valve or the motor) and whether the pump has to be removed.

Sandy ground freezes deeper

The dunes are made of sand, and sandy ground freezes deeper than clay. LBN 003-19 (the Latvian Building Code) allows the values for clay ground to be used for sandy ground with a coefficient of 1.2, so in Riga once in 100 years about 144 cm. For an external water pipe, LBN 222-15 (the Latvian Building Code) requires a laying depth at least 0.5 m greater than the depth at which a temperature of 0 °C is possible in the ground.

The most exposed to frost are the line from the borehole to the house, an outdoor tap and a pump in an unheated room. If a pipe has frozen anyway:

  1. Close the incoming supply Close the incoming supply valve and open a tap so the melting water can run out.
  2. Warm it slowly Warm it with hot water bottles or a heated cloth, starting from the tap end.
  3. No flame Never use an open flame or a blowtorch.

If it has frozen in the ground or in a wall, the price of thawing depends on the location, the pipe material and whether the ground must be dug or a wall opened.

Jaunciema Street and a name beyond the border

Suži was included within the boundaries of Riga in 1974, together with the former lands of Ādaži Parish along Jaunciema Street. The main traffic axis is Jaunciema gatve, and buses No. 11 and No. 29 run there.

The name “Suži” is also found in Ropaži Municipality. A local plan (2024) for the site “Suži, Garkalne Parish” provides for 50 detached houses with water from a borehole and a temporary sealed holding tank, with a duty to connect to the central network when it exists. The plan has been suspended since 21.08.2024.

If you are building a house with such a temporary solution, it is worth laying out the internal drains and the outlet so that they can later be switched to the street network without opening the floor. In Garkalne Parish a decentralised system is registered under binding regulations No. 45/22 of Ropaži Municipality, for new builds before the building is put into operation.

Frequently asked questions

How do I tell whether my house in Suži has a city water main?

Look at where the water bill comes from and what is at the incoming supply. If the house has a pump with a pressure tank that switches on when you open a tap, the water most likely comes from your own borehole. If you do not know, describe the incoming supply in the form.

The hydrophore switches on too often. What can be at fault?

Often the fault is in the tank: a pre-charge pressure that is too low or a worn diaphragm. The pre-charge pressure is checked only when the tank is disconnected and drained, and following the maker's advice it is set slightly below the pump's cut-in pressure.

Do you pump out the holding tank?

No, we work on the house side: the internal drains, traps and the outlet up to the tank. In Riga, how often a holding tank is emptied is calculated from consumption and volume, and without a meter 9.125 m³ per declared resident per year is assumed (regulations No. 66).

Does a house on a dune need its water system drained in winter?

If the house stands empty in winter, yes: close the incoming supply valve and drain the pipes, the water heater and the hydrophore pump, and also the heating system if it is filled with water and the house will not be heated. The price depends on whether the whole system is drained or blown through with a compressor and what has to be done in spring.
