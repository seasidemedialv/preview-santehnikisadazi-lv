Streets are being rebuilt and water networks renewed here, so many house owners have to think about the supply pipe up to the meter assembly and about rainwater at the foundations. In flats, the more frequent jobs are clearing a sink outlet, replacing a mixer tap or descaling a water heater.

What houses there are in Podnieki and what this means for plumbing

Podnieki is a neighbourhood of Ādaži town, not a separate parish village. Our reading of the map: OpenStreetMap marks it about 2 km east of the centre, with a bus stop “Podnieki” on Podnieku Street. The municipality writes that the population of the municipality grows by about 3% a year and that Podnieki is growing fastest.

The housing is mixed. The municipality's analysis lists 9 blocks of flats and 27 private houses around the Krastupes Street project. This means two different circles of work. In a flat, the plumber works up to the shared riser: traps, outlets, toilet, water heater, washing machine connection. In a private house, the responsibility reaches further, to the supply pipe at the street, the house drain outlet and the basement.

If a riser in a flat leaks or a shared pipe in the basement seeps, that is a matter for the building manager. We can work on the flat's side once the manager has shut off the riser.

The rebuild of Krastupes, Dadzīšu and Podnieku streets and your water supply pipe

The project “Adaptation to climate change in Podnieki, Ādaži Municipality” plans the rebuilding of stretches of Krastupes, Dadzīšu and Podnieku streets over 1.2 km, renewal of rainwater systems and the building of water management networks. During the rebuilding of Krastupes Street, SIA “Ādažu ūdens” is renewing the water supply networks, while the road project is led by the municipality.

The new street network does not change who is responsible for the pipe on the property. The binding regulations No. 72/2022 of Ādaži Municipality say that the applicant has the connection and the commercial metering assembly built at their own expense with a construction specialist who is entitled to carry out such work, under the operator's supervision. The operator issues the technical conditions free of charge within 20 working days.

Our work starts beyond the meter assembly, inside the house. If after the street works the place of the incoming supply or of the meter changes, the water pipe in the house may have to be rerouted to the new place. That means a new pipe section, a shut-off valve, a filter, a connection to the water heater. The regulations allow the meter in a heated room no further than 2 m from the outer wall if it cannot be placed in the standard place.

Binding regulations No. 72/2022 of Ādaži Municipality (amended by No. 11/2026), likumi.lv, checked 13.11.2026.

Rainwater running towards the Vējupe

The project document on Krastupes Street says that rainwater drainage there is not provided: gutters are broken, gullies are silted up, and the street slopes down towards the Vējupe. Until the street is rebuilt, every house with a basement or a low foundation edge lives with the way water runs off across its own plot.

One common mistake is to connect roof downpipes to the domestic drains. The binding regulations of Ādaži Municipality allow rainwater to be sent into the sewer only with the operator's permission and forbid pouring it into inspection chambers. Rainwater needs its own route, not the house outlet.

If water appears in the basement in spring or autumn, a drainage pump (sump pump) in a pit with a float switch helps. The maker (Grundfos) states that a permanently installed pump must have a non-return valve in the discharge pipe. LVĢMC data show that groundwater in Latvia is usually highest in the second half of March and in April, and a second time in October and November, so the pump is worth checking before these months.

Hard town water and water heaters

Podnieki is part of the Ādaži water supply system, where in April 2026 the hardness was 2.43 mmol/l. With such water, limescale builds up in the water heater and on the heating element: the water heater heats more slowly, and the flow at the taps decreases.

  • In mineral-rich water, the maker Dražice advises removing the limescale after 1–2 years of operation through the flange opening.
  • Ariston advises descaling the heating element about every 2 years, and more often in very hard water.
  • In tap aerators, limescale blocks the screen; they are cleaned or replaced when the flow becomes uneven.

During descaling, the water heater is drained, opened, the limescale is removed and the seal is checked. If the anode is worn, it is replaced separately.

Frequently asked questions

May roof downpipes be connected to the house drains in Podnieki?

Not without the operator's permission. The binding regulations No. 72/2022 of Ādaži Municipality allow rainwater to be sent into the sewer only with the operator's permission and forbid pouring it into inspection chambers. Rainwater needs separate drainage.

The street rebuild replaced the water network. Who rebuilds the pipe to my house?

The owner has the connection and the meter assembly built at their own expense with a construction specialist who is entitled to carry out such work, under the operator's supervision, and the technical conditions are issued by the operator. We can reroute the internal water pipe from the meter assembly to the taps and the water heater.

Do you also work in blocks of flats in Podnieki?

Yes, within the flat: sinks, toilets, mixer taps, water heaters, heated towel rails. The shared riser is shut off by the building manager; without that, a heated towel rail or a radiator cannot be replaced.

Why does a water heater heat more slowly over time?

The water of the Ādaži system was 2.43 mmol/l hard in 2026, and limescale builds up on the heating element. Descaling costs from €75; if a new anode or seal is needed, they are charged separately.
