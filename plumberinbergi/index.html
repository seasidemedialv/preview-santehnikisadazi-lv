Plumber in Berģi - connecting to the new networks and cellars

A plumber in Berģi is most often needed in a private house, because the Berģi neighbourhood of Riga, at the north-eastern edge of the city, is mostly detached housing.

In recent years new water supply and sewer networks have been built in Riga’s Berģi, so many houses need the drain outlet and indoor pipework rearranged. By Lake Jugla and the Baložkalns dunes, groundwater affects the work, while in the village of Berģi in Garkalne Parish the Ropaži Municipality regulations apply.

Two places with one name on either side of the city boundary

Berģi is a neighbourhood of Riga that was included in the city in 1974; before that it was the village of Berģi. Just beyond the boundary lies the village of Berģi in Garkalne Parish, Ropaži Municipality, which borders Riga’s Berģuciems at Upesciema Street. Each side has a different water company, different rules and different co-funding for a connection.

  • Riga’s Berģi neighbourhood: water supply and sewerage are served by SIA “Rīgas ūdens”, and the Riga City Council binding regulations apply.
  • The village of Berģi in Garkalne Parish: water supply and sewerage are provided by SIA “Garkalnes inženiertīkli”; in Garkalne Parish the tariff is €2.84/m³ for water and €1.94/m³ for sewerage, excluding VAT.

On the Riga side the detached houses are in two parts: in the north-west by Brīvības gatve and Berģu Street (Baložkalns), and around Berģu and Upesciema streets. Multi-storey buildings are at the start of Rožu Street and the end of Berģu Street. In the village of Berģi in Garkalne Parish, 322 people lived in 1989 and already 1,427 in 2022, so alongside older houses there are many new builds with full indoor plumbing. In your request, state the street.

The new Rīgas ūdens networks in the southern part, and the house outlet

In the Cohesion Fund project “Water management development in Riga, phase 5”, 8.50 km of water main and 19.70 km of sewer were built in the southern part of Berģi, and more than 400 properties can connect. At the start of 2025, a further 1,800 m of new sewer was completed around Jumiķu, Saulcerītes and Suitu streets and 900 m of water main was renewed, also on Melderu, Parka, Rožu and Berģu streets. Design of the networks for the area around Vējdzirnavu Street and Rožu Prospect was announced in 2025, and construction is planned to start in 2026.

Once there is a sewer in the street, on the house side the direction usually has to change: wastewater must go not to the old tank but to the new inspection chamber. Riga regulations No. 17 state that the central sewer ends at the outer wall of the inspection chamber; everything further on the property is a network maintained by the owner. This part is done by a plumber: the indoor drains, the outlet through the foundation or cellar wall, and the yard line to the chamber.

  • A cellar outlet needs a fall of at least 2 cm per metre, and its diameter must not be smaller than the largest connected riser (LBN 221-15).
  • A yard gravity line is laid no shallower than 0.7 m to the top of the pipe; pipes lying shallower must be protected from frost and traffic loads.
  • Where a pipe crosses a foundation, a 0.2 m gap is left around it and filled with a flexible, watertight material; in wet ground a sealing sleeve is needed.

Riga co-funding (regulations No. RD-23-207-sn, amended 18.12.2025): for a private house sewer connection up to 5 m €1,000, 5–20 m €1,500, over 20 m €2,000; a water supply connection is not co-funded. In the Cohesion Fund phase 5 area in Berģi, up to €739 is available for design and up to €1,800 for construction; the connection must be built by 31.12.2026, the application must be submitted by 01.04.2027, and the whole process can take 4–6 months. Checked 13.11.2026.

Indoor work, such as moving a toilet or shower outlet, is easier to do at the same time as the new outlet.

On the Garkalne Parish side: the Ropaži Municipality procedure

In the part of the village by the golf club, the detailed plan states that once a water main is built, houses on Kungu and Pūku streets must be connected to the central network within one year; until then a decentralised system is acceptable. In Ropaži Municipality a connection can receive municipal co-funding.

Ropaži Municipality binding regulations No. 34/23 (in force from 01.01.2024): 50% of the cost of a residential house connection, up to €2,000 for water supply, up to €3,000 for sewerage, no more than €60 per metre, up to €300 for documentation. Conditions: declared residence in the municipality for at least 6 months, no real estate tax debt, a main network at the property; indoor networks are not funded, and the work must be completed within 6 months of the contract. Checked 13.11.2026.

The last condition is important: plumbing inside the house is not part of the co-funding, so it is planned as a separate job with its own price.

Groundwater in Baložkalns and peat by Lake Jugla

In the Baložkalns dunes groundwater is usually at a depth of 1.5–3 m, while by Lake Jugla and the Jugla river there is peat and silty peat down to a depth of 3 m. In the narrow shore strip the flood risk is around 10%. A house with a cellar has to take this into account when planning drains and a pump.

LVĢMC data show that groundwater in Latvia is usually highest in the second half of March and in April, and again in October and November. If the cellar has a shower, washing machine or toilet below the cover of the nearest inspection chamber, LBN 221-15 requires them to be connected with a separate outlet. It also requires a device that prevents the cellar from flooding from the external sewer, for example a backflow valve.

If water appears in the cellar after rain or snowmelt, a drainage pump in a pit with a float switch usually helps. For a permanently installed pump, the manufacturer (Grundfos) requires a non-return valve in the discharge pipe so that water does not return to the pit.

Cloudy water after mains flushing

From 3 to 6 November 2026, SIA “Rīgas ūdens” flushed 4.2 km of water main from Sidraburgas Street to the outlet at Piķurga, and in Berģi and Jugla the water could be cloudy. After such flushing, sediment tends to remain in tap strainers, the house filter and the inlet of household appliances.

  1. Run the water Let the cold water run until it is clear.
  2. Rinse the strainers Unscrew the tap aerator and the shower head, and rinse the strainers.
  3. Describe the problem If weak flow remains at one tap, the fault is usually in the aerator or the filter at the tap; if throughout the house, look at the incoming supply. Describe it in the form.

Older houses in the former Jugla village

Development of the Jugla village began in the 1920s after the land reform, when land was divided into plots of 1,500–2,000 m², and after the war it continued mainly as low-rise housing. So alongside new builds there are houses with pipes of different ages.

Older houses may have galvanised steel pipes. When the zinc wears out, the steel rusts from the inside, the pipe narrows, pressure falls and the water turns brownish. If this happens even when nothing is being flushed in the network, the cause can be the house’s own pipes. A new yard pipe is laid deep enough: in Riga conditions an external water pipe comes to about 1.1–1.7 m in clay and up to about 1.9 m in sand (calculated from LBN 003-19 and LBN 222-15).

Frequently asked questions

My house is in Berģi in Garkalne Parish. Does the Riga co-funding apply to me too?

No. Riga regulations No. RD-23-207-sn and the Cohesion Fund phase 5 support apply to properties in Riga. In Garkalne Parish the Ropaži Municipality binding regulations No. 34/23 apply, with 50% co-funding and their own conditions, and the water company is SIA “Garkalnes inženiertīkli”.

Which part of the connection does the plumber build?

The house side: the indoor drains and water pipes, the outlet through the foundation and the line in the yard to the inspection chamber or meter chamber. In Riga the network maintained by the water company ends at the outer wall of these chambers. On the call we talk through what else has to be agreed with the water company, once you know your street and the connection point.

The tap flows weakly after mains flushing. Will it clear by itself?

Often it is enough to rinse the aerator and run the water. If weak flow remains at one tap, the fault is usually in the strainer or cartridge; if throughout the house, look at the filter or the incoming supply. Installing or replacing a mixer tap costs from €25 each.

Is a backflow valve enough for a cellar shower?

According to the manufacturer (KESSEL), backflow valves of types 0, 1 and 2 are intended only for wastewater without faecal matter. For a shower or washing machine in the cellar such a valve may be enough. For a toilet below the backflow level you need a type 3 valve (an electrically controlled flap with a backup manual flap) or a pumping unit that lifts the wastewater higher.
