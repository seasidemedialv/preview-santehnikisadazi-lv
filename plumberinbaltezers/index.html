Plumber in Baltezers - between Lielais and Mazais Baltezers

A plumber in Baltezers works mainly in private houses: the village between Lielais Baltezers and Mazais Baltezers grew after the Second World War as a village of private houses, and there are few multi-storey buildings.

Most of the village has central water supply and sewerage, but not everywhere. So in connected houses the most common jobs are blockages, cisterns and water heaters, while in houses without the network the live issues are preparing for a connection and protecting the cellar.

Who supplies the water and which rules apply to your address

In the village of Baltezers in Ādaži Parish, water supply and sewerage services are provided by SIA “Ādažu ūdens”. Since 1 August 2025 it also serves those parts of the Baltezers and Alderi villages that were previously served by SIA “Garkalnes ūdens”; after the split that company ceased to exist. So technical conditions for a connection and questions about the network in the street are dealt with by Ādažu ūdens.

Baltezers is often associated with Riga’s water supply. The Baltezers pumping station started work at the end of 1904, originally drawing water from 42 filter wells at a depth of 18–20 m, and since 1988 the old pump house has held the exhibition of the Riga Water Supply Museum. This water source supplies Riga, not the houses of the village, so questions about village tap water are not a matter for Rīgas ūdens.

Latvia also has a second Baltezers, in Garkalne Parish in Ropaži Municipality. There water services are provided by SIA “Garkalnes inženiertīkli”, and the Ropaži Municipality regulations apply, including binding regulations No. 34/23 on 50% co-funding of a residential house connection. If you are not sure which side your house is on, write the full address in the form.

Houses that do not yet have a central sewer

At village plan meetings and in a survey, residents say that a central sewer is missing in many places, and they call for the sewerage of lakeside cottages to be checked. If the network is built in your street, Ādaži Municipality binding regulations No. 72/2022 apply: the connection applicant builds the connection at their own expense, the operator issues technical conditions free of charge within 20 working days, and they are valid for at least a year. New or larger wastewater volumes require a one-off capacity fee to SIA “Ādažu ūdens”.

On the house side, we rebuild the outlet so that it no longer goes to the old tank but to the new inspection chamber. Where the outlet crosses the foundation or a cellar wall, a 0.2 m gap is left around the pipe and filled with a flexible material that is watertight and gas-tight, and in wet ground a sealing sleeve is needed. The length of the outlet from the riser to the axis of the inspection chamber must not exceed 12 m for a 100 mm pipe.

A cellar by the canal: backflow valve and pump

To the north-west the village is bounded by the Gauja–Baltezers canal. In the village plan, residents report that the canal bank is collapsing and call the area around Baltkrastu Street flood-prone. That is residents’ opinion, not map data, but if your house has a cellar or a cellar shower, it is a reason to check how its drain is set up.

LBN 221-15 requires sanitary appliances located below the cover of the nearest inspection chamber to be connected with a separate outlet and a device that prevents the cellar from flooding from the external sewer. Usually this is a backflow valve. The EN 13564 standard divides them into types 0 to 3, and the manufacturer (KESSEL) states that types 0, 1 and 2 are intended only for wastewater without faecal matter. If there is a toilet in the cellar, you need a type 3 valve or a pumping unit that lifts the wastewater above the backflow level.

A valve works only if it is maintained. The manufacturer (KESSEL) advises the user to check it once a month by opening and closing the emergency lid several times, and a specialist to service it at least every six months. On pumps: drainage and sewage pumps and sewage pump repair.

A lakeside house nobody lives in over winter

If a house by Lielais or Mazais Baltezers stands empty in winter, the problems usually come from two sources. The first is the water in the pipes. If the house will be empty for a long time, the water industry’s advice is to close the incoming supply valve and drain the system, and to insulate pipes in unheated rooms with no gaps at bends and valves. The second is the traps: in an appliance not used for a long time, the water evaporates from the trap, the water seal disappears and a sewer smell appears in the room.

In spring the system is flushed. Remove the tap aerators and shower hoses, flush the cold water first, then the hot, from the tap nearest the incoming supply to the farthest. We describe winter preparation and spring start-up on the page winterising a summer house.

Connected houses: a running toilet, slow drains

In a house connected to the network, every cubic metre is paid for twice: for the water and for the sewerage. So a toilet in which water keeps running after flushing is not just noise. Usually the fault is dirt or limescale between the flush valve seal and the outlet opening, and the manufacturer (Geberit) advises taking the valve out, cleaning it and putting it back. If that does not help, the mechanism is replaced.

If water drains slowly in several appliances at once, the blockage is usually in the common line, not in a trap. It is cleared with a drain snake that breaks up fat deposits; if the line clogs again and again, it is flushed with a high-pressure water jet. More: drain cleaning in Baltezers.

Frequently asked questions

My house is in Baltezers in Garkalne Parish. Are the rules the same?

No. Garkalne Parish is in Ropaži Municipality, where water services are provided by SIA “Garkalnes inženiertīkli” and the Ropaži Municipality binding regulations apply. In the village of Baltezers in Ādaži Parish, the Ādaži Municipality regulations and SIA “Ādažu ūdens” apply.

Is the water in the village main the same water that Rīgas ūdens takes at Baltezers?

For houses in the village, water is supplied by SIA “Ādažu ūdens”. The Baltezera ūdensgūtne of Rīgas ūdens, which is supplemented with water from Mazais Baltezers in infiltration basins, supplies Riga. For the quality of your own tap water, ask your supplier.

When does a cellar need a backflow valve?

When the cellar has a sink, shower, washing machine or toilet that is below the cover of the nearest inspection chamber. Then LBN 221-15 requires a separate outlet and a device against flooding from the external sewer. If there is a toilet in the cellar, you need a valve intended for wastewater with faecal matter, or a pumping unit.

How long are the technical conditions for a connection valid?

Under Ādaži Municipality binding regulations No. 72/2022, the operator issues them free of charge within 20 working days, and they are valid for at least a year.
