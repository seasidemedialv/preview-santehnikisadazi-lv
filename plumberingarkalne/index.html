In private houses old pipes more often have to be rebuilt or the indoor networks prepared for a connection to “Garkalnes inženiertīkli”, while in flats the jobs are blockages, taps and water heaters. This page describes what matters in Garkalne specifically.

A former resort: what that means for the pipes in a house

Garkalne began to form after the Ropaži railway station was built in 1889, and in the 1920s and 1930s it was a resort with beaches and tennis courts. If a house was built then and rebuilt later, one system can contain both new plastic pipes and old metal sections.

In galvanised steel pipes, once the zinc coating has worn out, rust deposits form on the inside. They narrow the pipe, pressure at the taps falls and the water turns brownish. If the rust is in many places, it can be more worthwhile to replace a section; this is assessed at the inspection.

  1. Inspection The plumber finds out where the pipe enters the house, what materials are in the system and which sections are behind the finishes.
  2. Choosing the route The new pipe run is planned through warm rooms so that it does not freeze in winter, and with a place for a valve that can be reached.
  3. Installation and testing After installation the system is filled, the joints are tested under pressure, and only then closed in with the finishes.

Connection to the street network and 50% support

Central water supply and sewerage in Garkalne are provided by the municipal SIA “Garkalnes inženiertīkli”. Ropaži Municipality binding regulations No. 34/23 provide co-funding of 50% for a residential house connection: up to €2,000 for water supply, up to €3,000 for sewerage, no more than €60 per metre, and up to €300 for documentation.

An important condition for plumbing work: indoor networks are not funded. The support goes to the external connection, but everything that happens behind the building wall (a new service pipe up to the meter, moving the sewer outlet, disconnecting the hydrophore, a pump with a pressure tank) has to be planned in your own budget. In addition, the work must be completed within 6 months of the contract, so it is better to prepare the inside of the house in good time.

  • Your residence must have been declared in the municipality for at least 6 months.
  • There must be no real estate tax debt.
  • The main network must be at the property.

Ropaži Municipality binding regulations No. 34/23, adopted 18.10.2023, in force from 01.01.2024. For certain groups of residents (for example large families and single pensioners) the co-funding can be higher. Checked 13.11.2026. More on the procedure: connecting to the central sewer.

Flat or private house: different jobs

Garkalne also has blocks of flats: in the water analysis report of “Garkalnes inženiertīkli”, a flat on Upes Street is named as a sampling point. In a flat the plumber usually cleans a sink or bath outlet, or replaces a mixer tap, a toilet or a water heater. Shutting off the risers is arranged by the building manager, and it is agreed before the work.

Low plots on the banks of the Krievupe

The village lies on the banks of the Krievupe. If your plot is lower by the river and the house has a cellar, bear in mind that the groundwater level in Latvia is usually highest in the second half of March and in April, and again in October and November. In those months damp walls and water on the floor at the lowest point can appear.

Before spring, check whether the drainage pump switches on when the float is lifted and whether the discharge pipe is frozen or blocked. If there is no pump, one can be installed: you need a pit, a pump on a solid base and a non-return valve on the discharge. More: drainage and basement pumps.

Water quality and the water heater

In the drinking water sample of 1 June 2026 from a tap at 34 Vidzemes Highway, the pH was 7.4, the electrical conductivity 475 µS/cm and the turbidity 0.47 NTU. Iron and hardness are not stated in this report, so limescale in a water heater is better judged by what you see in the opened tank. Limescale on a heating element depends on hardness, temperature and hot water use, and the manufacturer Dražice advises a lower set temperature so that there are fewer deposits.

Two villages with one name

This page is about the village in Garkalne Parish, Ropaži Municipality, at the A2 and P3 junction, about 21 km from the centre of Riga. In Ādaži Parish there is a separate small village with the same name; we work there too, just enter the municipality in the form to avoid confusion. It is roughly 6 km in a straight line from Ropaži’s Garkalne to the centre of Ādaži (our map reading from OpenStreetMap).

Frequently asked questions

Does the co-funding also cover pipes inside the house?

No. Ropaži Municipality regulations No. 34/23 do not fund indoor networks. The support applies to the external connection and the documentation, but work inside the house has to be paid for by the owner.

I live in a flat in Garkalne. Who is responsible for the riser?

The riser is common property of the building, so the manager decides on it. In a flat the plumber works with the supply branches, the outlets and the appliances, and if the work requires shutting off the riser, you arrange that with the manager before the work.

Will Garkalne Parish be joined to Ādaži Municipality?

In a 2023 survey 64.11% of respondents supported joining the parish to Ādaži Municipality, but the Ropaži Council decided to give up only the Baltezers and Mārsili villages, and in November 2024 the government postponed the boundary change. For now the Ropaži Municipality regulations apply in Garkalne.

The water from the tap is brownish. Is it the water main’s fault?

Not necessarily. If brownish water comes from only a few taps or after standing for a while, the cause is often old galvanised pipes in the house. Describe in the form which taps and when it happens.
