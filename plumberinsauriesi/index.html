Saurieši is a village in Stopiņi Parish by the P5 road, with a school, shops and a plasterboard factory, and since autumn 2024 water and sewerage here have been served by a different company than before. So part of the work is about the water assembly, and part about houses that have their own septic tank.

Why a running toilet costs twice here

In Stopiņi Parish, the tariff of SIA “Garkalnes inženiertīkli”, excluding VAT, is €1.38 per cubic metre of water and €1.85 per cubic metre of sewerage. If a house has a meter and a sewer connection, every litre that quietly runs away through the toilet or a dripping tap is counted both as water and as wastewater.

If water in the toilet runs all the time after flushing, the fault is usually dirt or limescale between the flush valve seal and the outlet opening. If a mixer tap drips even when closed, the ceramic cartridge is usually worn. In both cases the repair is small, but it should be done rather than waited on.

  • Check: in the evening write down the meter reading and compare it in the morning, if nobody used water overnight.
  • If the reading has changed, close the valves at the toilet and the taps one by one to find the culprit.
  • If the meter turns even when everything is closed, the leak may be in the incoming supply pipe underground.

The operator change and the water assembly

Until autumn 2024, water supply and sewerage in Saurieši were provided by the municipal agency “Saimnieks”; in the 2021 regulations of Stopiņi Municipality, Saurieši was among the villages where it had exclusive rights. Now the service is provided by “Garkalnes inženiertīkli”, as in the whole of Stopiņi Parish.

When the operator changes, many people look into the meter chamber or the assembly in the house for the first time in a long while. If the valve before the meter has rusted and no longer closes, the water cannot be shut off in an emergency. Such a valve and the assembly's connections are replaced by a plumber, while the meter seal and the meter itself are handled with the service provider.

Houses with a septic tank: the requirements of Ropaži Municipality

If a house is not connected to the central sewer, the regulations of Ropaži Municipality require the decentralised system to be registered with the service provider and the septic tank or holding tank to be emptied at least once a year. Changes must be reported within a month, and the previous year's water meter readings must be submitted by 1 March each year.

Emptying is the work of a septic tank emptying contractor. The plumber works on the house side: the outlet from the building to the septic tank, its slope, blockages and the pipe freezing if it was laid shallow. A drain outlet from the basement needs at least 20 ‰, that is, a fall of 2 cm per metre. More: connection to a septic tank.

Binding regulations No. 45/22 of Ropaži Municipality (amended by No. 43/24 and No. 15/25): registration, emptying at least once a year, readings by 1 March. It is forbidden to discharge wastewater into a rainwater or drainage system. Checked 13.11.2026.

Older pipes in houses

In 1989 Saurieši had 1,334 residents, so the village has houses that were built long ago. If your house still has galvanised steel pipes, watch for these signs: brownish water after a night, falling pressure and seeps at threaded joints. Then a repair in one place can mean the next leak beside it, so replacing a section is worth weighing up at an inspection.

Hidden leaks in a wall are given away by bubbling paint, yellow stains, a smell of mould or a faint sound of running water. If you notice this, close the incoming supply valve and describe in the form where the dampness is. For urgent cases: water leak.

The yard line to the septic tank in winter

If water drains slowly from the sink only in winter, but everything is fine in summer, the reason can be a yard line laid too shallow. The building code requires an external gravity sewer pipe to be at a depth of no less than 0.7 m to the top of the pipe, and pipes lying shallower must be protected from freezing and traffic load. This applies especially where a car drives over the line or where a path is cleared of snow.

A frozen drain line is not warmed with an open flame. If the line is blocked with grease or roots, it is cleaned with a motorised drain snake, which breaks up the deposits and cuts the roots. If the freezing repeats every winter, the problem is solved only by insulating the pipe or relaying it deeper.

Water quality and reports

“Garkalnes inženiertīkli” publishes drinking water reports for Saurieši for February and December 2025 (sampling point at 9 Burtnieku Street, Upeslejas) and for June 2026. If water from the tap looks or smells different than usual, first compare the cold water tap at the incoming supply with a more distant tap: if they differ, the fault is most likely in the house's pipes or the water heater.

Frequently asked questions

Where do you register a septic tank in Saurieši?

SIA “Garkalnes inženiertīkli” accepts registration confirmations for decentralised systems across Ropaži Municipality, including Saurieši. If you sell the house or rebuild the system, changes must be reported within a month.

May the outlet from a septic tank or a basement be connected to a rainwater drain?

No. The regulations of Ropaži Municipality forbid discharging wastewater into a rainwater or drainage system. If there are appliances in the basement, their outlet must go to the drains or the septic tank.

I have a septic tank. Do I still have to submit meter readings?

Yes. Under the binding regulations No. 45/22 of Ropaži Municipality, the owner of a decentralised system submits the previous year's water meter readings by 1 March each year. From the consumption, it is calculated how often the holding tank must be emptied.
