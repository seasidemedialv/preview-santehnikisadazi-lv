The biggest question here is the rebuilding of Jaunkūlu Street with new water and sewer mains: if your house is on this stretch, the connection and the rebuilding of the internal pipework must be planned afterwards. The rest is everyday work: blockages, taps, water heaters.

The southern edge of Ādaži by the A1: homes next to businesses

Jaunkūlas is a place name in the southern part of Ādaži town, around Jaunkūlu Street, not a separate village. According to OpenStreetMap data, the “Jaunkūlas” stop is on the A1 road and on the Berģi–Baltezers–Ādaži road, and the surroundings include residential areas, industrial areas (a crisps factory, a timber merchant) and a block of flats.

Our work here is homes: private houses and flats. In a house, the plumber's work covers everything from the incoming supply to the last tap and from the toilet to the house outlet. In a flat, the work ends at the shared riser, which the building manager shuts off.

Jaunkūlu Street rebuild 2026–2027: what a house owner can expect

In March 2026, the rebuilding of a 661 m stretch of Jaunkūlu Street began in Ādaži. It includes external water and sewer mains over 469.5 m, from Rīgas gatve to Plostnieku Street, and the work must be finished by 31 December 2027. Water supply and sewerage in Ādaži town are provided by SIA “Ādažu ūdens”.

The binding regulations No. 72/2022 of Ādaži Municipality say: when new main networks are put into operation, an owner in an area planned for the central system must connect within one year. If your house is on this stretch, it is worth planning the connection now rather than after the street opens.

  1. 1. Technical conditions The operator issues them free of charge within 20 working days, and they are valid for at least a year. For new or larger volumes of wastewater, a one-off capacity charge must be paid.
  2. 2. Connection and meter assembly The owner has them built at their own expense with a construction specialist who is entitled to carry out such work, under the operator's supervision.
  3. 3. Inside the house The plumber rebuilds the water pipe from the new meter assembly to the fixtures and the sewer outlet up to the first inspection chamber. We do this part.

Binding regulations No. 72/2022 of Ādaži Municipality (amended by No. 11/2026), likumi.lv, checked 13.11.2026. Co-financing of connections in Ādaži Municipality was abolished in 2022 (binding regulations No. 24/2022); there is none at present.

When the septic tank is no longer needed: rebuilding the outlet

In a house that has so far sent its wastewater to a septic tank, the drain outlet may run towards the septic tank rather than towards the street. When connecting to the street network, the direction of the outlet may have to change, or a new pipe section may have to be laid around the house.

  • A house outlet from the basement needs a slope of at least 20 ‰ (2 cm per metre), and its diameter must not be smaller than the largest connected riser.
  • Where the outlet passes through the foundations, a gap is left around the pipe and filled with a flexible, waterproof material.
  • An external gravity pipe is not laid shallower than 0.7 m to the top of the pipe; a pipe lying shallower is protected from frost and load.

Until the connection is ready, the binding regulations No. 3/2025 of Ādaži Municipality apply: septic tanks are emptied at least once a year, pumping out at least 70% of the volume. Pumping out is not our work; we clean and rebuild the pipe from the house to the septic tank.

Water heater and safety valve in a flat and a house

In the Ādaži water supply zone the hardness in 2026 was 2.43 mmol/l, so limescale builds up in water heaters. If a water heater is not cleaned, limescale settles on the heating element, and it heats more and more slowly.

A separate question is a safety valve that drips water. Water expands when heated, and a little dripping during heating is normal. If it also drips when the water heater is not heating and no water is being used, either the pressure in the water main is too high or the valve is faulty. The maker Dražice advises checking the magnesium anode and, if needed, replacing it after 2 years of operation.

Access during the street works

Ādaži lies by the A1, about 21.6 km from the centre of Riga, and Jaunkūlas is on the southern edge of the town. During the rebuild, the detour for heavy vehicles is along Draudzības and Saules streets. If your street is dug up, write in the form from which side the house can be reached, so that access can be settled on the call.

Frequently asked questions

Will I have to connect to the new Jaunkūlu Street network?

The binding regulations No. 72/2022 of Ādaži Municipality require connecting within one year after the new main networks are put into operation, if the property is in an area planned for the central system. For your exact address, ask the operator SIA “Ādažu ūdens”.

Does the municipality co-finance the connection?

In Ādaži Municipality, co-financing of connections was abolished in 2022 (binding regulations No. 24/2022) because there was no money for it in the budget. There is no co-financing in force at present.

How much does it cost to rebuild the drains in a house when the septic tank is disconnected?

Each internal outlet to a fixture costs from €35 per outlet. For relaying a yard line, the price depends on the job (length, depth, digging, ground). On the call you talk through the job and the price once it is known where the new connection will be.

Water drips from the water heater safety valve. Is that a fault?

A little dripping during heating is normal. If it also drips when the water heater is not heating, check the pressure or the valve.
