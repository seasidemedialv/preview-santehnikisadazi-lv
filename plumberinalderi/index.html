A fast-growing village on the shore of Mazais Baltezers

Alderi is a medium-sized village in Ādaži Parish, on the north-eastern shore of Mazais Baltezers, at the end of the Gauja–Baltezers canal. The canal, with its 1903 sluice, is a state-protected cultural monument and separates Alderi from Ādaži. In 2011 the village had 220 residents; in 2025 it already had 516.

Growth this fast means many new or recently rebuilt houses. In a new house the plumbing is built from scratch: a manifold, water and drain outlets for every appliance, and often water underfloor heating as well. The key moment is before the walls and floors are closed, when the pipes can still be pressure-tested and re-laid without breaking tiles.

Two kinds of house in one village: borehole or street network

From 1 August 2025 the parts of the Baltezers and Alderi villages in Ādaži Parish that were previously served by SIA “Garkalnes ūdens” are served by SIA “Ādažu ūdens”. So part of Alderi already has central networks. At the same time, a residents’ survey expressed a wish to introduce central water supply and extend the sewer network so that more households can connect.

It is worth writing in the form which of the two situations your house is in. If the water comes from the street network, the problem is traced from the meter assembly inwards. If the water comes from your own borehole, the heart of the system is the pump with the hydrophore, and weak pressure or a jerky flow is first of all a question of the pump and the tank.

A hydrophore for a house with its own borehole

A hydrophore set usually consists of a pump, a diaphragm pressure tank, a pressure switch and fittings: a non-return valve, hoses and a pressure gauge. Manufacturers advise checking the tank pre-charge pressure at least once a year. A small pressure loss is normal, but a large drop can point to a worn diaphragm.

  • The pump switches on and off very often: lost pre-charge pressure, a leak in the pipework, a damaged diaphragm or a tank that is too small.
  • If water comes out when you press the tank’s air valve, the diaphragm is damaged.
  • If the pump does not switch off, the cause can be a cut-off pressure that is too high, a stuck non-return valve or a faulty switch.

The pre-charge pressure is measured only when the tank is disconnected and drained of water; otherwise the reading is wrong.

Drains while the network is not everywhere yet

Where there is no street sewer, the house sends wastewater to a septic tank or a biological treatment unit. Ādaži Municipality binding regulations No. 3/2025 require such systems to be registered with the agency “Carnikavas komunālserviss”, changes (a sale, a rebuild) to be reported within a month, and the septic tank to be emptied at least once a year, pumping out at least 70% of its volume.

Near the lake and the canal it matters especially that the line from the house to the septic tank is watertight and laid with the correct fall. Wastewater may be released into the environment from the septic tank only through an infiltration system, for example a drainage field. Our part is the house side: the outlet, the yard pipe, and cleaning or replacing it. Emptying and building septic tanks is done by other specialists.

Ādaži Municipality binding regulations No. 3/2025, likumi.lv, checked 13.11.2026.

When the network reaches your street

Ādaži Municipality binding regulations No. 72/2022 state that after new main networks are put into operation, an owner in areas planned for the central system must connect within one year. The owner builds the connection and the meter assembly at their own expense, with a construction specialist who is entitled to do such work, under the operator’s supervision.

On money, one thing is worth knowing: in Ādaži Municipality the co-funding of connections was cancelled in 2022 (binding regulations No. 24/2022) because the budget had no funds for it, and there is currently no co-funding in force. The indoor part, redirecting the house outlet from the septic tank to the street and disconnecting the water pipe from the borehole, is plumber’s work.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get municipal co-funding for a connection in Alderi?

In Ādaži Municipality the co-funding of connections was cancelled in 2022 by binding regulations No. 24/2022, and there is currently no co-funding in force.

My hydrophore switches on every few seconds. What is wrong?

Usually the tank pre-charge pressure has been lost or the diaphragm is damaged; it can also be a leak in the pipes or a tank that is too small. If water comes out of the air valve, the diaphragm is damaged. The price depends on the fault.

Where do I register a septic tank in Alderi?

In Ādaži Municipality decentralised systems are registered by the agency “Carnikavas komunālserviss” (binding regulations No. 3/2025). On a sale or rebuild, changes must be reported within a month.

Is my house already on the central network?

The part of Alderi that used to be in the SIA “Garkalnes ūdens” zone has been on the SIA “Ādažu ūdens” network since 01.08.2025. For your exact address, ask the operator.
