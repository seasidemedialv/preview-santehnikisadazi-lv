Houses stand far apart, and each can have its own water and sewage solution. So plumbing work here starts with the question of where the house gets its water, and the most common topics are frost, draining for winter and water in the basement in spring.

First: what system your house has

In Iļķene houses stand scattered, and each can have its own water and sewage solution. So we do not assume that a house is or is not connected to a network. In the form, briefly describe four things:

  • where the water comes from: a well, a borehole or a water main;
  • where the pump or hydrophore (a pump with a pressure tank) stands: in the house, in the basement or in a pit in the yard;
  • where the wastewater goes: to a tank, a treatment plant or a network;
  • whether the house is lived in all year and whether it is heated in winter.

The village is about 11 km from Ādaži and 39 km from Riga. These four answers help us understand the job already on the call and say what might be needed.

When a pipe is frozen

A frozen pipe is thawed slowly. First close the incoming supply valve and open a tap so the melting water can run out. Warm it with hot water bottles or a heated cloth, starting from the tap end. Never use an open flame or a blowtorch: all UK water industry guidance (WaterSafe, CCW) forbids it.

If a pipe freezes in the same place every winter, insulation or a heating cable helps. For a self-regulating heating cable, the maker (Danfoss DEVI) requires a thermostat for overheat protection and a residual current device with a trip current of no more than 30 mA. A pipe frozen in the ground, in a basement or in a wall is a different job: the spot has to be found, and sometimes the ground must be dug or the wall opened. In more detail: frozen pipes.

If the house is not heated in winter

In an unheated house at risk of frost, the water heater is drained before the cold weather and disconnected from the electricity on all poles. The maker (Dražice) advises draining the water pipes as well, up to the point where they do not freeze. When draining the water heater, first close the cold water supply and open a hot tap so that no vacuum forms in the tank.

A pump must have the water drained from its chambers, otherwise frozen water can damage the internal parts (Grundfos). If the water comes from a well or a borehole, the hydrophore tank and the pipes leading to it are drained too. If the house has a water-filled heating system, it must also be drained or kept warm; antifreeze is used only in a closed heating circuit, never in drinking water pipes. In spring the system is refilled gradually and the connections are checked. Preparation for winter is covered on the page winterising plumbing.

After a longer absence: the water heater and rarely used taps

If nobody has lived in the house for a while, the water in the pipes and the water heater has been standing. Legionella bacteria multiply considerably in water between +20 °C and +45 °C, including in water heaters and standing water. The Centre for Disease Prevention and Control (SPKC) advises running hot and cold water for a few minutes at least once a week from rarely used taps and showers, and also after a longer absence. SPKC also advises cleaning and disinfecting water heaters at least once a year.

SPKC advises that hot water at every outlet should be above +50 °C and should reach that after running the water for about a minute. If the water heater can no longer manage this, one reason can be limescale on the tank walls and the heating element; the amount depends on the water hardness, the temperature and the amount of hot water used. About cleaning: water heater cleaning.

Near the Gauja and water in the basement

Iļķene lies on the right bank of the Gauja. An old ferry road from Riga to St Petersburg once ran here; before the Vidzeme Highway it was the main land route through Vidzeme. Being near a river does not in itself mean there will be water in the basement, but if it appears, the timing is usually predictable. According to LVĢMC (the Latvian Environment, Geology and Meteorology Centre), groundwater in Latvia is highest in the second half of March and in April, and a second time in October and November.

For a permanent solution, a pit with a drainage pump (sump pump) and a float switch is built in the basement. The pump is placed on a firm base, and its inlet screen must be fully under water while it runs. If water by the house rises quickly, Sadales tīkls (the electricity distribution network operator) calls on people to switch off the electrical circuit breakers or fuses in good time, if this can be done safely; do not approach a flooded fuse board. More: water in the basement.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a central water main in Iļķene?

For a specific address, the water utility can tell you. In Ādaži Municipality the services are provided by SIA “Ādažu ūdens”. It is enough for the plumber to know what your house has: a well, a borehole or a network.

Can a frozen pipe be heated with a gas torch?

No. An open flame can damage the pipe and cause a fire. Close the incoming supply, open a tap and warm the pipe slowly with hot water or a heated cloth.

Water appears in the basement in spring. What should you do first?

If the water is approaching sockets or electrical appliances, switch off the electricity at the fuse board only if this can be done safely, without standing in water. Move belongings higher up and describe in the form how much water there is and where it seems to come from.
