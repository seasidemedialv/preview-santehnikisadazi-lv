Three things affect plumbing work here: the town's rules on connecting to the network, long yard lines to the street or the septic tank, and the low ground by the water, which calls for thought about basement outlets.

The oxbow lake that divides Ādaži into two parts

The Vējupe is one of the largest oxbow lakes of the Gauja: 1.6 km long, 36 ha in area, on average 0.7 m deep and 10 m at the deepest point. It lies within Ādaži town, on the left bank of the Gauja, and is connected to the Gauja through a culvert under Gaujas Street. Our reading of the map: OpenStreetMap also marks a neighbourhood “Vējupe” in Ādaži, by the oxbow lake of the same name.

The oxbow lake divides Ādaži into two parts, and the newer part of the town grew up on its right bank around the former Rembergs Manor. For a plumber this means that one neighbourhood has houses of different ages. In an older house you first need to understand what the pipes are made of and where they run; a newer house more often has a manifold, plastic pipes and underfloor heating.

Pipe replacement that requires connecting to the town network

In the area between Gaujas, Lauku and Druvas streets and the Vējupe, a local plan is in force (binding regulations No. 36/2025 of Ādaži Municipality). It says that new builds must connect to the central water main and sewer if the network is built up to the plot boundary. Connection is also mandatory when a building's water pipe or sewer is replaced.

It is worth thinking about this before the work. If you plan to replace the house's water pipe or sewer, first find out whether the town network is at your plot boundary. If it is, the job can become a connection project, and the operator clarifies this: water supply and sewerage in Ādaži town are provided by SIA “Ādažu ūdens”, which issues the technical conditions.

  1. Technical conditions The operator issues them free of charge within 20 working days (binding regulations No. 72/2022), and they are valid for at least a year.
  2. Connection up to the meter The owner has it built at their own expense with a construction specialist who is entitled to carry out such work, under the operator's supervision.
  3. The internal part New water and sewer pipes in the house, the house outlet up to the first inspection chamber outside. We can build this part.

Binding regulations No. 36/2025 of Ādaži Municipality (local plan) and No. 72/2022, likumi.lv, checked 13.11.2026.

Large plots, long yard lines

In this area the local plan provides for detached houses with a minimum plot of 1,200 m², and 1,600 m² in the first row along the river. On such a plot the house often stands further from the street, and the sewer line in the yard becomes long.

In an external sewer, inspection chambers must be where the direction, slope or diameter changes, and on straight sections every 35 m if the pipe is smaller than 200 mm. If there are no chambers in the yard or they have been buried, a blockage is harder to reach. Then we work with a motorised drain snake from the nearest accessible point, and a heavily clogged line is flushed with high-pressure water.

Where there is no town network yet, the house sends its wastewater to a septic tank. The binding regulations No. 3/2025 of Ādaži Municipality require a septic tank to be emptied at least once a year, pumping out at least 70% of the volume. Pumping out is done by septic tank emptying contractors; our part is the pipe from the house to the septic tank, its slope and blockages.

Low ground by the water and basement fixtures

The water level of the Vējupe is only about 1.5 m above sea level. That does not mean every house has a damp basement, but when planning a shower, a washing machine or a toilet in a basement, it is worth knowing how low they will be relative to the street sewer.

LBN 221-15 (the Latvian Building Code) requires: if a sanitary fixture is below the cover of the nearest inspection chamber, it is connected with a separate outlet and a device that stops the sewer from flooding the basement, for example a backflow valve. If there is no natural fall towards the street, the wastewater is pumped with a compact sewage pump by the toilet or with a pump chamber.

How much does a plumber in the Vējupe area cost?

All prices are labour prices; materials are extra. The minimum call-out price is usually one working hour.

  • Cleaning an outdoor (yard) sewer line: from €50 for the first working hour.
  • High-pressure jetting of drains: from €60 per hour, at least 1 h.
  • Replacing or installing pipes along a wall or floor: from €10/m, chasing and finishing are extra.
  • The plumber's hourly rate: from €25/h.

For installing a sewage pump there is no “from” price, because it depends on the type of pump, the length and lift of the pressure pipe, the electrical connection and the digging work.

Frequently asked questions

If I replace the house water pipe or sewer by the Vējupe, will I have to connect to the town network?

In the area between Gaujas, Lauku and Druvas streets and the Vējupe, the local plan (binding regulations No. 36/2025) requires it if the network is built up to your plot boundary. Find this out from the operator before starting the work.

Does a basement shower need a backflow valve?

If the shower or toilet is below the cover of the nearest inspection chamber, LBN 221-15 requires a separate outlet with a device against the basement flooding from the sewer. If there is no fall, a pump is needed.

How much does it cost to clear a long yard sewer line?

The first working hour with a motorised drain snake costs from €50. If the line has to be flushed with a high-pressure unit, jetting costs from €60 per hour, at least 1 h. In the form, describe how far the street or the septic tank is from the house and whether there are inspection chambers in the yard.

Do you pump out septic tanks?

No. Pumping out a septic tank is done by septic tank emptying contractors registered with the municipality. We work on the house side: the outlet, the pipe to the septic tank and its blockages.
