Plumber in Divezeri - houses without the central networks

A plumber in Divezeri works in houses where everything is the owners’ own: the village has no central water supply or sewerage, and residents use borehole wells and local sewage systems.

The built-up area is low-density detached houses and farmsteads between forests, Lake Dūņezers and Lake Lilaste. So each house’s pump, hydrophore (a pump with a pressure tank) and the pipes to the sewage unit are the owners’ responsibility, and whether the house has water depends on them.

When the electricity goes, the water goes too

The Divezeri village plan says that because of the nearby forests, storms and icing can cause long power outages. In a house with its own borehole, that means the borehole pump does not work without power, and the taps have only the water that is still in the pressure tank. In the heating system the circulation pump stops, and in winter the house starts to cool.

In rooms with cold water pipes the temperature must be above 2 °C, otherwise the pipes have to be protected from freezing (LBN 221-15). When water freezes it expands, and ice takes up about 9% more volume, so a full pipe or a pump housing can burst. If you know the power will be off for a long time and the house is cooling, it is worth closing the incoming supply and draining the pipes in unheated rooms. The manufacturer Grundfos states that a pump not in use in frost conditions must have the water drained from the pump chambers.

When the power returns, water does not always appear at once. If the house has a surface pump with pressure control (for example Grundfos JP), the manufacturer states that if no pressure builds up in the system within 5 minutes after switch-on, dry-run protection activates and the pump switches off. Then you have to find out why the pressure did not build: air in the suction line, an empty borehole or a leak. For frozen pipes: thawing frozen pipes.

The pump does not switch off or the pressure is too weak

A borehole pump together with a hydrophore works according to the pressure switch settings. For a switch with an adjustment range of 1–5 bar, the manufacturer Caleffi states a factory setting of 1.4–2.8 bar. The manufacturer Grundfos advises a cut-out pressure 0.8–1.5 bar higher than the cut-in pressure, with the cut-in pressure calculated from the pressure needed at the highest tap, the height difference up to it and the losses in the pipes.

If the pump runs without stopping, the cause can be a cut-out pressure that is too high for the pump to reach, a non-return valve stuck open or a faulty pressure switch. LBN 221-15 provides that on the pressure line at each pump a one-way valve, shut-off fittings and a pressure gauge are required. If your unit has no pressure gauge, it is hard to say at once where the pressure is being lost.

In the form, write the pump make if you know it, and describe what is happening: the pump runs continuously, switches on often or does not switch on at all. On repair: hydrophore repair.

The road to the house and what to write in the form

According to the municipality’s village plan, Divezeri is about 14 km from the centre of Ādaži. The village has no pronounced centre, covers 5.03 km², has no street lighting or public transport, and residents describe Smilškalnu, Dūņezera, Vecvārnu and Grunduļu streets as potholed and dusty.

So in the form an exact address or house name and a short description of the problem are important. The more precise the description, the sooner the job is clear on the call. If there is no water at all, also write whether the house currently has electricity.

A local sewer: the line, registration, emptying

In all Divezeri houses wastewater flows to the owners’ own sewage system. The plumber’s work is the line from the house to the unit: the fall, the joints, the cleaning points and the depth. An external gravity sewer pipe is laid no shallower than 0.7 m to the top of the pipe, and shallower pipes must be protected from freezing. Sandy ground freezes deeper than clayey: LBN 003-19 has the frost depth of clayey ground multiplied by 1.2 for sandy ground.

The systems themselves are regulated in Ādaži Municipality by binding regulations No. 3/2025. Unregistered decentralised systems must be registered with the agency “Carnikavas komunālserviss”, and changes must be reported within a month. A septic tank must be emptied at least once a year, and only septic tank contractors registered with the municipality may carry the waste away; that is not plumber’s work. The explanatory note to the regulations says that in Ādaži Parish there are about 1,100 such systems, of which 915 are registered.

Ādaži Municipality binding regulations No. 3/2025 “Procedure for providing and recording decentralised sewerage services”, in force from 04.03.2025. Checked 13.11.2026 on likumi.lv.

The Puska river, culverts and water in a pit

The village is divided by the Puska river and forests; residents mention an overgrown lake and the need to put the culverts and the outflow of the Ciemeļupīte stream in order. The plan provides for renewing a culvert under Smilškalnu Road, and the municipality maintains drainage systems to reduce the risks of floods and heavy rainfall.

If water collects in a house cellar or a borehole pit after rains, a drainage pump pumps it out. Its inlet strainer must be fully under water during operation, and the pump is placed on a solid base so that silt does not clog the strainer. Remember that a drainage pump, like a borehole pump, does not work without electricity. More: water in the cellar.

Frequently asked questions

After the power came back, the pump gives no water. What should I check?

First the pressure gauge: if there is no pressure, the pump may have switched off because of dry-run protection. On many pumps it trips if no pressure builds up within a set time after switch-on. Do not switch the pump on again many times in a row, but describe the situation in the form.

Can a house be left without heating in winter?

Only if the water system and also the heating system have been drained, because radiators and a heating-boiler circuit filled with water freeze just the same. The heating circuit can contain antifreeze, but it is used only in a closed heating circuit, never in drinking water pipes. In rooms with cold water pipes the temperature must be above 2 °C. If the house will stand unheated, close the incoming supply and drain the pipes, the water heater and the pump; more: plumbing winterising.

Where do I register a local sewer in Divezeri?

In Ādaži Municipality decentralised systems are registered by the agency “Carnikavas komunālserviss” under binding regulations No. 3/2025. Changes, such as a rebuild or the sale of the property, must be reported within a month.

The pressure at the tap is weak only on the second floor. Why?

The cut-in pressure must also cover the height difference up to the highest tap and the losses in the pipes. If the switch is set low or the filter is blocked, the upper floor feels it first. More about this: low water pressure.
