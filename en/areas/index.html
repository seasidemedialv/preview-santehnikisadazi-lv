Ādaži Municipality

Ādaži has been a town since 1 July 2022 and is the centre of the municipality, while Carnikava became a town on 22 August 2026. In the town of Ādaži we also work in Podnieki, the Vējupe area and Jaunkūlas. Villages: Alderi, Atari, Baltezers, Divezeri, Eimuri, Iļķene, Kadaga and Stapriņi, as well as Carnikava.

The central water supply and sewerage in the municipality are provided by SIA “Ādažu ūdens” (the Ādaži water utility), but not everywhere. Divezeri has no central water supply or sewerage, and in most of Eimuri and in Atari people mostly use their own wells and septic tanks, so hydrophore and pressure questions often come up there. Groundwater is usually highest in spring and autumn, and then water can appear in basements near the Gauja and the lakes.

Villages of Ropaži Municipality

In Ropaži Municipality we work in Garkalne and Upesciems in Garkalne parish, and in Saurieši and Ulbroka in Stopiņi parish. Water supply and sewerage here are provided by SIA “Garkalnes inženiertīkli” (the Garkalne utility networks). Binding regulations No. 34/23 of Ropaži Municipality provide co-financing of 50% for the connection of a residential house, but they do not cover the house's internal networks.

The north-eastern edge of Riga

Berģi and Bukulti are both Riga neighbourhoods and villages in Garkalne parish, and next to Riga's Dreiliņi is the village of Dreiliņi in Stopiņi parish, so we work on both sides of the city boundary: Berģi, Bukulti, Dreiliņi and Suži by Lake Ķīšezers. On the Riga side, water services are provided by Rīgas ūdens. In the southern part of Berģi, phase 5 of a Cohesion Fund project built 19.70 km of sewer, allowing more than 400 properties to connect, while the construction of a water main and pressure sewer in Bukulti is planned for 2026–2027.
