Plumber per hour: from €25/h

The plumber's hourly rate is from €25/h for one hour of work. The minimum charge for a call-out is usually one hour. The hourly rate suits jobs whose scope is hard to state in advance, for example when you have to find out why pressure drops or where water is disappearing.

The call-out costs from €40: the master plumber's arrival, inspection and finding the problem. If the work is done straight away, the call-out is usually included in the price of the work.

How the price is made up

All prices are “from” prices because two similar jobs can take different amounts of time. The final price depends on what has to be done in your house. On the call you talk through the job and the price before the work starts.

  • Access: whether the pipe or joint is visible, or a wall or floor has to be opened, or the yard dug. Chasing and making good the finish are extra.
  • Appliance: a wall-hung water heater of up to about 100 l costs differently from a large tank, and a floor-standing toilet differently from a concealed frame.
  • Water source: in a house with its own well or borehole, the job often also involves the hydrophore, the non-return valve and the pressure tank.
  • Materials: pipes, fittings, taps, seals and spare parts are paid for separately from the work.
  • Distance: the travel charge outside Ādaži depends on the distance.

The travel charge outside Ādaži depends on the distance, and we tell you on the call before the work. If you are not sure whether your place is in our work area, give the district and address in the form. All places: where we work.

Price examples

  • Replacing a kitchen mixer tap: from €25 per job. If new flexible hoses are needed, they are extra, as is the tap itself.
  • Replacing a toilet: from €50 for installing and connecting the new floor-standing toilet. Removing the old toilet is usually +€10–35.
  • A blocked yard sewer line between the house and the inspection chamber: from €50 for the first hour with a motorised drain snake. If the line has to be flushed, high-pressure jetting costs from €60 an hour, minimum 1 h.
  • The old bathroom plumbing has to be replaced completely: from €450 for the work in a standard room. Materials, tiling and making good the chases are extra.

Jobs whose price depends on the situation

For hydrophores, drainage and sewage pumps, thawing frozen pipes, winterising, water heater repair and heating flushing there are no fixed “from” prices. The scope of these jobs varies too much: one hydrophore only needs the pressure switch replaced, another needs a new pump, non-return valve and supply pipes. Frequently asked questions

How much does a plumber cost per hour?

The plumber's hourly rate is from €25/h. The minimum charge for a call-out is usually one hour. It is the labour price; materials are extra.

Do I pay for the call-out if the work is done straight away?

The call-out costs from €40, but if the work is done straight away, it is usually included in the price of the work.

Are materials included in the price?

No. All prices on this page are labour prices. Pipes, fittings, appliances and spare parts are extra.

Why is there no price for a hydrophore?

Because the fault can be in the pressure switch, the diaphragm, the non-return valve or the motor, and sometimes a repair does not pay off compared with a new pump. Describe the situation in the form, and on the call you talk through the job and the price.
