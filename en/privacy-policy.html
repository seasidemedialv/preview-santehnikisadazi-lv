Privacy policy

This page describes how santehnikisadazi.lv processes the personal data you enter in the request form. The site does not use cookies. The policy was updated on 13.11.2026.

Controller

The controller of personal data is the service provider operating with the site santehnikisadazi.lv. Send questions about data processing and requests to exercise your rights with the form on the page contact, stating in the extra field that the question is about personal data.

What data we process

  • In the request form: name, phone number, district, address, type of problem, urgency and the extra information you write.
  • Technical data processed by the hosting provider to keep the site running and secure: IP address, browser and device information and the time of the request.

Please do not write data in the form that is not needed for the work, especially health or other sensitive data.

Cookies

The site does not set cookies and does not use advertising or visitor-tracking tools. If this changes, we will update this policy before the change.

Why and on what basis

Purpose Data Legal basis
Answering the request, contacting you and talking through the job and the price Form data Steps taken before entering into a contract at your request, GDPR Article 6(1)(b)
Doing the requested work Form data, address Performance of a contract, GDPR Article 6(1)(b)
Meeting accounting and tax requirements Transaction data Legal obligation, GDPR Article 6(1)(c)
Keeping a record of requests, resolving disputes and site security Form and technical data Legitimate interests, GDPR Article 6(1)(f)

Who we pass the data to

  • The specialist who does the work you requested. They receive only the data needed to carry out the request.
  • Processors used by the site and for handling requests: providers of site hosting, form processing and automation, and messaging services. They process the data only on our behalf under GDPR Article 28.
  • Public authorities, if required by law.

We do not sell your data and do not pass it to third parties for marketing.

Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Some service providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. In that case the transfer takes place only with the safeguards provided in Chapter V of the GDPR, for example on the basis of a European Commission adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep the data

We keep the request data for as long as is needed to answer the request, do the work and resolve possible questions about it. Data linked to a transaction is kept for as long as accounting and tax law requires. After that the data is deleted.

Your rights

  • To access your data and receive a copy of it.
  • To correct inaccurate data.
  • To request erasure of the data or restriction of processing.
  • To object to processing that takes place on the basis of legitimate interests.
  • To receive your data in a portable format, if the processing is based on a contract.

If you believe your data has been processed incorrectly, you can lodge a complaint with the Data State Inspectorate (Datu valsts inspekcija).

Changes

We may update this policy if the site, the form or the law changes. The version published on this page is the one in force. Terms of use: terms of use.
