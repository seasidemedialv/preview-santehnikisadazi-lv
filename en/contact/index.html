What to enter in the form

  • Name and phone number, so we can contact you.
  • District and address: street and house number, and for a flat also the flat number.
  • Type of problem: emergency, pipes, drains, mixer tap, water heater, bathroom or other.
  • Urgency: as soon as possible, this week or plan for later.
  • Extra information: a short description of the situation.

What to describe in the extra field

You cannot attach a photo to the form, so describe the situation in words. The clearer the description, the more precisely you can talk through the job and the price on the call.

  • What is happening and where: for example, a joint on the water heater in the basement is leaking, or the bath drains slowly.
  • Since when, and whether it repeats.
  • Type of home: house, terraced house or flat.
  • Water source: central water supply, or your own well or borehole with a hydrophore.
  • Drains: central sewer or septic tank.
  • The maker or model of the appliance, if you know it, and what has already been done.

If water is leaking right now

Close the valve on the incoming water supply, in a house with its own well switch off the hydrophore, and if the water heater is leaking, switch off its power. Move valuables away from the water. Then fill in the form with the urgency “as soon as possible”. More in how to shut off the water in a house.

Frequently asked questions

Why is there no phone number?

We take requests only with the form. This way all the information needed reaches the specialist at once, and after you submit the form, the specialist contacts you within 5 minutes.

Can I write in English or Russian?

Yes, you can write your request in Latvian, English or Russian.

Can I attach a photo to the form?

No, you cannot attach a photo to the form. Describe the situation in the extra field: what is happening, where and what the appliance is.

What if I do not know which type of problem to choose?

Choose “other” and describe the situation in the extra field. On the call you work out together what job is needed.
