Leaks and pipe repair in Ādaži

If a joint, tap or pipe is leaking, first close the valve on the incoming supply. In a house with its own well or borehole, also switch off the hydrophore so the pump does not push water into the damaged spot. If water reaches anything electrical, switch the power off at the fuse board only if you can do so safely without standing in water.

Stopping a leak or seep in an accessible spot costs from €40: finding the leak and replacing a joint, a seal or a short piece of pipe. Fitting one water or drain outlet costs from €35, and pipe replacement from €10/m. More in emergency plumber and pipe repair.

Drain cleaning in Ādaži

A blockage in the drains inside the house is cleared with a drain snake: a rotating spiral breaks up grease deposits and frees the dirt. If that is not enough, the line is flushed with a high-pressure water jet, which removes deposits from the pipe walls. In a house, the blockage is often in the yard sewer line between the house and the inspection chamber or septic tank.

Clearing a blockage in the drains inside the house costs from €50, cleaning the yard sewer line from €50 for the first hour, and high-pressure jetting from €60 an hour. More in drain cleaning.

Water heater installation and repair

Installing or replacing a wall-hung water heater costs from €70: the water heater is fixed to the wall and connected to the existing supply pipes with a safety valve. Cleaning, or descaling, costs from €75. The repair price depends on what is damaged (heating element, thermostat, anode or safety valve), on the spare parts for the particular model and on whether the repair pays off compared with a new water heater. More in water heaters.

Hydrophore and water pressure in a house

In a house with its own well or borehole, the water is supplied by a hydrophore. The set usually consists of a pump, a pressure tank with a diaphragm, a pressure switch and fittings. If the pump often switches on and off, the usual causes are a wrong or lost tank pre-charge pressure, a leak in the pipes or a damaged diaphragm. If water comes out when you press the air valve of the tank, the diaphragm is damaged.

The price of installing a hydrophore depends on whether it is a new installation or a replacement, on the distance to the well and the suction depth, and on where it is installed. The repair price depends on the fault and the spare parts. More in hydrophores.

Frozen pipes and winterising

When water freezes it expands: ice takes up about 9% more volume, so a pipe or pump body full of water can burst. Never heat a frozen pipe with an open flame. Close the valve on the incoming supply, open a tap so the melting water can run out, and heat slowly with hot-water bottles or a warmed cloth, starting from the tap end.

The price of thawing depends on where the pipe has frozen (indoors, in the basement or in the ground), on the material and on whether digging or opening a wall is needed. If the house will stand empty in winter, the water system is drained. More in plumbing winterising.

What is typical for houses in Ādaži and the area

In many places outside the centre of Ādaži, a house has its own water and its own drains. Divezeri has no central water supply or sewerage, residents use borehole wells and local sewage systems; the same applies to most of Eimuri. In Atari the networks cover only part of the village, so people mostly use their own water supply systems and septic tanks. In such houses, a plumber's work often starts at the hydrophore, the pressure tank and the line from the well.

The networks are slowly expanding. In phase III of the project in Carnikava, a water main and sewer have been built, for example, in Līņu, Vimbu and Dambja streets. The reconstruction of Jaunkūlu street in Ādaži includes about 470 m of external networks, planned for completion by the end of 2027. Binding regulations No. 72/2022 of Ādaži Municipality say that in areas planned for the central system, the owner must connect within one year after new main networks are put into operation. How this works is in connecting to the central sewer.

Water in the basement is another local issue. A municipal project document states that Ādaži lies in an area of national-level flood risk, and in March 2021 an ice jam on the Gauja near Carnikava raised the water by about a metre. Groundwater in Latvia is usually highest in the second half of March and in April, and again in October and November. Appliances in a basement that sit lower than the cover of the nearest inspection chamber are connected with a separate outlet and a backflow valve. Pumps: drainage and sewage pumps.

Some houses are former summer houses. Carnikava became a summer-house resort for Riga residents at the beginning of the 20th century, and Kadaga includes Upmalas, a summer-house village between Lake Kadaga and the Gauja. When such a house starts to be used all year, the old pipes, their insulation and the lines under the yard need to be reviewed. An external sewer pipe lying at a depth of 0.7 m or less must be protected from freezing. All the work on a house in one place: plumbing for a house.

You cannot attach a photo to the form, so describe the situation in words: what is happening, where, since when and what has already been done. If you know the maker or model of the appliance, write it in.

Do you also pump out septic tanks?

No. Emptying a septic tank is a septic-tank contractor's job, and in Ādaži Municipality only contractors registered with the municipality may do it. A plumber works on the house side: the drains inside the house, the outlet from the house and the line to the septic tank.
