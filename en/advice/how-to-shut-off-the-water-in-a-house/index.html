How to shut off the water in a house

To shut off the water in a house, close the main incoming valve, but if the water comes from your own well or borehole, first cut the power to the hydrophore (a pump with a pressure tank). If the leak is at the water heater, also switch off its electricity and close its cold water supply. It is better to find the valve now than when water is already running.

What to do straight away if water is leaking

  1. Switch off the pump. In a house with its own well or borehole, the hydrophore keeps pumping water into the leak as long as it has power. Switch off the pump's switch or its circuit breaker in the fuse board.
  2. Close the incoming valve. UK water companies state that the main valve is usually where the water pipe enters the house, and it closes by turning clockwise.
  3. Switch off the water heater. If the leak is at the water heater or the hot water pipes, switch off the water heater's power and close its cold water supply.
  4. Beware of electricity. If water reaches sockets or the fuse board, switch the electricity off only if it can be done safely. Keep away from appliances standing in water and from bare wires.
  5. Open a tap at the lowest point. This lets the remaining water drain out of the pipes, and less will flow at the leak.
  6. Move valuables and describe the situation. Lift anything the water could damage off the floor, and write in the form where it leaks and what is already closed.

Where to look for the valve in a house with its own well or borehole

Shut-off fittings are mandatory at the hydrophore unit: Latvian Building Code LBN 221-15 requires a non-return valve, shut-off fittings and a pressure gauge on the delivery line of each pump, and shut-off fittings and a pressure gauge on the suction line. So the first valve after the hydrophore pressure tank usually shuts off the whole house. The hydrophore is often in the basement, a utility room or an insulated box where the pipe from the well enters the house.

If you close only the valve but leave the pump on, the pump will stop when it reaches its cut-out pressure. If the leak is between the pump and the valve, it will continue, so cutting the power is the first step. Pressure remains in the tank and pipes, so water still runs for a moment after the pump is switched off.

LBN 221-15 “Internal Water Supply and Sewerage of Buildings” (Cabinet Regulation No. 332), clause 170. Checked 13.11.2026.

Where to look for the valve if you have a central water supply

The incoming supply usually ends with a meter assembly in a meter pit in the yard or in the house. The binding regulations of Ādaži Municipality allow the meter to be placed in a heated room no more than 2 m from the outer wall if it cannot go in the standard place. So in the house, look for the valve at the outer wall where the pipe enters.

If the house has a pressure reducer, LBN 221-15 requires shut-off fittings after it as well. When you close the valve after the meter, the house water pipe is shut off, but there is still pressure in the meter pit and in the incoming supply up to it.

Binding regulations No. 72/2022 of Ādaži Municipality on the meter location; LBN 221-15, clause 131. Checked 13.11.2026.

How to shut off the water to a single appliance

  • Sink and toilet supplies usually have small angle valves under the fixture. They are closed when a tap, a flexible hose or the cistern mechanism has to be changed.
  • For a concealed cistern the valve is in the cistern: Geberit Sigma cisterns have a built-in angle shut-off valve on the water supply, reached through the service opening behind the flush plate.
  • For a water heater, close the cold water valve before the safety valve. The maker Dražice states that shut-off valves must not be fitted between the water heater and the safety valve.
  • The heating system is a separate closed loop: closing the water supply valve does not drain the radiators.

If the water heater has to be drained

First disconnect the water heater from the power. The maker Dražice states: close the cold water supply and open a hot water tap so that no negative pressure builds up in the tank; it can also be drained through the safety valve in its test position.

In an unheated house at risk of frost, the water heater is drained before cold weather and disconnected from the electricity on all poles. The same maker advises draining the water pipes too, to the point where they do not freeze.

If the house will stand empty

If the house will stand empty for a long time, the water industry's advice is to close the incoming valve and drain the system so that no water stays in the pipes. The maker Grundfos states that a pump not in use during a frost period must have all its water drained, otherwise frozen water can damage pump parts. More on this on the page winterising a summer house water system.

What not to do

  • Do not force a tool onto a valve that will not move: an old valve can break, and then it leaks even more.
  • Do not leave the hydrophore on if the leak is between the pump and the valve.
  • Do not fit a shut-off valve between the water heater and its safety valve.
  • Do not heat a frozen pipe with an open flame. Close the incoming valve, open a tap and heat slowly, starting from the tap side.
  • Do not use electrical appliances that have got wet until an electrician has confirmed it is safe.

When to call a plumber and what it costs

You need a plumber if the valve does not close fully, if the valve itself leaks, if the house has no main valve, or if water keeps running after closing. Then a new valve can be fitted at a place that can be reached.

Frequently asked questions

Which way do I turn the valve to close it?

Usually clockwise. On a ball valve, the handle in the closed position is across the pipe.

Is it enough to switch off the hydrophore?

The pump no longer pumps, but pressure remains in the tank and pipes, so water still runs for a while. Close the valve after the tank as well.

Do I have to cut the power before draining a water heater?

Yes. Then close the cold water supply and open a hot water tap, as the maker Dražice states, so that no negative pressure builds up in the tank.
