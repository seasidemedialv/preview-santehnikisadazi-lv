Connecting to the central sewer - in Ādaži and Ropaži Municipalities

Connecting to the central sewer in Ādaži and Ropaži Municipalities starts with technical conditions from the water utility, not with digging. In Ādaži Municipality the operator is SIA “Ādažu ūdens”, and in Garkalne Parish and Stopiņi Parish of Ropaži Municipality it is SIA “Garkalnes inženiertīkli”. In Ropaži Municipality the municipality co-funds up to 50% of the connection costs, while in Ādaži Municipality the support of the former Carnikava Municipality has been abolished. Work inside the house remains the owner's responsibility.

Apply to the company whose network runs past your property. SIA “Garkalnes ūdens” was split up in 2025 and no longer exists.

In Ādaži Municipality: steps to the connection

  1. Technical conditions. SIA “Ādažu ūdens” issues them free of charge within 20 working days, and they are valid for at least a year. Work is not started without them.
  2. Capacity charge. If the house will discharge a new or larger volume of wastewater into the network, a one-off capacity charge is payable to SIA “Ādažu ūdens”. Ask the operator for the amount.
  3. Construction at your own expense. The applicant builds the connection and the commercial metering assembly at their own expense. The work is done by a suitably qualified contractor under the operator's supervision.
  4. Meter location. If the water meter cannot be installed in the standard place, the regulations allow it to be placed in a heated room no more than 2 m from the outer wall.
  5. Contract with the operator. The Water Management Services Law states that services are provided only under a concluded contract.

Binding regulations No. 72/2022 of Ādaži Municipality “Procedure for Public Water Management Services in Ādaži Municipality” (in force from 02.11.2022, amended by binding regulations No. 11/2026); Water Management Services Law. Checked 13.11.2026.

In Ropaži Municipality: co-funding up to 50%

Binding regulations No. 34/23 of Ropaži Municipality (adopted 18.10.2023, in force from 01.01.2024) provide co-funding of 50% for connecting a residential house to the central water supply and sewer.

For what Co-funding
Sewer connection 50%, up to €3,000, no more than €60 per metre
Water supply connection 50%, up to €2,000, no more than €60 per metre
Documentation up to €300
Low-income households, large families, single pensioners, persons with disabilities and others may be in full, up to €8,000 and €120 per metre

Conditions:

  • a declared place of residence in the municipality for at least 6 months;
  • no real estate tax debt;
  • the main network is by the property;
  • the work must be completed within 6 months of the contract being concluded.

Internal networks are not funded, so rebuilding pipes in the house remains the owner's responsibility. Ask the municipality exactly where the co-funded connection ends before signing the contract.

Binding regulations No. 34/23 of Ropaži Municipality on co-funding for connections to central networks (adopted 18.10.2023, in force from 01.01.2024). Checked 13.11.2026.

Co-funding abolished in Ādaži Municipality

In the former Carnikava Municipality, the municipality co-funded a connection to the central sewer up to 50%, or €1,200, and together with the water supply up to 60%, or €1,700. Under binding regulations No. 24/2022 of Ādaži Municipality this support was abolished from 10 May 2022, because the budget had no funds for it.

So in Ādaži Municipality, plan on paying the connection costs yourself. Before the work, ask the municipality whether new support rules have been adopted.

Binding regulations No. 24/2022 of Ādaži Municipality, which abolished the former Carnikava Municipality binding regulations SN/2018/3 on co-funding. Checked 13.11.2026.

When connecting is mandatory

In Ādaži Municipality, in areas planned for the central system, the owner must connect within one year of the new main networks being put into operation.

At national level, clause 18 of Cabinet Regulation No. 384 stated: if a decentralised system did not meet the requirements by 31.12.2021 and there is a central sewer by the street or road adjoining the property, the connection had to be made by 31.12.2023.

Until the house is connected, a septic tank or holding tank is a decentralised system, and the municipality's regulations apply to it:

  • In Ādaži Municipality (binding regulations No. 3/2025, in force from 04.03.2025): the system must be registered with the agency “Carnikavas komunālserviss”; a septic tank and a holding tank must be emptied at least once a year, pumping out at least 70% of the volume. Only a sewage collector registered with the municipality may carry it away, and the documents must be kept for at least 2 years.
  • In Ropaži Municipality (binding regulations No. 45/22, amended by No. 43/24 and No. 15/25): the registration confirmation must be submitted to SIA “Garkalnes inženiertīkli”; septic tanks and holding tanks must be emptied at least once a year; the previous year's water meter readings must be submitted by 1 March each year.
  • In both municipalities, changes such as a sale or rebuild must be reported within a month, and for violations individuals receive a warning or a fine of 2–70 fine units.

Binding regulations No. 72/2022 and No. 3/2025 of Ādaži Municipality; binding regulations No. 45/22 of Ropaži Municipality; Cabinet Regulation No. 384 “Regulations on the Management and Registration of Decentralised Sewerage Systems”, clause 18. Checked 13.11.2026.

What has to be rebuilt on the house side

Under LBN 221-15, a building's internal drains run from the sanitary fixtures to the first inspection chamber outside the building. When switching from a septic tank to the network, this part is often extended or moved to the new connection point, and it has its own requirements:

  • The house outlet from the basement must have a slope of at least 20 ‰ (2 cm per metre), and its diameter must not be smaller than the largest connected riser.
  • The length of the outlet from a riser or inspection point to the axis of the inspection chamber must not exceed 8 m for a 50 mm pipe, 12 m for a 100 mm pipe and 15 m for a 150 mm pipe.
  • The external gravity pipe is laid 0.3 m shallower than the standard soil frost depth that can occur once in 100 years, but no shallower than 0.7 m to the pipe surface; shallower pipes must be protected from frost and traffic load.
  • Fixtures that are lower than the cover of the nearest inspection chamber, for example in a basement, are connected with a separate discharge and a backflow valve, so that the external drain does not flood the basement.
  • In Ādaži Municipality rainwater may be discharged into the sewer only with the operator's permission, and pouring it into inspection chambers is forbidden. The Ropaži Municipality regulations forbid discharging wastewater into a rainwater or drainage system.

LBN 221-15 “Internal Water Supply and Sewerage of Buildings” (Cabinet Regulation No. 332), clauses 5, 259, 260, 262 and 263; LBN 223-15 “Sewerage Structures” (Cabinet Regulation No. 327), clause 67. Checked 13.11.2026.

What not to do

  • Do not start digging or buy pipes until you have the technical conditions.
  • Do not connect roof downpipes to the new connection without the operator's permission.
  • Do not sign a Ropaži co-funding contract if you know you will not finish the work in 6 months.
  • Do not take away the septic tank contents yourself: until you are connected, only a registered sewage collector may do that.

When you need a plumber and what it costs

A plumber prepares the house side: moves the internal drain pipes from the old septic tank outlet to the new direction, builds the outlets and fits a separate discharge with a backflow valve for basement fixtures. Building the external connection and digging is a separate job, done by a contractor under the operator's supervision.

Inside the house, making or replacing a drain outlet up to a fixture costs from €35 per outlet, replacing pipes along a wall or floor from €10/m, and a plumber call-out from €40. This is the labour price; materials are extra; chasing and making good the finish are also separate. The price of a line in the yard depends on the job: it is set by the length, the depth, the digging and the soil.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get co-funding for a sewer connection in Ādaži Municipality?

The former Carnikava Municipality co-funding was abolished from 10 May 2022, because the budget had no funds for it. Before the work, ask the municipality whether new support has been adopted.

Does the Ropaži co-funding cover work in the house?

No. Regulations No. 34/23 do not fund internal networks. The co-funding applies to the connection and the documentation, with limits both on the total and per metre.

How long do technical conditions take in Ādaži Municipality?

SIA “Ādažu ūdens” issues them free of charge within 20 working days, and they are valid for at least a year.

May rainwater be connected to the sewer?

In Ādaži Municipality only with the operator's permission, and pouring it into inspection chambers is forbidden. In Ropaži Municipality discharging wastewater into a rainwater or drainage system is forbidden.
