Low water pressure in a house - causes and what to check

Low water pressure in a house can come from a blocked aerator or filter at the tap, from the pressure reducer or the hydrophore (a pump with a pressure tank) settings, from a leak, or from old pipes that are too narrow. First find out whether one tap runs weakly or the whole house, and whether the water comes from the central water supply or your own well. That decides where to look.

Tap, aerator and filters

The maker Neoperl states that limescale and other deposits build up in aerators over time and reduce the flow. An aerator is cleaned or replaced at the latest when limescale is visible or the stream has become uneven.

If too little water comes from a mixer tap, hansgrohe advises checking the supply pressure (and the pump, if there is one) and cleaning the filters at the mixer tap inlets and in the shower hose seal. For thermostatic mixers the hot and cold water pressure must be equal.

Pressure reducer at the incoming supply

A reducer protects the house water pipe against too high a pressure and keeps the outlet pressure constant even if it fluctuates at the inlet. For the D06F model the maker Resideo states a factory-set outlet pressure of 3 bar and an adjustment range of 1.5–6 bar. A filter must be fitted before the reducer.

The same maker gives these reasons for low pressure: shut-off valves that are not fully open, a pressure that has not been set, a blocked reducer filter, or a valve installed against the flow. Under EN 806-5 such fittings are checked and serviced once a year.

Hydrophore: settings and tank

In a house with its own well, the pressure is set by the hydrophore pressure switch. The maker Grundfos advises calculating the cut-in pressure like this: the minimum pressure needed at the highest tap, plus the height difference up to it, plus losses in the pipes. The cut-out pressure must be 0.8–1.5 bar higher. Factory settings differ by product: for a Caleffi switch they are 1.4–2.8 bar, and for Grundfos JP PT pumps the cut-in pressure is 2.2 bar.

For Grundfos JP pumps the tank pre-charge is advised at 0.2 bar below the cut-in pressure, and in other Grundfos documents it is 90% of the cut-in pressure. It is measured and adjusted only when the tank is disconnected and completely emptied.

If the pump starts and stops often, the reason is usually a lost pre-charge, a leak, a damaged diaphragm or a tank that is too small. If water comes out when you press the tank's air valve, the diaphragm is damaged. A surface pump also has a suction limit: for Grundfos JP models it is 8 m including losses, so from a deeper well such a pump will not deliver the pressure needed.

Pipes and leaks

LBN 221-15 requires pipe diameters to be chosen so that the pressure of the external network is used to the maximum: too small a diameter reduces the pressure at the taps. This can happen in summer houses that are rebuilt for year-round living and have more fixtures added to the old pipes.

In galvanised steel pipes, once the zinc has worn away, the steel corrodes and rust deposits form inside, narrowing the pipe. The US water utility EBMUD states that this causes low pressure and brownish water.

If the pressure drops even when no tap is open, there may be a leak. WaterSafe advises closing all taps, connecting a pressure gauge and watching whether the pressure drops over a few hours. A leak in an underground incoming supply can be given away by damp patches, a sunken lawn or paving, and unusually green grass in dry weather.

LBN 221-15 “Internal Water Supply and Sewerage of Buildings” (Cabinet Regulation No. 332), clauses 35 and 70. Checked 13.11.2026.

What not to do

  • Do not change the pressure switch setting without readjusting the tank pre-charge: Grundfos states that they must be changed together.
  • Do not set the cut-out pressure higher than the pump can reach, otherwise it will not switch off.
  • Do not exceed 6 bar: LBN 221-15 states that the pressure at the lowest outlet must not be higher than 0.6 MPa.
  • Do not remove the pressure reducer to get more pressure: it protects the water heater and the appliances. The maker Dražice requires a reducer if the pressure exceeds 80% of the opening pressure of the water heater's safety valve.
  • Do not measure the tank pre-charge while there is water in it.

When to call a plumber and what it costs

If the aerator and filters are clean but the pressure is still weak, or if the pump clicks and starts often, an inspection is needed. The price of hydrophore repair depends on the fault (pressure switch, diaphragm, non-return valve, motor), on whether the pump has to be removed and taken to a workshop, on the price of spare parts, and on whether the repair pays off against a new pump. The price of checking the pressure tank depends on whether adjusting the air pressure is enough or the diaphragm has to be replaced, on the size of the tank, and on whether the system has to be drained.

Installing or replacing a mixer tap costs from €25, replacing pipes from €10/m, and a plumber call-out from €40. This is the labour price; materials are extra.

Frequently asked questions

What should the pressure in the house water pipe be?

LBN 221-15 sets the upper limit: at the lowest outlet the pressure must not exceed 6 bar. For mixer taps hansgrohe advises 1–5 bar. For washing machines Bosch states 1–10 bar and a flow of at least 8 l/min, but for some models the minimum is higher: see your appliance's instructions.

Why does the hydrophore start and stop often?

Usually because the tank pre-charge is lost or wrong, there is a leak in the pipes, the diaphragm is damaged or the tank is too small. If water comes out of the tank's air valve, the diaphragm is damaged.

Can the pressure be increased by turning the switch?

It can, but only within the pump's capability and together with the tank pre-charge. If the cut-out pressure is higher than the pump can reach, it will not switch off.

Why did the pressure become weak after a repair?

Resideo names shut-off valves that are not fully open and a blocked filter as reasons for low pressure. Check the valves, the reducer filter and the tap aerators.
