Sewer smell in the house - where it comes from

A sewer smell in a house appears when drain air gets past the water seal in a trap, or escapes where a line is not airtight. In a house the possible sources are a dried-out trap in a rarely used shower or drain, a damaged or missing trap, the drain venting, and a full septic tank or holding tank. Start with the simplest: pour water into every rarely used fixture.

How a trap holds back the smell

A trap holds water that stops drain air from entering the room. The maker Geberit gives a 50 mm deep water seal for a sink trap. UK guidance, for example, sets 75 mm for a sink and a kitchen sink and 50 mm for a bath, shower and toilet, and under it at least 25 mm of water must remain in the trap in use, otherwise air gets into the room.

LBN 221-15 states: if a fixture has no built-in trap, one is fitted under the fixture, and several fixtures in different rooms must not be connected to one trap.

LBN 221-15 “Internal Water Supply and Sewerage of Buildings” (Cabinet Regulation No. 332), clauses 213 and 216; UK Approved Document H (2015). Checked 13.11.2026.

What to do yourself

  1. Pour in water. In every rarely used sink, shower, floor drain and sauna drain. Wavin states that in an unused fixture the water in the trap evaporates, and the speed depends on the air temperature and humidity.
  2. Clean the shower drain and the trap. A trap should be removable or have a cleaning opening. A shower and bath trap is hard to clean without taking off the cover plate, so Geberit advises pulling hair out with long tweezers.
  3. Check the joints. Under the sink and behind the toilet, look for damp spots and loose joints.
  4. Listen. If, when you flush the toilet or empty the bath, another fixture gurgles, the fault is in the venting.
  5. Check the septic tank. Remember when it was last pumped out, and look in the yard inspection chamber to see whether the water stands higher than usual.

Drain venting

LBN 221-15 states that drains are vented through a riser whose top is at least 0.5 m above a pitched roof (0.3 m above a flat roof not in use). A vent riser must not be connected to room ventilation or a flue.

In buildings of up to five storeys, a drain air admittance (vacuum) valve can be fitted at the end of an unvented riser, if the building has at least one vented riser; the valve must be above the edge of the highest fixture. The UK Building Regulations advise leaving the valve accessible and removable so that it can be cleaned, and not fitting it outside the building or in a dusty space.

LBN 221-15, clauses 249, 251 and 280; Approved Document H (2015), clause 1.33. Checked 13.11.2026.

Septic tank and holding tank

Cabinet Regulation No. 384 requires a holding tank to be airtight and emptied often enough that nothing leaks out and no smell spreads. In Ādaži Municipality a septic tank and a holding tank must be emptied at least once a year, pumping out at least 70% of the volume; in Ropaži Municipality also at least once a year. If the house will soon be connected to the network, see connecting to the central sewer.

Cabinet Regulation No. 384, sub-clause 4.5; binding regulations No. 3/2025 of Ādaži Municipality; binding regulations No. 45/22 of Ropaži Municipality. Checked 13.11.2026.

What not to do

  • Do not plug the end of the vent riser on the roof to keep the smell out of the yard: then water will start to be pulled out of the traps.
  • Do not connect drain venting to ventilation or a flue: LBN 221-15 forbids it.
  • Do not build an inspection chamber inside the house, for example in the basement: LBN 221-15 forbids inspection chambers on domestic drains inside a building.
  • Do not mask the smell with air fresheners if it keeps coming back: the cause remains.

Frequently asked questions

Why does it smell only sometimes?

If the smell appears when a lot of water is let out elsewhere, the water is pulled out of the trap, and the fault is in the venting. If the smell appears after an absence, the water in the trap has evaporated.

In my summer house the drains smell in spring. What should I do?

If the traps were emptied for winter or the water evaporated, there is no water seal. Pour water into every fixture. After a long period of standing, CIPHE advises flushing the water supply: first the cold, then the hot water, from the tap nearest the incoming supply to the farthest.

How can I tell whether the air admittance valve is to blame?

If traps gurgle and the smell appears when a lot of water enters the drains, the valve may be blocked or seized. It must be accessible and removable so that it can be cleaned.

Can the smell come from the septic tank if it is in the yard?

It can, if the septic tank or holding tank is full or the outlet is blocked, and then the smell is usually stronger in the yard. Cabinet Regulation No. 384 requires the tank to be emptied often enough that no smell spreads.
