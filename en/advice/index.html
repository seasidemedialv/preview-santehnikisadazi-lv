Rules and connections

If a new sewer network has been built by your house, read connecting to the central sewer: what the binding regulations of Ādaži and Ropaži municipalities say, who the water utilities are and what co-financing is available. In Ropaži Municipality it is 50% of the connection costs, while in Ādaži Municipality the co-financing was cancelled in 2022.

Frequently asked questions

Does the advice also work for a flat?

In part. The articles are about a house with its own incoming supply, hydrophore or septic tank. In a flat, the risers and the building's incoming supply are usually the building manager's responsibility, so contact them before any work.

Can I request a plumber after reading an article?

Yes. Fill in the form and write in the extra field what you have already checked. It helps you talk through the job and the price faster on the call.
