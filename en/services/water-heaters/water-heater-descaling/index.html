What water heater cleaning includes

  • Disconnecting the water heater from the power on all poles and closing the water supply.
  • Draining by opening the hot tap so that no vacuum forms in the tank.
  • Opening the bottom flange and removing the scale from the tank and from the heating element.
  • Checking the magnesium anode and the flange gasket.
  • Closing the flange, filling the tank and checking that the connection does not leak.

A new anode or a new gasket is not included in the price. If the cleaning shows that the heating element is faulty, that is already a repair; for more see the page water heater repair.

How often to clean a water heater

Recommendations differ by source, so it is best to know them all. The Centre for Disease Prevention and Control (SPKC) advises cleaning and disinfecting water heaters at least once a year. For water with many minerals, the maker Dražice advises having a specialist remove the scale after 1–2 years of use. The maker Ariston advises descaling the heating element about every 2 years, and more often in very hard water.

If the water comes from your own well or borehole, its composition differs from house to house, and how fast deposits build up in the water heater is best seen at the first cleaning. After that the cleaning interval can be adjusted to how much scale there was in the tank.

Water heater anode replacement

The magnesium anode is a metal rod in the tank that rusts in place of the tank and so protects it from corrosion. The maker Dražice advises checking the anode and, if needed, replacing it after 2 years of use, because its life depends on the water hardness and composition. Ariston advises replacing the anode every 2 years, but checking it every year in corrosive water or water with a lot of chlorides.

It is easiest to check the anode on the same visit, when the water heater is drained for cleaning. If only a thin core is left of it, it is replaced; the anode itself is bought for the specific water heater model.

Why an enamelled water heater is not descaled with acid

The maker Dražice states that an enamelled tank must not be cleaned with acids or with a scale-removal pump; the scale is removed mechanically through the flange opening. That is why descaling a water heater means opening the tank, not pouring a product in through the water pipe.

The amount of deposits depends on the water hardness, the temperature and the amount of hot water used. Dražice advises not heating the water hotter than needed, because a lower temperature reduces deposits. At the same time the hot water at the taps must be kept above +50 °C, more on that in the next section.

Smelly water from a water heater: what it means

If the hot water smells of rotten eggs but the cold water does not, the smell usually arises in the water heater itself, where warm water stands for a long time together with deposits and the anode. Then the tank is drained, cleaned and the anode is inspected.

SPKC states that Legionella bacteria multiply considerably in water from +20 °C to +45 °C, including in water heaters and standing water, and advises keeping the hot water above +50 °C at all taps. In its electronic water heaters the maker Ariston uses an anti-Legionella cycle that holds the water at 60 °C for one hour. If the water heater has stood unswitched for a long time, for example in a summer house, SPKC advises running the hot and cold water for a few minutes after the absence.

How the cleaning is done

  1. Disconnecting and draining The power is disconnected on all poles, the cold water supply is closed, the hot tap is opened and the water is drained through the safety valve or the drain tap.
  2. Opening The bottom cover is removed, the thermostat is disconnected and the flange with the heating element is taken out.
  3. Removing the scale The scale is removed from the bottom of the tank and cleaned off the heating element mechanically, without scratching the enamel.
  4. Checking The anode, the gasket and the heating element are inspected. If anything needs replacing, you are told before the tank is closed up.
  5. Filling The flange is closed, the water heater is filled until water runs from the hot tap without air, and only then is the power switched on.

Frequently asked questions

How much does water heater cleaning cost?

From €75 per water heater. The price includes draining, opening, scale removal, a gasket check and filling. A new anode or gasket costs extra.

Can a water heater be descaled with vinegar or citric acid?

For an enamelled tank, the maker Dražice forbids cleaning with acids or with a scale-removal pump. The scale is removed mechanically through the flange opening.

How do I know the anode needs replacing?

The anode can be seen only when the water heater is drained and opened. The makers advise checking it after 2 years of use, and every year in corrosive water. If only a thin core is left of the anode, it is replaced.

Why wait until the water heater is full after cleaning?

In an empty tank the heating element is not in the water and quickly breaks. That is why the water heater is filled until water runs from the hot tap without air, and only then is the power switched on.
