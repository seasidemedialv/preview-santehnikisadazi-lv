The price covers fixing and connecting it with a safety valve; removing the old water heater and the electrical connection are extra. If you are replacing an old water heater, describe its size and location in the form, and on the call you talk through the job and the price.

What the price from €70 includes

  • Fixing the wall-hung water heater to a wall that can bear the weight of a full tank.
  • Connection to the existing cold and hot water supply pipes.
  • A safety valve on the cold water inlet and a drain pipe.
  • Filling, bleeding through the hot tap and checking the connections.

Extra costs: removing the old water heater, the electrical connection, new supply pipes if the water heater is moved to another place, and materials. If a new water outlet to the water heater is needed, that is a separate job from €35 per outlet.

Water heater replacement: what is done with the old one

Before removal, the old water heater must be disconnected from the power on all poles and drained. The maker Dražice states that you first close the cold water supply and open the hot tap so that no vacuum forms in the tank, and only then let the water out. A water heater full of water is much heavier than an empty one, so it is not taken off the wall full.

After removal, the brackets, the wall and the supply pipes are inspected. On the new water heater the bracket spacing may differ, and the old safety valve is often clogged with scale, so it is often replaced together with the water heater.

How the installation is done

  1. Place and wall The water heater is hung on a load-bearing wall or a wall with suitable fixings, in a place that does not freeze and where the flange can be reached so that the tank can be cleaned later.
  2. Safety valve A safety valve is fitted on the cold water inlet with an opening pressure equal to the maximum permitted pressure of the water heater. There must be no shut-off valve between the water heater and the valve.
  3. Drain The valve drain pipe runs downwards, is open to the air and is not plugged, so that water that expands during heating can run out.
  4. Pressure check If the pressure in the water supply is higher than permitted, a pressure reducer is fitted before the valve. The maker Dražice specifies this if the pressure without any water use exceeds 5.5 bar.
  5. Filling The water heater is filled until water runs from the hot tap without air, and only then is the power switched on. An empty water heater is never switched on.

What water heater for a house

The volume is chosen by how many people wash one after another, whether you fill a bath and whether hot water is also needed in the kitchen and the sauna. If the house has a heating boiler or a heat pump, an indirect water heater (indirect cylinder) is often the better choice. For a summer house lived in only in summer, a smaller water heater that is easy to drain in autumn is suitable.

If the water comes from your own well, make sure the water heater has a flange opening for cleaning and an accessible anode. The maker Ariston advises checking the anode every year, rather than every two years, in corrosive water or water with many chlorides.

Connecting an indirect water heater to a heating boiler

An indirect water heater has a built-in heat exchanger, a coil of pipe heated by the water of the heating boiler or heat pump. The maker Dražice states that the heat exchanger is intended only for the heating circuit, while in a heat pump system the water in the upper part of the tank can be topped up with an additional electric heating element.

The coil is connected to the boiler circuit with its own pump or a three-way valve, while the drinking water side is the same as for an electric water heater: a safety valve, a drain and, if needed, a reducer. This work is not included in the price from €70, because it depends on the boiler room and the existing heating scheme. Installing a heat pump is a different trade.

Instantaneous water heater and a small water heater under the sink

An instantaneous heater heats water only while the tap is open and needs a high electrical power. A small water heater under the sink suits a remote kitchen or an outbuilding where the main water heater is too far away to run a hot water pipe. Before choosing, check whether the electrical supply of the house will cope with the load.

Hot water circulation in a house

If the water heater is far from the bathroom, the hot water has to run for a long time. A circulation loop with a small pump keeps the water in the pipes warm, and it reaches the tap faster. The Centre for Disease Prevention and Control (SPKC) advises keeping the hot water above +50 °C at all taps, because Legionella bacteria multiply in water from +20 °C to +45 °C. Circulation helps if long pipes would otherwise stand cool.

Frequently asked questions

Does the price from €70 include removing the old water heater?

No. The price covers fixing and connecting a new wall-hung water heater with a safety valve. Removing the old water heater and the electrical connection are extra.

Can the old safety valve be reused?

It can, if its opening pressure matches the maximum pressure of the new water heater and the valve works. In the maker Dražice's table this pressure is 6, 7 or 10 bar depending on the model, so the valve is compared with the data of the new water heater.

Why does the safety valve of a new water heater drip?

Water expands when heated, so water usually appears at the valve outlet, and this is normal. If it drips even when the water heater is not heating, the pressure in the water supply and the valve are checked.

Can a water heater be put in an unheated cellar or outbuilding?

It can, if the room does not freeze in winter or if the water heater is drained before the cold. The maker Dražice states that if there is a risk of frost, the water heater must be drained and disconnected from the power on all poles.
