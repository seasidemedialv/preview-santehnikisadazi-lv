What to do now

  1. Switch off the power Switch off the water heater's circuit breaker in the fuse board. If water is running onto a socket or wires, touch the fuse board only if you can do so safely, without standing in water.
  2. Close the water Close the tap on the cold water supply to the water heater. If there is none, close the main valve of the house or, in a house with a well, switch off the hydrophore.
  3. Describe what you see Whether the water comes from the valve outlet, from the bottom around the flange or from the tank itself, or whether the water heater trips the circuit breaker. This lets us get a rough idea of the fault already on the call.

Water heater not heating: the most common causes

  • The heating element is burnt out. The water heater is on, but the water stays cold.
  • The heating element leaks current to the casing. Then, when you switch the water heater on, the residual-current device or the circuit breaker trips.
  • The thermostat or the overheating protection is faulty. The water does not heat or, the other way round, gets too hot.
  • There is a lot of limescale on the heating element. The maker Dražice states that deposits depend on the water hardness, the temperature and the hot water use; a thick layer of scale slows heating and overheats the element.

If the water heater trips the residual-current device, do not switch it on again until the heating element has been checked. The electrical circuit outside the water heater is checked by an electrician.

Water heater leaks or drips

Dripping from the safety valve outlet while the water heater is heating is normal. The maker Dražice states that on a full heating cycle the water volume grows by about 3.5% of the tank volume, and this water runs out through the valve. If it drips even when the water heater is not heating and nobody is running water, usually either the pressure in the water supply is too high or the valve is faulty.

Water around the flange at the bottom usually means a worn gasket or loose screws. This is fixed by replacing the gasket after draining. If the tank itself leaks, the metal has rusted through, and such a water heater is not repaired in parts but replaced with a new one: see the page water heater installation.

Safety valve replacement

Every pressure water heater must have a safety valve on the cold water inlet, and its opening pressure must match the maximum pressure of the water heater. In the maker Dražice's table it is 6, 7 or 10 bar depending on the model, and Ariston states 7 bar. The water heater does not have to be removed for the replacement, but some of the water must be drained from it.

If the valve has started to drip without heating, the pressure is checked first. The maker Dražice states that if the water supply pressure exceeds 80% of the valve opening pressure, a pressure reducer must be installed before the valve. In a house with a hydrophore (a pump with a pressure tank) the pump cut-out pressure is checked as well.

How the repair is done

  1. Disconnecting The water heater is disconnected from the power on all poles and the cold water supply is closed.
  2. Draining The hot tap is opened so that no vacuum forms in the tank, and the water is drained through the safety valve in its test position or through the drain tap.
  3. Opening The bottom cover and the flange are removed, and the heating element, the thermostat and the anode are checked. If there is a lot of limescale, it is removed on the same visit.
  4. Replacement The faulty part is replaced with one suited to this model. The flange is closed with a new gasket if the old one is compressed.
  5. Filling and checking The water heater is filled before the power is switched on, and we check that the flange does not leak and that the water heats up.

When a repair pays off and when a new water heater is better

Replacing a heating element, thermostat or anode usually pays off if the tank is intact and the parts are available for the specific model. If the tank leaks, if several parts are faulty at once or if the spare parts are no longer made, compare the price of the repair with the price of a new water heater on the call. Installing a new wall-hung water heater costs from €70.

Frequently asked questions

My water heater trips the residual-current device. Can I use it?

No. Most often it means that the heating element leaks current to the casing. Leave the water heater switched off and describe it in the form so that the heating element can be checked.

Does the water heater have to be taken off the wall to replace the heating element?

Usually not. On a wall-hung water heater the heating element is at the bottom behind a cover, and draining the water heater is enough. It has to be removed if the flange cannot be reached or if the water heater has to be replaced.

Is it worth repairing an old water heater?

If the tank does not leak and parts are available for the model, replacing the heating element or thermostat usually pays off. If the tank itself leaks, it cannot be repaired and a new water heater is needed.
