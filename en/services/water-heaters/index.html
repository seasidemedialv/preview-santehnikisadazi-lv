What work on a water heater in a house involves

In a house, hot water most often comes from an electric pressure water heater: a tank with a heating element, a thermostat and a magnesium anode, connected to the cold water supply. In houses with a wood or pellet boiler or a heat pump there is often an indirect water heater, in which heating water warms the tap water through a built-in pipe coil (a heat exchanger).

The plumber's work is the water side: fixing and connecting the water heater, the safety valve, draining, opening and cleaning the tank, replacing the heating element, thermostat or anode. Building the electrical line to the water heater is a separate job.

If the house takes its water from its own well or borehole, the water heater is part of the system together with the hydrophore (a pump with a pressure tank). The pressure in the water heater then depends on the pump settings, and the water composition affects how quickly deposits build up in the tank. For more on the pump and pressure, see the page hydrophores and water pressure.

When you need a plumber and when checking yourself is enough

Not every drip means a fault. When heated, water expands, so water usually drips from the safety valve outlet, and the maker Dražice calls this normal. If the water heater is not heating and nobody is running water, but the valve still drips, either the pressure in the water supply is too high or the valve is faulty. Then an inspection is needed.

  • First check the fuse board: if the water heater's circuit breaker or residual-current device has switched off and trips again after being switched on, do not switch the water heater on any more and describe it in the form.
  • Water runs from the bottom of the tank or from the flange around the heating element: close the cold water supply to the water heater and switch off the power.
  • The hot water has become lukewarm or runs out faster than before: limescale on the heating element or a faulty thermostat is often to blame.
  • The hot water smells: the tank must be drained, opened and inspected.

Water heater installation and replacement

A new water heater is connected so that there is a safety valve on the cold water inlet and no shut-off valve between the water heater and the valve. The valve drain pipe runs downwards, is open to the air and is in a place that does not freeze. When an old water heater is replaced, it is first properly drained and removed, and then we check that the wall and the supply pipes suit the new one.

What to consider when choosing, how an indirect water heater is connected to the heating boiler and when hot water circulation is useful in a house is described on the page water heater installation.

Water heater repair

The three most common faults are: the heating element is burnt out or leaks current to the casing, the thermostat no longer switches heating off or on, or the safety valve drips even when the water heater is not heating. If the tank itself leaks, rather than a connection or the flange, a repair is usually no longer worth it. For the causes and the course of the repair, see the page water heater repair.

Water heater cleaning: price and when it is needed

Water heater cleaning costs from €75. The Centre for Disease Prevention and Control (SPKC) advises cleaning and disinfecting water heaters at least once a year. The makers also advise checking the magnesium anode and, if needed, replacing it after about two years of use. With very hard water, scale builds up on the heating element faster.

What the cleaning includes, how an enamelled tank is descaled and what to do if the hot water smells: see the page water heater cleaning.

Safety valve and pressure: what to know about every water heater

The opening pressure of the safety valve must match the maximum permitted pressure of the water heater. In the maker Dražice's table it is 0.6, 0.7 or 1 MPa (6, 7 or 10 bar) depending on the model, and Ariston states 0.7 MPa (7 bar). That is why the valve is chosen for the specific water heater, not by what is at hand.

If the pressure in the water supply is higher than permitted, a pressure reducer is fitted before the water heater. Dražice specifies a reducer if the pressure without any water use exceeds 5.5 bar. In a house with a hydrophore the pressure is set by the pump switch, so it is checked together with the water heater.

The maker Dražice advises testing the safety valve with its test button at least once a month and after every standstill longer than 5 days. This is a simple job that the owner can do.

What to write in the form

  • What is happening: not heating, leaking, dripping from the valve, smelling, a new one is needed or the old one must be replaced.
  • Water heater type and volume, if you know it: wall-mounted, floor-standing or indirect, the approximate age and make.
  • House type and water source: the central water supply or your own well with a hydrophore.
  • Where the water heater is: the bathroom, a utility room, the cellar, and whether the room is heated in winter.
  • Whether there is an electrical connection and a safety valve. You cannot attach a photo to the form, so describe in words what you see.

On the call you talk through the job and the price before anyone comes to you.

What most often goes wrong

  • A shut-off valve is fitted between the water heater and the safety valve. The makers forbid this, because with the valve closed the safety valve can no longer release pressure.
  • The drain pipe is plugged or led to a place where it freezes.
  • The water heater is drained without opening the hot tap. The maker Dražice states that you first close the cold water supply and open the hot tap so that no vacuum forms in the tank.
  • The water heater is left full in an unheated house. If there is a risk of frost, the maker advises draining it before the cold and disconnecting it from the power on all poles.
  • An enamelled tank is descaled with acid. The maker forbids this; the scale is removed mechanically through the flange opening.

Frequently asked questions

How much does water heater installation cost?

Installing or replacing a wall-hung water heater of up to about 100 l costs from €70. Removing the old water heater, the electrical connection and materials are extra. For a larger, floor-standing or indirect water heater, you talk through the price on the call after you have described the location and the connection.

How often should a water heater be cleaned?

SPKC advises cleaning and disinfecting water heaters at least once a year. For water with many minerals, the maker Dražice advises removing the scale after 1–2 years of use and checking the anode after 2 years.

Water drips from the water heater safety valve. Is that a fault?

While the water heater is heating, a small amount of dripping is normal, because the water expands. If it drips even when the water heater is not heating and nobody is running water, the cause is usually too high a pressure in the water supply or a faulty valve. Then the valve or the pressure must be checked.

What temperature should a water heater be set to?

SPKC advises keeping the hot water above +50 °C at all taps, because Legionella bacteria multiply in water from +20 °C to +45 °C. The maker Dražice advises 60 °C. Water that hot can scald, so Ariston advises a thermostatic mixing valve on the hot water outlet.
