The tap itself is usually a cheap job. The price is affected more by what surrounds it: the shut-off valves under the sink, the flexible hoses and access.

When you do not need a plumber

  • A short drip right after the mixer tap is closed is physically normal and not a defect (hansgrohe).
  • If the flow has become weak or splashes sideways, unscrew and clean the aerator at the end of the tap. The maker Neoperl states that limescale and other deposits build up in it over time, and advises cleaning or replacing it when limescale is visible or the flow has become uneven.
  • If the weak flow is at one tap only and the pressure is normal at the others, the fault is usually in that tap, not in the whole house.

Dripping mixer tap: cartridge replacement

The maker hansgrohe states that a mixer tap that keeps dripping even when closed usually has a damaged or limescale-clogged ceramic cartridge, and it can be replaced. Cartridges differ by maker and model, so the new one is chosen to match the old one. If you know the make and model of the tap, write it in the form.

Old two-handle taps often have a valve head with a washer instead of a cartridge. That can also be replaced if a suitable head is available. If the body is rusty or cracked, repair is no longer worth it.

Weak flow from a mixer tap

If too little water comes out, hansgrohe advises checking the supply pressure and the pump, if there is one, and cleaning the filters at the mixer tap inlets and in the shower hose seal. In a house with its own well or borehole, weak pressure at all taps usually means the hydrophore (a pump with a pressure tank) needs a look: hydrophores and water pressure. Why the pressure can drop is explained in the article low water pressure in a house.

Mixer tap installation and replacement

Replacing a mixer tap means shutting off the water, disconnecting and removing the old tap, fitting the new one and connecting it to the existing supply pipes, then checking the connections. If the old flexible hoses have hardened or rusted, they are replaced together with the tap; they cost extra. If there are no shut-off valves under the sink, it is worth fitting them now, so that next time the water does not have to be shut off for the whole house.

A kitchen tap with a pull-out spray, a basin tap, a bath tap and a shower thermostat are all fitted differently. LBN 221-15 specifies a wall-mounted mixer tap 200 mm above the rim of the washbasin and a bath tap 800 mm from the floor, tolerance ±20 mm. For fitting a sink together with a tap, read the page sink installation, and for a kitchen sink the page kitchen sink installation.

Mixer tap heights, tolerance ±20 mm: Cabinet Regulation No. 332, LBN 221-15, Annex 14 (likumi.lv), checked 13.11.2026.

Thermostatic mixer tap in a house

For mixer taps the maker hansgrohe states a recommended operating pressure of 1–5 bar and a maximum of 10 bar, and thermostats need equal hot and cold water pressure. In a house the hot water comes from the water heater and the cold from the mains or the hydrophore, so before fitting a thermostat it is worth checking both sides.

At the draw-off point the hot water must be 55–70 °C under LBN 221-15. The maker Ariston warns that water hotter than 50 °C can cause serious burns at once, especially to children and older people. That is why a thermostat in a shower is practical in a home with children. Shower rails and concealed systems are described on the page shower system installation.

What to write in the form

You cannot attach a photo to the form, so describe:

  • where the tap is: in the kitchen, at the basin, at the bath or in the shower;
  • what is happening: dripping, leaking under the sink, weak flow, stiff to turn;
  • whether the tap should be repaired or you have already bought a new one;
  • the make and model, if visible on the tap or known;
  • whether the water comes from the central network or from your own well or borehole with a hydrophore.

What goes wrong when replacing a mixer tap

  • The old, hardened flexible hoses are connected to the new tap, and after a while they start to leak.
  • A tap is bought whose connection or size does not fit the hole in the sink.
  • A thermostat is fitted in a house where the hot and cold water pressure differ, and the temperature jumps.
  • The connections are tightened too hard, and a plastic or brass part cracks.

Frequently asked questions

Can I buy a new mixer tap myself?

Yes. Before buying, check that the tap fits the sink or wall outlets and that flexible hoses are included. In the form, write which tap you have bought.

Do the flexible hoses have to be replaced together with the tap?

If they are old, hardened or rusty, yes. A new tap usually comes with its own hoses; if not, they are extra.

Do you repair old two-handle taps?

Yes, if a suitable head or washer can be found. If the body is rusty or cracked, it is better to fit a new tap.

Why does the tap drip again after the cartridge replacement?

The cartridge may not be the right one for that model, or there may be limescale or damage in the body seat. Then on the call you talk through whether repair is still worth it.
