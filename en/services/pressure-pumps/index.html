If a house in Ādaži or the Riga region takes its water from its own well or borehole, the pressure at the taps is set by the pump, the pressure tank and the pressure switch. We install and replace hydrophores, check and adjust the tank pre-charge pressure and look for why the pressure is weak. The price depends on the job; a call-out with an inspection costs from €40.

How a hydrophore works in a house

A hydrophore set usually consists of a pump, a diaphragm pressure tank, a pressure switch and fittings: a non-return valve, hoses and a pressure gauge. When you open a tap, the pressure drops, the switch starts the pump and, when the pressure reaches the set upper limit, the pump stops. The tank holds a little water under pressure so that the pump does not have to start after every glass of water.

In Ādaži Municipality there are villages where there is no central water supply at all or only in a small part. In Divezeri residents use deep borehole wells and local sewage systems, while in Eimuri the central water supply covers only a small part of Ādaži parish. In such houses the hydrophore is the whole water supply, and when it stops there is no water at any tap.

The Latvian Building Code LBN 221-15 provides that pumps may work in intermittent mode together with hydropneumatic pressure tanks, and that compact pump units with a tank of up to 50 l may be installed right at the point of use.

LBN 221-15 “Internal water supply and sewerage of buildings” (Cabinet Regulation No. 332), clauses 153.2, 159 and 170. Checked 13.11.2026.

When you need a plumber and when you do not

If the pressure is weak at only one tap or shower, the cause is often the aerator or a filter at the tap itself. The maker hansgrohe advises first cleaning the filters at the mixer tap inlets and in the shower hose seal, and Neoperl states that limescale builds up in aerators over time and reduces the flow. You can do this yourself.

You need a plumber if the pressure is weak throughout the house, if the pump starts and stops every few seconds, if it never stops, if water comes out of the tank air valve or if a hydrophore has to be installed from scratch. For the first checks in more detail, see the article weak water pressure in a house.

How much does it cost?

The price of hydrophore work depends on the system. The installation price depends on whether a new unit is fitted or an old one replaced, on the distance to the well or well point and the suction depth. It also depends on whether a non-return valve, filter, pressure switch and new connections are needed, and on the room (cellar, utility room, insulated chamber).

The price of checking and adjusting the pressure tank pre-charge depends on whether an air pressure correction is enough or the diaphragm has to be replaced, on the tank size and on whether the system has to be drained. The repair price is set by the fault, by whether the pump has to be taken to a workshop, by the price of spare parts and by whether the repair is worth it compared with a new pump.

Hydrophore installation

LBN 221-15 requires a non-return valve, shut-off fittings and a pressure gauge on the discharge line of each pump, and shut-off fittings and a pressure gauge on the suction line. The non-return valve at the end of the suction line in the well keeps water in the pipe so that the pump does not have to prime from scratch every time.

A surface pump has a limited suction lift. For example, the maker Grundfos states for its JP series pumps a maximum suction lift of 8 m (with losses, for 20 °C water) and a maximum system pressure of 6 bar. If the water level in the well is deeper, a submersible pump is needed. The hydrophore is placed in a room that does not freeze, because the maker states that if there is a risk of frost the pump and tank must be drained.

Hydrophore replacement

A hydrophore is replaced if the motor or the pump body is damaged, if spare parts are no longer available or if the old pump no longer gives the pressure needed. Sometimes it is enough to replace only the tank. After replacement the switch and the tank pre-charge are set again: the maker Grundfos states that when the switch settings are changed, the tank pre-charge must be readjusted too.

Hydrophore diaphragm and tank pre-charge

The air side of the tank holds the pre-charge. For JP pumps the maker Grundfos advises setting it 0.2 bar below the pump cut-in pressure, while other Grundfos documents give 90% of the cut-in pressure. The pre-charge is measured only when the tank is disconnected from the system and completely drained.

The makers Zilmet and Grundfos advise checking the pre-charge at least once a year. A small loss is normal, but a large drop may point to a worn diaphragm. If water comes out when you press the air valve, the diaphragm is damaged. How it is replaced is described on the page hydrophore repair.

Weak water pressure in a house

If the pressure is weak throughout the house, the causes are usually of three kinds: the pump switch is set low, a filter or non-return valve is clogged, or the pipes are too narrow. The maker Grundfos advises calculating the cut-in pressure as the pressure needed at the highest tap plus the height difference and the losses in the pipes, and setting the cut-out pressure 0.8–1.5 bar higher.

LBN 221-15 also states that pipe diameters are chosen so as to use the available pressure, because too small a diameter reduces the pressure at the taps. That is why on the upper floor or in a distant bathroom the pressure is often weaker than at the pump.

Booster pump and pressure reducer

In a house with a central water supply where the pressure at the inlet is too low, a booster pump with a tank is fitted. If the pressure is too high, a reducer is fitted. LBN 221-15 provides that in a domestic water supply the pressure at the lowest outlet must not exceed 6 bar, and the reducer is placed after the meter, the shut-off fitting or the pump, with pressure gauges before and after it.

A filter is fitted before the reducer, and the place must be protected from frost. The maker Resideo states that low pressure after a reducer is often caused by a clogged filter, valves that are not fully open or a reducer installed against the direction of flow.

What to write in the form

  • What is happening: no water, weak pressure, the pump starts often, does not switch off, leaks.
  • Water source: a well, a borehole or the central water supply.
  • Pump type, if you know it: a surface hydrophore or a submersible pump in the well, the tank volume, the approximate age.
  • Where the hydrophore is: in the cellar, a utility room or the well chamber, and whether it freezes there in winter.
  • What the pressure gauge shows when the pump starts and stops. You cannot send a photo in the form, so write the readings and observations in the text.

What most often goes wrong

  • The switch settings are changed but the tank pre-charge is left as it was, and the pump starts to cycle too often.
  • The pre-charge is measured while there is water in the tank, and the reading is wrong.
  • The hydrophore stands in an unheated place and is not drained in autumn. Frozen water damages the pump body.
  • The suction line has no non-return valve or it does not hold, and the pump loses its water after every stop.

Frequently asked questions

How much does hydrophore installation cost?

The price depends on whether a new unit is fitted or an old one replaced, on the distance to the well and the suction depth, and on the fittings needed and where the unit goes. Describe the system in the form, and on the call you talk through the job and the price.

What pressure should a hydrophore be set to?

It depends on the height of the house and the appliances. The maker Grundfos advises a cut-out pressure 0.8–1.5 bar higher than the cut-in pressure. For a Caleffi switch the factory setting is 1.4–2.8 bar. The tank pre-charge is then set slightly below the cut-in pressure.

How often should the hydrophore tank be checked?

The makers Zilmet and Grundfos advise checking the pre-charge at least once a year. It is measured when the tank is disconnected and empty.

Can a hydrophore be left in an unheated summer house?

Only drained. The maker Grundfos states that a pump not in use in winter must be drained, otherwise frozen water can damage its internal parts.

How quickly do you get in touch if there is no water?

After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes. If the pump runs but no water comes, switch it off so that it does not run without water.
