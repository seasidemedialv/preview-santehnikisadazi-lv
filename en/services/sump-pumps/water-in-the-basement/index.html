Water in the basement in Ādaži - what to do and how to prevent it

Water in a basement in Ādaži and the Riga region usually comes from one of three sources: a burst pipe, groundwater in spring or autumn, or sewage flowing back up through the floor drain.

First deal with the electricity and shut off the water if a pipe is leaking. The lasting fix is a pit with a drainage pump and a backflow valve, and its price depends on the pit and the outlet.

What to do now

  1. Electricity If the water is rising, switch off the electricity at the fuses or circuit breakers only if that is not dangerous. Do not go near a flooded consumer unit or cables, and keep away from damaged appliances and exposed wires, as they may be live.
  2. Incoming water supply If the water is clean and keeps running, close the main valve. If the water comes from your own well or borehole, switch off the hydrophore (a pump with a pressure tank).
  3. Drains If the water is dirty and smells, do not use the toilets, showers or washing machine, because every litre ends up in the basement.
  4. Belongings Lift or carry valuables and documents out of the basement.
  5. Request Describe in the form where the water comes from and how high it is. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes.

After a flood, do not use mains-powered electrical appliances until an electrician has confirmed that they are safe: all electrical equipment affected by water must be checked by a specialist.

If a pipe is leaking, that is a water leak job. If the water comes up through the floor drain, you first need drain cleaning.

Basement floods in spring: groundwater

LVĢMC (the Latvian Environment, Geology and Meteorology Centre) data show that groundwater in Latvia is usually highest in the second half of March and in April, when the snow stored over winter melts, and again in October and November after heavy rainfall. In 2022 the groundwater level rose by 0.04–0.52 m from the start of the year to the spring peak. If the basement floor is below this level, water will come in every year.

A project document of Ādaži Municipality lists Ādaži as a flood-risk area of national importance. In Carnikava on 2 March 2021 an ice jam on the Gauja raised the water by about a metre, and six houses flooded on the bank without a dyke. In Baltezers residents call the area around Baltkrastu iela prone to flooding, and in Bukulti around the Jugla canal the groundwater is shallower than 1.5 m.

Pumping water out of a basement with a permanent pump

The lasting solution for groundwater is a pit at the lowest point of the basement with a drainage pump switched on and off by a float. For a permanently installed pump with a float switch, the maker Grundfos requires a non-return valve on the discharge, and the pump must stand on a solid base so that the strainer does not clog. The outlet is led to a place where the water may be discharged and is protected from frost outdoors.

A pump does not stop water in the wall: it only stops it from building up. Waterproofing the basement walls and drainage around the foundations are a different job. On choosing and installing a pump: sump pumps.

Backflow valve installation

If sewage comes up through the basement floor drain, the fault may be in the external sewer. Latvian Building Code LBN 221-15 requires fixtures that are lower than the cover of the nearest inspection chamber to be connected with a separate outlet and a device that prevents the basement from flooding from the external sewer. A backflow valve closes when the water in the street network rises above the backflow level, which is usually the street level.

  • EN 13564 divides valves into types: 0 with one flap, 1 with an automatic flap and a manual shut-off, 2 with two automatic flaps, 3 with an electrically controlled flap and a reserve manual flap.
  • The maker KESSEL states that types 0, 1 and 2 are intended only for wastewater without faeces; for a toilet in the basement, type 3 or pumping is suitable.
  • If the sewer is higher than the basement outlet point, the wastewater must be pumped above the backflow level with a pumping station.
  • KESSEL advises the user to check the valve once a month and a specialist to service it at least every six months.

How much does a lasting solution cost?

The price of installing a drainage or basement pump depends on whether the pit (well ring) is ready, the length of the outlet line to the discharge point, the float switch and electrical connection, the non-return valve and frost protection for the outlet. A plumber's call-out with inspection costs from €40, and stopping a leak or pipe seepage in an accessible place costs from €40. That is the price of labour; the pump and materials are extra.

Frequently asked questions

Do you pump out a flooded basement?

This page is about fixing the cause: the leak, the pump in the pit and the backflow valve. If there is a lot of water in the basement that has to be taken away, that is done with a sewage tanker.

Why does water come into the basement every spring?

The groundwater level is usually highest in the second half of March and in April. If the floor is below this level, the water will come in again, and it can be collected in a pit with a drainage pump.

Sewage is coming back up through the floor drain. What should I do?

Do not use any fixtures, check that the yard inspection chamber is not full, and request drain cleaning. After that consider a backflow valve, as LBN 221-15 requires for fixtures below the level of the inspection chamber cover.
