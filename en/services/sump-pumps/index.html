Sump pumps in Ādaži - basement and sewage pumps

Drainage and basement pumps in Ādaži and the Riga region are installed where water does not run away by itself: in a basement that takes in groundwater in spring, or at a toilet and shower that sit lower than the sewer line.

A drainage pump handles cleaner water with small particles, while a sewage pump is made for wastewater from a toilet. The price depends on the pit, the outlet and the electrical connection; after you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes.

A drainage pump and a sewage pump are not the same thing

A drainage (dirty water) pump stands in a pit in the basement or in a well and pumps out groundwater, leak water or grey water. The maker of the Grundfos Unilift KP allows particles up to 10 mm, so it is not meant for faeces and paper.

A sewage pump collects wastewater from a toilet, shower and sink and pushes it to the sewer line. Small pump units behind a toilet, such as the Grundfos Sololift2, comply with EN 12050-3 and are intended for domestic use and a limited number of users. For larger volumes a pump chamber is used.

When a pump is needed and when you can do without one

  • Water appears in the basement every spring or autumn: you need a pit with a drainage pump and a float switch.
  • There is a shower or toilet in the basement or sauna lower than the cover of the nearest inspection chamber: LBN 221-15 requires these fixtures to be connected with a separate outlet and a device against flooding from the external sewer.
  • If wastewater can be drained by gravity, a pump is not needed: the Sololift2 maker itself advises gravity drainage if the fall can be made.
  • If water appeared in the basement once after a burst pipe, the leak must be fixed first, not a pump installed.

How much does drainage pump installation cost?

There is no single price for installing a drainage or basement pump. The price depends on whether the pit (well ring) is ready, the length of the outlet line to the discharge point, the float switch and electrical connection, the non-return valve and protecting the outlet from frost. The price of installing a sewage pump depends on whether it is a compact pump by the toilet (Sololift type) or a buried pump chamber. It also depends on the length and lift of the discharge pipe, the electrical connection and the alarm, and on any digging for an outdoor station.

A plumber's call-out with inspection costs from €40, the hourly rate is from €25/h. That is the price of labour; the pump and materials are extra. On the call you talk through the job and the price. All prices are on the page prices.

Drainage pump installation in a basement

The pump needs a pit at the lowest point of the basement, where the water gathers by itself. For the Unilift KP with a float switch, the maker Grundfos gives minimum pit dimensions of about 356 and 407 mm, but the pit is also sized by how fast the water comes in and how much the pump can remove. The pump is put on a solid base, and the inlet strainer must be fully under water during operation so that silt does not block it.

The float switch turns the pump on and off by water level. If the pump is installed permanently, the maker requires a non-return valve in the discharge pipe, and also advises a coupling and a gate valve so that the pump can be taken out. The outlet is led to a place where the water may be discharged and is protected from frost outdoors: ice takes up about 9% more volume than water, and a full pipe can burst.

Sewage pump installation: a toilet in the basement

LBN 221-15 (the Latvian Building Code) allows small factory-made pump units with a sealed tank to be placed behind a toilet bowl if ventilation of the unit is provided. A toilet, shower cabin, bidet and sink may be connected to the Sololift2 WC-1 and WC-3; the house must have another toilet above the backflow level, and the pipe between the toilet and the unit must not be longer than 150 mm.

The maker states that the discharge pipe must start vertically and horizontal pipes must fall at least 1% towards the riser. The unit must be fixed to the floor in a frost-free room. If it ever starts to malfunction, read about the causes on the page sewage pump repair.

A backflow valve against wastewater in the basement

If the water in the street network or the yard line rises above the backflow level, wastewater can come out through the basement floor drain or shower. A backflow valve closes at that moment. If the sewer is higher than the basement outlet point, the wastewater must be pumped above this level with a pumping station. On backflow valve types and checks: water in the basement.

Why this matters here

LVĢMC (the Latvian Environment, Geology and Meteorology Centre) data show that groundwater in Latvia is usually highest in the second half of March and in April, when the snow melts, and again in October and November after heavy rainfall. A project document of the municipality lists Ādaži as a flood-risk area of national importance. In Carnikava the municipality maintains polders and land drainage so that the low-lying places by the Gauja and the sea do not flood, and in Bukulti around the Jugla canal the groundwater is shallower than 1.5 m.

What to write in the request

  • a drainage or sewage pump, new or a replacement;
  • whether the basement already has a pit or well ring;
  • where the water could be discharged and how far that place is;
  • whether there is a socket or an electrical supply nearby;
  • if a toilet or shower is in the basement: how far and how high the nearest sewer line is;
  • the water source: the central water supply, or your own well or borehole with a hydrophore (a pump with a pressure tank).

Photos cannot be sent through the form, so describe the basement dimensions and the equipment in text.

What often goes wrong

  • There is no non-return valve on the outlet: after each stop the water from the pipe flows back into the pit, and the pump starts too often.
  • The float has no room in the pit, it sticks, and the pump does not switch on or off.
  • The pump stands on silt, and the strainer clogs.
  • The outlet outdoors is not protected from frost, and in winter it freezes.
  • Drainage water is led into the sewer without approval. In Ādaži Municipality rainwater may be led into the sewer only with the operator's permission, so ask the operator first about drainage water.

Frequently asked questions

Is a drainage pump suitable for toilet wastewater?

No. A drainage pump is meant for water with small particles, for example the Grundfos Unilift KP up to 10 mm. A toilet needs a sewage pump unit.

Can I install a toilet in the basement if the sewer is higher?

You can, with a compact sewage pump unit behind the toilet, if ventilation is provided for it and the house has another toilet above the backflow level. If the fall can be made, gravity drainage is better.

Why does a drainage pump need a non-return valve?

Without it, the water from the outlet pipe flows back into the pit after each stop. For a permanently installed pump with a float switch, Grundfos makes a non-return valve on the discharge mandatory.

Where should the water from a basement pump be discharged?

To a place where it may be discharged, and with an outlet that does not freeze in winter. Before you lead water into the sewer, find out the operator's requirements.
