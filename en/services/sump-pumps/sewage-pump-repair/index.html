How this job differs from drainage pump repair

A sewage pump handles wastewater from a toilet, so it sits in a closed tank with ventilation and a non-return valve, and opening it calls for attention to hygiene. A drainage pump stands in an open pit in cleaner water and is simpler to repair; see the page drainage pump repair.

This page is about pumps inside the house: compact units behind a toilet and pumps in a basement. Household pump units such as the Grundfos Sololift2 WC-1 and WC-3 are intended for domestic use and a limited number of users.

Signs and the most common causes

Sign Most common cause
The pump hums but does not pump out wipes, cotton wool or hair caught on the impeller
The pump will not switch off or switches on by itself a dirty or faulty level switch, a non-return valve that leaks or lets water back
A smell by the toilet or the unit a faulty lid seal or ventilation
Water rises in the toilet, the unit switches off overheating from running too long or from hot water
The pump does not work after winter the unit was in an unheated room and frost damaged it

What the unit can and cannot take

The maker of the Sololift2 states that only toilet paper, faeces and wastewater may enter the unit. Wet wipes, hygiene products, cotton wool, food, hair and chemicals damage it. The water temperature may be up to 50 °C, and the duty cycle is 30 seconds on and 30 seconds off, so running hot water for a long time from a bath or washing machine overloads the unit.

The maker gives a maximum pump head of 8 m, but the system head must not exceed 6 m. If the discharge pipe is long and climbs high, the unit works at its limit, and a repair will not help for long. These figures apply to this particular model; for another maker they may differ.

How the repair works

  1. Fixtures out of use Before the work, the toilet, shower and sink connected to the pump are not used, so that no new wastewater flows into the tank.
  2. Power off The unit is disconnected from the mains before the lid is opened.
  3. Tank and impeller The tank is opened, wipes and anything else that has caught are removed, and we check that the impeller turns freely.
  4. Switch, non-return valve, seals The level switch, the discharge non-return valve and the lid seal are checked; worn parts are replaced.
  5. Test The toilet is flushed several times and water from the other fixtures is run, to see whether the pump switches on, pumps out and switches off.

Sewage pump replacement

If the motor has burnt out, the body has cracked or parts are no longer available for the model, the unit is replaced. At replacement it is worth checking requirements that may not have been followed before. The maker Grundfos states that the discharge pipe must start vertically and horizontal pipes must fall at least 1% towards the riser. The pipe between the toilet and the unit must be no longer than 150 mm, and the house must have another toilet above the backflow level. LBN 221-15 (the Latvian Building Code) requires ventilation of the unit.

The unit must be in a frost-free room and fixed to the floor, and the maker advises checking its operation at least yearly. If it can be done, the best solution is gravity drainage without a pump: the Sololift2 maker itself advises gravity drainage if the fall can be made. Installing a new unit is described on the page sump pumps.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use the toilet connected to the pump while it is faulty?

No. If the pump does not pump out, the wastewater stays in the tank and may come out through the toilet or the shower drain. Use another toilet in the house.

Why does a sewage pump smell?

Often the cause is the lid seal or ventilation. LBN 221-15 requires ventilation for such units. A smell can also come from a dried-out trap in a fixture that is not used for a long time.

Can I connect a washing machine to a pump behind the toilet?

The maker of the Sololift2 WC-1 and WC-3 allows a toilet, shower cabin, bidet and sink to be connected. For connecting other appliances, see the instructions for your model.
