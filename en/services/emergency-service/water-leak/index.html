Water leak in Ādaži and Riga Region - find and fix

A water leak in Ādaži and the Riga Region is either visible, when a pipe drips or has burst, or hidden, when water runs inside a wall, under the floor or in the ground between the well and the house.

A visible leak is stopped first with the main stopcock; a hidden one has to be found first. Fixing a leak in an accessible spot costs from €40; labour only, materials are extra.

Leaking pipe: a visible leak

If water leaks from a joint, a tap supply line or a burst pipe, close the main stopcock and, if you have your own well, switch off the hydrophore (a pump with a pressure tank). Then no more water comes, and the plumber only has to replace a joint or a seal, or cut out the damaged section and fit a new one. In an accessible spot this is a short job.

It is harder when one old metal pipe leaks in several places. In a galvanised steel pipe, once the zinc has worn away, the steel rusts from the inside, and a new leak appears next to the repaired one. Then it is cheaper to replace the section: pipe replacement in a house.

Signs of a hidden leak

Water running inside a wall or floor shows itself indirectly. On a wall these are bubbling or peeling paint or wallpaper, yellow or brown stains, mould, a musty smell and a quiet sound of running water when nobody is using any.

In an underground pipe from the well or meter to the house the signs are different: damp patches, a sunken lawn or paving, unusually green grass in dry weather and changes in pressure. Not every outdoor leak is visible above ground.

A house with its own well has one more sign: the hydrophore starts by itself although no tap is open. This means the pressure in the system is dropping. The cause may be a leak, but also a non-return valve that lets water flow back to the well. An inspection tells them apart: hydrophores and water pressure.

How to check whether there is a leak at all

  1. Close all taps Also switch off the washing machine, the dishwasher and the cistern filling, if they can be shut off.
  2. Watch the meter or the pressure gauge With a central water supply, see whether the meter is turning. With your own well, watch the hydrophore pressure gauge: if the pressure drops and the pump starts, water is going somewhere.
  3. Check with a pressure gauge Advice from the British water industry: close all taps and connect a pressure gauge to the outdoor tap. If the pressure drops within a few hours, a leak is possible.
  4. Shut off section by section If the house has a manifold or separate valves, close them one at a time. The section where the pressure stops dropping when it is shut off is the one to search.

This narrows down the place, but the damage itself can only be repaired once the pipe is reached. How far a wall or floor has to be opened is talked through before the work.

A pipe burst after frost

When water freezes it expands by about 9%, so a full pipe can burst. The crack usually starts to leak when the pipe thaws. A plastic PEX pipe usually also survives freezing, but repeated freezing weakens it, and a PEX pipe cast in concrete without a protective sleeve can be punctured when it freezes.

For an outdoor water pipe from the well to the house, the Latvian Building Code LBN 222-15 requires a burial depth at least 0.5 m greater than the depth at which the ground can reach 0 °C. If the old pipe was laid shallower, it bursts again and repairing one spot is not enough. Winter jobs: frozen pipes.

A leak protection system after an incident

After a big leak many people think of a leak sensor that closes a valve on the incoming supply when there is water on the floor. Whether and where it can be connected to your incoming supply depends on whether there is room for a valve and a power supply by it. If this interests you, write it in the form together with a description of the leak.

Frequently asked questions

Can a leak in a wall be found without opening the wall?

Often the place can be narrowed down by closing sections and watching the pressure, and the signs on the wall show the direction. To repair it, though, the pipe still has to be reached, so a wall or floor is usually opened only above the damage.

Who pays if it leaks before the meter?

The part of the network before the ownership boundary is maintained by the water utility, so report such a leak to it: in Ādaži Municipality SIA “Ādažu ūdens”, in Garkalne and Stopiņi parish SIA “Garkalnes inženiertīkli”, on the Riga side SIA “Rīgas ūdens”. Beyond the boundary the pipe is yours.

Should the electrics be checked after a leak?

If the water reached sockets, appliances or the fuse board, yes. Do not use electrical appliances that water has reached until an electrician has checked them.

Can I keep using water if it leaks only a little?

If it leaks in an accessible spot and the water does not get into a wall or floor, only that section can be shut off. If water runs into the structure, it is better to close the main stopcock, because the damp in a wall or floor spreads further than you can see.
