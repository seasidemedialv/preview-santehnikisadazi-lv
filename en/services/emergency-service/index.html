What to do right away, before the plumber is with you

  1. Close the main stopcock It is usually where the water enters the house: in the basement, a utility room or under the kitchen sink. The valve closes when turned clockwise. If the house is on the central water supply, there is also a shut-off valve at the meter.
  2. Switch off the hydrophore If the water comes from your own well or borehole, the pump keeps pushing water into the system as long as it has power. Switch off the pump switch or its breaker at the fuse board, and only then close the valve after the pump.
  3. Switch off the water heater If the leak is on the hot water side or the water heater itself is leaking, cut its electricity. If the water heater has to be drained, first close the cold water supply and open a hot tap, so that no vacuum forms in the tank.
  4. Protect the electrics If water is running onto sockets or the fuse board, switch off the power at the fuse board only if you can do it safely without standing in water. Unplug damaged appliances, and keep away from exposed wires.
  5. Lift your things Lift documents, electronics, rugs and boxes off the floor or carry them out. Collect the water with rags or a bucket so that it does not soak into the floor and walls.
  6. Fill in the form Describe what is happening, where it leaks and what you have already done. If you cannot find the valve, write that too.

When it is not yet an emergency

Not every dripping tap needs a night call-out. If a mixer tap drips only right after it is closed, that is physically normal. If it drips all the time, the fault is usually a worn or limescaled cartridge, and it can be replaced as a planned job: mixer tap repair and replacement.

Water usually also drips from a water heater safety valve while it is heating, because water expands when heated. That is not a fault. If the valve also drips when the water heater is not heating and nobody is using water, the cause is too high a mains pressure or the valve itself; this has to be fixed, but usually not at night.

If there is no water in the whole street or it leaks before the meter, the fault is in the water utility's network. In Ādaži Municipality that is SIA “Ādažu ūdens”, in Garkalne and Stopiņi parish of Ropaži Municipality SIA “Garkalnes inženiertīkli”, on the Riga side SIA “Rīgas ūdens”. The boundary between the utility's network and yours is set by the Law on Water Management Services.

A frozen pipe in winter

When water freezes it expands by about 9%, so a pipe full of water or a pump body can burst. The crack often shows only when the pipe thaws, so after thawing check whether anything starts to leak.

A pipe is thawed slowly: close the supply valve, open a tap so that the melting water can run out, and heat with hot water bottles or a warmed cloth, starting from the tap end. Never use an open flame or a blowtorch. In the ground under driveways and cleared paths the frost goes deeper, because there is no snow cover. More: frozen pipes.

What to write in the form

  • what is happening: leaking, burst, backing up, frozen, no water;
  • where: room, wall, floor, basement, yard;
  • the water source: central water supply or your own well, borehole with a hydrophore;
  • whether the valve is already closed and the pump switched off;
  • the type of home: house, summer house, flat;
  • whether the water has reached the electrical installation.

You cannot attach a photo to the form, so describe it in words: how hard it leaks, what the pipe is (metal or plastic) and whether the damaged spot is visible.

What often goes wrong in an emergency

  • The hydrophore stays on, and the pump keeps pushing water into the burst pipe until it is switched off.
  • A frozen pipe is heated with a flame: plastic melts, and the joints of a metal pipe suffer.
  • To stop a safety valve dripping, its outlet is plugged. The outlet must stay open, pointing down and in a place where it does not freeze.
  • Electrical appliances that water has reached are used before an electrician has checked them.
  • People wait for the leak to “stop by itself”, and meanwhile the water soaks into the floor and walls.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work at night and at weekends?

Yes, we also work at night and at weekends. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes.

How quickly will the plumber be there?

We do not promise an arrival time in advance. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes, and on the call we tell you when the master plumber can be with you and what to do yourself until then.

What if I cannot find the main stopcock?

Look where the water enters the house: in the basement, a utility room, by the hydrophore or under the kitchen sink, and with a central water supply also at the meter. If the water comes from your own well, switch off the pump. That stops the pressure even if you do not find the valve.

Does a call-out cost more at night?

The published prices are “from” prices. What changes the price in your particular case, including night or weekend time, is talked through on the call before the work.

Should I switch off the electricity if water is running on the floor?

If the water reaches sockets, appliances or the fuse board, yes, but only if you can do it safely without standing in water. After that, do not use electrical appliances that water has reached until an electrician has checked them.

Do you also repair the water pipe in the yard?

Yes, if it is your network, for example the pipe from the well to the house. If the damage is before the meter in the central network, it has to be reported to the water utility.
