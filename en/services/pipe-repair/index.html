We repair leaks and bursts, replace stopcocks that no longer close and install new sections of water supply up to the fixtures. Fixing a leak in an accessible spot costs from €40, pipe replacement or installation from €10/m; labour only, materials are extra.

What pipes the houses around Ādaži have

New houses stand here next to former summer houses that are now lived in all year round. Carnikava became a summer-house resort for Riga residents in the early 20th century, and since independence it has been one of the faster-growing villages near Riga. So on one street you can find both old metal pipes and a new plastic water supply, often both in one house.

Older houses may have galvanised steel pipes. When the zinc coating wears away, the steel rusts, deposits form inside, the pipe narrows, the pressure drops and the water turns brownish. New houses usually have PEX, multilayer or PP-R pipes. For PE-Xa pipes the maker Uponor states an expected service life of more than 50 years at 70 °C and 10 bar.

When a repair is enough and when the pipe has to be replaced

  • Repair: one joint or seal leaks, a short section has burst after frost, a tap supply line is damaged.
  • Section replacement: an old metal pipe leaks for the second time in the same line, or it can no longer be tightened without damaging the thread.
  • Whole-house replacement: old galvanised pipes, low pressure at all taps and brownish water that does not clear when you run the water.

When a lot of pipe has to be replaced, see pipe replacement in a house, which describes how such a job is planned.

Pipe installation

A new water supply cannot simply be run by the shortest route. The Latvian Building Code LBN 221-15 requires the diameters of the internal water supply to be chosen so that the pressure at the incoming supply is used as far as possible: a diameter that is too small lowers the pressure at the taps. Horizontal pipes need a fall of at least 2 ‰ towards the draining point, and rooms with cold water pipes must be warmer than 2 °C, otherwise the pipes must be protected from frost.

Plastic lengthens when it warms up. A 10 m section of PE-Xa pipe becomes longer by about 70 mm when it warms by 50 °C. For an ordinary PP-R pipe the maker aquatherm advises compensation every 10 m of straight section, and with a glass-fibre layer only above 40 m. A PEX pipe is not fitted in direct sunlight, and the bend radius must not be smaller than 8 outer diameters (5 with a bending support).

If the whole water supply of the house has to be built from the incoming supply, see plumbing installation in a house.

Stopcock replacement

The main valve has to close when something leaks. If the old valve is seized or no longer shuts off the water, it is no use in an emergency. To replace it, the water has to be stopped before the valve: with a central water supply at the meter assembly, with your own well by switching off the pump and draining the section. It is also worth checking the filter and the pressure reducer after the valve, if there are any.

Pipe pressure test

After a larger repair, and before pipes are covered in a wall or floor, the water supply is pressure tested. The standard EN 806 advises a test pressure 1.1 times the maximum design pressure. The maker KAN-therm advises three steps: a preliminary test at 1–4 bar to inspect the joints, then 30 minutes at the test pressure and another 30 minutes at half that pressure with no drop. The pressure gauge is connected at the lowest point of the system.

Water manifold installation

A manifold splits the water from one incoming supply into separate lines to each fixture or room. Each line can be shut off separately, so if one bathroom leaks, the rest of the house keeps its water, and the leak is easier to find. The manifold is placed in an accessible, warm spot where it can be reached without opening a wall.

Pipes sweat or rumble

If cold water pipes are wet in summer, it is usually condensation, not a leak. The maker Uponor advises insulated pipes where there is a risk of condensation or freezing. If pipes rumble or hiss, one cause may be too high a pressure: LBN 221-15 states that at the lowest water outlet it must not exceed 6 bar. A pressure reducer keeps the pressure even and reduces flow noise. Causes of weak pressure: weak water pressure in a house.

What to write in the form

  • what is happening: leaking, burst, low pressure, needs replacing or installing;
  • where the pipe is: exposed, in a wall, under the floor, in the basement, in the ground;
  • the material, if you know it: metal, plastic, copper;
  • the water source: central water supply or your own well with a hydrophore (a pump with a pressure tank);
  • how long the section is or how many fixtures have to be connected.

You cannot attach a photo to the form, so describe it in words. The more specific the description, the more specific the talk about the price.

What often goes wrong in pipe work

  • Tape is stuck on, or paint applied directly to, a PEX pipe. Plasticisers worsen the pipe's long-term properties.
  • A long PP-R section is fixed tightly without compensation, and the hot water line bows or pulls at the joints.
  • Pipes in an unheated basement or in the ground are insulated with gaps at bends, valves and joints, and the frost finds exactly these spots.
  • After a repair the pipes are covered without a pressure test.

Frequently asked questions

Can an old metal pipe be connected to a plastic one?

Yes, with an adapter fitting. If the metal pipe itself is already rusting from the inside, a new leak often appears next to the joint, so on the call it is worth deciding whether to replace the whole section.

How much does a metre of new pipe cost?

Pipe replacement or installation costs from €10/m: fitting the pipe along a wall or floor with fixings. Chasing, restoring the finish and materials are extra.

Can pipes be put inside a wall?

They can, but then a chase and restoring the finish are needed, which is a separate job. It is better to keep joints in a wall to a minimum, and the water supply is pressure tested before it is closed up.

Why do pipes burst in winter specifically in the basement?

Rooms with cold water pipes must be warmer than 2 °C. In an unheated basement or summer house this is not so, and the pipes have to be insulated or drained for the winter: plumbing winterising.

Do you also repair the pipe from the well to the house?

Yes, it is part of your network. If digging is needed, you talk through the digging scope and price separately, because they are not in the per-metre price.
