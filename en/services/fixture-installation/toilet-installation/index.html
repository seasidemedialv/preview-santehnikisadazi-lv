A toilet is replaced on the existing drain outlet, or installed in a new place if the outlet is already there. In the form, write what toilet you have now and which one you plan.

What is different about a toilet

A toilet is the heaviest fixture in a bathroom, and it has the thickest drain connection. LBN 221-15 requires a drain pipe of at least 100 mm for a toilet, while 32 mm is enough for a hand basin and 40 mm for a shower. The water supply to the cistern can be thin, at least 8 mm.

So in a toilet replacement the main question is not the toilet itself, but whether the outlet of the new toilet matches the old 100 mm outlet. The outlet of a toilet can point at the wall or at the floor, and the distance from the wall differs. If they do not match, a different collar or a bend helps, but sometimes the outlet has to be rebuilt.

Latvian Building Code LBN 221-15 “Internal Water Supply and Sewerage of Buildings” (Cabinet Regulation No. 332 of 30.06.2015), Annex 3: minimum drain and supply diameters. Checked 13.11.2026.

The frame must be installed while the wall is not yet clad: first the frame is connected to the water and the drain, then the wall is clad and tiled, and only then is the toilet hung. The maker Geberit states that its Duofix frames are tested for a load of up to 400 kg, so the safety of a wall-hung toilet depends more on how the frame is fixed to the floor and the wall than on the frame itself.

How a toilet is replaced

  1. Shutting off the water The valve at the toilet or the main stopcock is closed, the water is flushed out of the cistern and the water left in the bowl is scooped out.
  2. Removing the old toilet The fixings are unscrewed, the supply is disconnected and the toilet is pulled out of the collar. The drain opening is plugged so that no smell enters the room.
  3. Checking the outlet It is checked whether the end of the pipe is intact and whether the collar suits the outlet of the new toilet. At this point it becomes clear whether extra parts are needed.
  4. Installation The toilet is set down, connected to the drain, levelled and fixed to the floor. The water is connected with a flexible hose through a shut-off valve.
  5. Test and sealing The toilet is flushed several times, watching for leaks at the collar and the supply. The joint at the floor is filled with sanitary silicone.

The maker Sika states for its Sikasil-117 sanitary silicone that a layer about 3 mm thick cures in about 24 hours (at 23 °C and 50% humidity), while a thicker joint cures more slowly. The toilet can be used, but it is better not to wash the joint in the meantime.

Urinal installation

A urinal is more often fitted in a workshop, a garage or a company toilet than in a home. It needs a water supply for flushing and a drain outlet, and many models are mounted on their own concealed frame. If the outlets are in place, the job is similar to installing a wall-hung toilet. If not, the outlets must be built first, and on the call you talk through the job and the price.

How much does toilet installation cost?

  • Installing or replacing a floor-standing toilet: from €50. Removing the old toilet is usually charged extra.
  • Concealed frame with a wall-hung toilet: from €85. Plasterboard cladding and tiling are extra.
  • If the outlet does not match and has to be rebuilt: building an outlet from €35 per outlet.
  • Materials (collar, flexible hose, fixings, silicone) are extra.

The price goes up if the old toilet is cemented into the floor, if the floor is not level or if the toilet has to be moved to another place.

Frequently asked questions

Will the new toilet fit in the old place?

Often it does, if the outlet direction (towards the wall or the floor) and the distance from the wall are similar. In the form, write the type or model of both toilets, and the specialist will say whether a different collar or an outlet rebuild is needed.

Can I buy the toilet myself?

Yes. A toilet usually comes with a cistern, but the collar and the flexible hose do not always. You can bring them along or agree that the plumber sources the materials.

Can a floor-standing toilet be changed to a wall-hung one without a rebuild?

No. A wall-hung toilet needs a frame in the wall and new wall cladding, so it is a bathroom rebuild job. Frame installation costs from €85, the finishing is extra.

When can the toilet be used after installation?

The toilet can be flushed right after the test. The silicone joint at the floor needs time to cure: for Sikasil-117 the maker states about 24 hours for a 3 mm layer.
