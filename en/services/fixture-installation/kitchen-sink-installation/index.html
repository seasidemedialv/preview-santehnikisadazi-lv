Under a kitchen sink a mixer tap and a dishwasher usually meet as well, so the trap is chosen with the branches needed. A mixer tap is from €25, a dishwasher connection from €35.

What is different about a kitchen sink

Soap and hair go into a bathroom sink, and fat and food waste into a kitchen one. The advice of SIA “Rīgas ūdens” (Riga's water utility) says that fat, oil and coffee grounds settle on the pipe walls and form blockages, so they should be thrown in the bin, and a strainer is useful in the sink. The drain pipe in a kitchen is also thicker: LBN 221-15 sets at least 40 mm for it and 32 mm for a hand basin.

In a kitchen the pipe to the drain riser is often long, especially if the sink is on an island or by a window. For pipes up to 50 mm LBN 221-15 requires a slope of 30 ‰, that is, 3 cm per metre, and the slope must not change between connections. If a sink has been moved far from its old place, too little slope is a common reason for slow draining.

Latvian Building Code LBN 221-15 “Internal Water Supply and Sewerage of Buildings” (Cabinet Regulation No. 332 of 30.06.2015), paras. 232, 269 and 272 and Annex 3. Checked 13.11.2026.

Fitting a sink in a worktop

The sink is placed in a cut-out in the worktop. A drop-in sink has its rim above the worktop and is held by fixing clips from below, an undermount sink is fixed under the worktop, and the edge of the cut-out must be finished. The joint between the sink and the worktop is sealed so that water does not get into the cut edge of the worktop.

If the cut-out has not been sawn yet, write that in the form. The cut-out in a stone or composite worktop is usually made by the worktop maker, because it is a different skill. In a laminate worktop the cut-out can be sawn using the sink template.

A trap with branches and a dishwasher

A double-bowl sink needs a trap with two inlets, and a dishwasher needs a branch in the trap for its outlet hose. LBN 221-15 sets at least a 40 mm drain pipe and a 10 mm water supply for a dishwasher. All the fixtures are in one room, so they can be connected to one trap, but a fixture from another room must not be connected to it.

The British Building Regulations recommend a 75 mm deep water seal for a kitchen sink trap. The trap must be removable, because in a kitchen it is cleaned more often than elsewhere. Dishwasher connection is described on the page washing machine connection.

Installing a waste disposal unit

A disposal unit is installed in place of the sink waste outlet, and its outlet is connected to the trap. It needs a socket under the sink, and that is an electrician's job. LBN 261-15 requires residual-current protection of no more than 30 mA for general sockets up to 32 A.

If the house is connected to a septic tank, read the septic tank maker's instructions on what may be discharged into it before buying a disposal unit.

Latvian Building Code LBN 261-15 “Internal Electrical Installation of Buildings”, para. 28.¹ (amendments by Cabinet Regulation No. 355 of 17.06.2025). Checked 13.11.2026.

How a kitchen sink is installed

  1. Removing the old sink The valves are closed, the tap, the dishwasher hose and the trap are disconnected, the clips are unscrewed and the sink is lifted out.
  2. Tap and waste outlets The mixer tap and the waste outlets are fitted in the sink while it is still outside, because the space under the worktop is narrow.
  3. Fixing in place The sink is placed in the cut-out with a gasket or silicone and the clips are tightened.
  4. Connection The trap with branches, the tap hoses to the valves and the dishwasher outlet are connected.
  5. Test Both bowls are filled and emptied at the same time, and a short programme is run for the dishwasher, watching whether anything drips under the sink.

Frequently asked questions

Do you saw the cut-out in the worktop?

Write in the form what the worktop is. For stone and composite worktops the cut-out is usually made by the worktop maker; for a laminate worktop the cut-out can be discussed on the call.

Why does the new sink drain slowly?

Often the fault is the slope of the pipe or a blocked old drain pipe, not the sink itself. LBN 221-15 requires a fall of 3 cm per metre for pipes up to 50 mm.

Can a dishwasher be connected to the same trap?

Yes, if the trap has a branch. The machine and the sink are in the same room, so it is allowed.

Can the sink be moved to an island?

It can, if a drain with the right slope can be run under the floor. This is described on the page plumbing outlet installation.
