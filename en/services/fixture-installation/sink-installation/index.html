Sink height

Annex 14 of the Latvian Building Code LBN 221-15 for residential buildings says that a hand basin is usually fitted with its top edge at 800 mm. A wall-mounted mixer tap is placed 200 mm above the basin edge, with a tolerance of ±20 mm. This is a guide, not a requirement for every home: a family with very tall or very short people can choose a different height.

The height must be decided before the walls are finished, because it determines where the water and drain outlets come out of the wall. If the sink goes on a cabinet, the cabinet sets the height, and the outlets must come out so that they stay inside the cabinet.

Latvian Building Code LBN 221-15 “Internal Water Supply and Sewerage of Buildings” (Cabinet Regulation No. 332 of 30.06.2015), Annex 14 (fixture heights) and Annex 3 (diameters). Checked 13.11.2026.

The sink type decides the fixing

Sink type What to consider
Wall-hung sink the wall must carry the load; a plasterboard wall needs a built-in support or frame, which must be fitted before the cladding
Sink on a cabinet the cabinet must be assembled and placed before connecting, and openings for the outlets cut at the back
Countertop sink the mixer tap must be tall or wall-mounted, and the trap is chosen by the type of waste outlet
Small hand basin in the toilet a 32 mm drain pipe is enough, but the space under it is narrow

If the sink replaces an old one, the main question is whether the fixing holes and the waste outlet of the new sink match the old ones. If not, new holes must be drilled in the wall, and the old ones filled.

The trap under the sink

The trap holds a water seal that stops sewer air from entering the room. The British Building Regulations (Approved Document H) recommend a 75 mm deep seal for a sink trap, while the maker Geberit states 50 mm for its sink trap. The trap must be removable or have a cleaning opening, so that a blockage can be cleared.

LBN 221-15 does not allow fixtures from different rooms to be connected to one trap. If a washing machine stands next to the sink in the same room, the trap can be taken with a branch for its hose. More about traps: sink trap replacement.

How a sink is replaced

  1. Removing the old sink The valves under the sink are closed, the mixer tap hoses and the trap are disconnected, and the sink is taken off its fixings.
  2. Checking the outlets It is checked whether the drain pipe and the water valves are intact and suit the new sink and tap.
  3. Fixing The sink is levelled and fixed to the wall or placed on the cabinet. The mixer tap is usually fitted on the sink before it is fixed, because it is easier to reach then.
  4. Connection and test The trap and the tap hoses are connected, the water is turned on, the sink is filled to the overflow and emptied, watching for any drips.
  5. The joint at the wall The gap between the sink and the wall is filled with sanitary silicone so that water does not get behind the sink.

How much does sink installation cost?

  • Installing a sink with a trap, connected to existing outlets: from €40.
  • Installing or replacing a mixer tap: from €25; flexible hoses, if needed, are extra.
  • If an outlet has to be moved or built from scratch: from €35 per outlet (one point).
  • The sink, trap, fixings and silicone are materials, and they are extra.

The price goes up with a brick or concrete wall where new holes must be drilled, with assembling a cabinet and with removing the fixings of the old sink.

Frequently asked questions

At what height is a sink fitted?

Annex 14 of LBN 221-15 for residential buildings gives usually 800 mm to the top edge of a hand basin, with a tolerance of ±20 mm. On a cabinet, the cabinet sets the height.

Can the old trap be reused?

It can, if it is intact and fits the waste outlet of the new sink. Often a new sink needs a different trap, and it is a cheap material compared with the labour.

Do you assemble the cabinet?

Assembling a cabinet raises the price, and it can be discussed on the call. If the cabinet is already assembled and in place, the job is shorter.

Can a sink be hung on a plasterboard wall?

It can, if the wall has a built-in support or frame fitted before the cladding. In empty plasterboard the fixings do not hold the load for long. In the form, write the wall material.

Can the sink be moved to another wall?

It can, but then new water and drain outlets must be built. The page plumbing outlet installation describes how this is done and what changes the price.
