What an outlet is and why it is planned before finishing

An outlet is the end of a pipe in the wall or floor, to which a fixture is connected later. A sink usually needs three: cold water, hot water and the drain; a toilet needs two, and a washing machine two. In the price list each of them is one point.

The position of the outlets is set by the fixture and its height, so the fixtures are chosen first and only then are the pipes run. If an outlet is moved after tiling, a finished wall has to be opened, and that costs more than the plumbing work itself.

Diameters and slope

Fixture Drain pipe, at least Water supply, at least
Toilet 100 mm 8 mm (for the cistern)
Bath, shower 40 mm 10 mm
Hand basin 32 mm 10 mm
Washing machine or dishwasher 40 mm 10 mm
Floor drain 50 or 100 mm not needed

Drains work by gravity, so they need a fall. For pipes up to 50 mm, LBN 221-15 sets a slope of 30 ‰ (3 cm per metre), and for 75–100 mm pipes 20 ‰ (2 cm per metre); the largest permitted is 150 ‰, and the slope must not change between connections. Horizontal water pipes need a slope of at least 2 ‰ towards the draining point, which also helps with winterising.

Latvian Building Code LBN 221-15 “Internal Water Supply and Sewerage of Buildings” (Cabinet Regulation No. 332 of 30.06.2015), Annex 3, paras. 107, 232, 269 and 272. Checked 13.11.2026.

Moving a toilet

A toilet is harder to move than a sink, because it needs a 100 mm pipe with a fall of 2 cm per metre. If the toilet has to be moved 3 m from the riser, the pipe under the floor or in the wall must drop by 6 cm. If there is no room for that in the floor, the toilet can be raised on a step, or a different place closer to the riser can be chosen.

If the fall cannot be made, small pump units that go behind the toilet are available. LBN 221-15 allows them if the unit is ventilated. The maker Grundfos states for its Sololift2 WC units that where a fall can be made, a gravity system is recommended, and the house must have another toilet above the backflow level. About such pumps: drainage and sewage pumps.

Moving outlets in an old house or summer house

In houses converted from summer houses, outlets are often in random places, and some of the pipes may be old. In old galvanised steel pipes, when the zinc wears away, rust forms inside, narrows the pipe and gives low pressure and brownish water. There is no sense in adding a new outlet to such a pipe, and it is better to replace a section: see pipe replacement in a house.

The second question is warmth. If the new outlet runs through an unheated room, remember the LBN 221-15 requirement: a room with cold water pipes must be above 2 °C, otherwise the pipes must be protected from freezing. Drains also need inspection (cleaning) openings, for example at the bottom of risers and at the start of a branch if three or more fixtures are connected to it.

How new outlets are built

  1. Plan The fixture positions and heights are marked, and it is chosen where to take the water from and where to connect the drain.
  2. Route The pipes run along the wall or floor, or in chases. Chasing and restoring the finish are not in the price of pipe installation.
  3. Installation The pipes are joined with the required slope and the outlets are fixed so that they do not move when the wall is finished.
  4. Test before covering LBN 221-15 requires a pressure test and flushing for a newly built water supply. The drain joints are also checked before the wall is closed.

Before the wall is closed, photograph the pipes with a tape measure next to them. Later, when drilling for a shelf or cabinet, you will know where the pipes run.

Frequently asked questions

How many points does one bathroom need?

Count the fixtures and their connections: a sink usually three, a toilet two, a shower three. In the form, write the list of fixtures, and on the call you talk through the job and the price.

Can a toilet be moved to the other side of the room?

It can, if a 100 mm pipe with a fall of 2 cm per metre can be run under the floor. If not, the options are a step or a small pump unit behind the toilet.

Can the outlets be built before the fixtures are bought?

Better not. The height and position of the outlets depend on the particular fixture, so at least choose the models in advance.

Do you also do chasing and tiling?

Tiling is a different trade. Chasing can be discussed on the call; it is not included in the price of pipe installation.
