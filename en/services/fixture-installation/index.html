What fixture installation is and what it consists of

Fitting the fixture is the last step. A toilet, sink or washing machine is connected to outlets that already come out of the wall or floor: cold and hot water and the drain. If the outlets are in the right place and at the right height, the job is short. If not, they are rebuilt first, and that is a separate job with its own price.

In houses in Ādaži Municipality and the nearby villages of Ropaži Municipality, plumbing is often done in different conditions than in a flat. The water comes from your own well or borehole with a hydrophore (a pump with a pressure tank), the wastewater goes to a septic tank or to a new central sewer connection, and some houses are former summer houses with old pipes. This changes little for the fixture itself, but the pressure, the risk of freezing in an unheated room and the condition of the trap do have to be taken into account.

When you need a plumber and when you can do it yourself

Some jobs can be done without a master plumber. If water keeps running into the toilet after flushing, the maker Geberit advises taking out the flush valve, cleaning it with water or a dry cloth and putting it back, because the fault is often dirt or limescale between the seal and the opening. The same maker advises pulling hair out of a shower trap with long tweezers.

You need a plumber when a fixture has to be connected from scratch, when water appears on the floor, when a toilet, frame or sink has to be replaced, and when a fixture has to be moved elsewhere. Also when it is not clear where a puddle comes from: a leak on the supply and a leak at a drain connection look similar, but are fixed differently.

How much does fixture installation cost?

The prices in the table are labour prices from the public price list, materials are extra. The final sum is set by the particular job, and on the call you talk through the job and the price before the plumber starts.

  • Removing the old fixture: removing an old toilet is usually charged extra.
  • Whether the outlets fit: if the outlet or the supply of the new fixture is in a different place, an outlet rebuild is needed, from €35 per point.
  • Wall and floor: for a wall-hung sink or a frame the wall must carry the load, while plasterboard cladding and tiling are not plumbing work.
  • Access: a concealed cistern or a trap behind a bath panel takes longer.
  • Time: if the work is not at a fixed price, it is charged by the hourly rate, from €25/h, and the minimum charge is usually 1 hour.

A plumber's call-out costs from €40, and if the work is done straight away, it is usually included in the labour price. All prices in one place: plumber prices.

Plumbing installation in a bathroom

In a bathroom several fixtures are usually fitted at once: a toilet, a sink, mixer taps, a bath or a shower. The order matters. The outlets are built while the walls are still open, the concealed frame is installed before the cladding, and the toilet, sink and taps after tiling. If a step is skipped, a finished wall has to be opened later.

Baths, shower cabins and shower systems are described on the page bathroom plumbing, and changing taps on the page mixer tap repair and replacement. Tiling and a full bathroom renovation are a different trade.

Plumbing connection in a kitchen and a utility room

In a kitchen a mixer tap, a dishwasher and a trap with branches meet under the sink, and grease that causes blockages gets there. In a utility room or cellar a washing machine stands, and in a house its work is affected by the hydrophore pressure. If the room is not heated, the water in the pipes and in the machine can freeze in winter.

LBN 221-15 requires a room with cold water pipes to be above 2 °C, otherwise the pipes must be protected from freezing. Remember this if a washing machine is put in a garage, a sauna or a summer kitchen.

Latvian Building Code LBN 221-15 “Internal Water Supply and Sewerage of Buildings” (Cabinet Regulation No. 332 of 30.06.2015), paras. 107 and 109. Checked 13.11.2026.

What to write in the form

You cannot attach a photo to the form, so describe the situation in words. This helps the specialist:

  • what has to be done: a new fixture, replacement or repair, and how many fixtures;
  • the type of fixture and, if you know, the maker and model (for example a floor-standing or wall-hung toilet);
  • whether the fixture is already bought and whether the outlets are in place;
  • a house or a flat, the floor and the room, and whether the room is heated;
  • the water source: the central water supply or your own well or borehole with a hydrophore;
  • the drains: central or a septic tank;
  • how urgent: whether anything is leaking now.

What most often goes wrong

  • A fixture is bought before it is checked whether its outlet matches the existing drain outlet.
  • Fixtures from different rooms are connected to one trap, although para. 216 of LBN 221-15 does not allow it.
  • The end of a washing machine outlet hose is submerged in the sink water, and the water can be sucked back into the machine.
  • Screw connections are tightened with a spanner, although the maker (Bosch) requires washing machine hoses to be tightened by hand.
  • A silicone joint is loaded before it has cured: the maker Sika states that sanitary silicone about 3 mm thick cures in about 24 hours, and a thicker joint takes longer.

Frequently asked questions

Can I buy the fixture myself?

Yes. In the form, write the maker and model, so that the specialist can say whether it fits the existing outlets and what parts will still be needed. Materials and fixtures are not in the labour price.

Do you install fixtures in a flat too?

Yes, connecting a toilet, sink, trap and machines in a flat is done the same way. If the shared riser has to be shut off, that must be agreed with the building manager.

Can several fixtures be installed at once?

Yes. Write all the fixtures in the form at once, so that on the call you can talk through the whole job and the price. Each fixture has its own labour price in the price list.

Do you also tile or build plasterboard walls?

No. Those are different trades. The plumber builds the outlets and installs the frame before the cladding, and installs the fixtures after the finishing.
