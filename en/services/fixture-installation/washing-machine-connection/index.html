The machine is connected, checked for leaks, and the water intake and pump-out are tested. A dishwasher connection costs the same. If there are no outlets yet, building a new outlet is a separate job, from €35 per point.

What the machine needs from the house

  • Water: the maker Bosch requires the washing machine to be connected only to cold drinking water, with the new inlet hose supplied.
  • Pressure: Bosch states an optimal pressure of 1–10 bar and a flow of at least 8 l/min; if the pressure is higher, a pressure reducer must be fitted.
  • Drain: LBN 221-15 sets at least a 40 mm drain pipe and a 10 mm water supply for a washing machine or dishwasher.
  • Electricity: LBN 261-15 requires residual-current protection of no more than 30 mA for general sockets up to 32 A. The socket is an electrician's job.

LBN 221-15 (Cabinet Regulation No. 332), Annex 3; Latvian Building Code LBN 261-15 “Internal Electrical Installation of Buildings”, para. 28.¹ (Cabinet Regulation No. 355 of 17.06.2025). Checked 13.11.2026.

Connecting a washing machine to the drain

The outlet hose is connected to a branch of the trap or to a separate drain outlet in the wall. For its Standard and AquaSecure models Bosch states a height from the floor to the drain of 60–100 cm, and for the AquaStop model 0–100 cm. Other makers may give different figures, so check the instructions for your model.

If the hose is simply hung in a sink, its end must not be submerged in the pumped-out water, otherwise water can be sucked back into the machine. The hose is fixed so that it does not slip out and the water does not end up on the floor.

In a house with its own well and hydrophore

If the water comes from your own well or borehole, the pressure in the house is set by the hydrophore (a pump with a pressure tank) pressure switch. For example, the maker Caleffi states a factory setting of 1.4–2.8 bar (switch-on and switch-off) for its switch. That is above the 1 bar minimum stated by Bosch, but if the hydrophore works badly, the machine can fill slowly or show a water supply error. Then the problem is in the pump, not the machine: see hydrophores and water pressure.

In houses the machine is often placed in a cellar, garage or sauna. LBN 221-15 requires a room with cold water pipes to be above 2 °C. When water freezes it expands, and a pipe or a machine part full of water can burst, so in an unheated room the machine and its hoses are drained in winter.

Dishwasher connection

A dishwasher is usually connected under the kitchen sink: the water hose to a separate valve, the outlet to a trap branch. For a built-in dishwasher the hoses are pulled out through the side of the cabinet before the machine is pushed into the niche and the front is fixed. After connecting, a short programme is run and the joints under the sink are inspected. If the sink is only now being fitted, see kitchen sink installation.

Fridge water connection

Fridges with an ice maker or water supply need a cold water supply with its own shut-off valve. It is usually branched from the nearest cold water pipe under the sink. The hose and the fittings are set by the fridge maker's instructions, so write the model in the form. The work is charged by the hourly rate or as a new outlet.

How the connection is done

  1. Checking the place It is checked whether the tap shuts off, whether the drain outlet is at the right height and whether the floor is level.
  2. Connecting the water The new inlet hose is connected; Bosch requires the screw connections to be tightened by hand only.
  3. Connecting the outlet The outlet hose is fixed to the trap branch or the outlet at the required height.
  4. Testing A short programme is run, watching whether water flows in, whether the machine pumps out and whether anything drips.

Frequently asked questions

Can a washing machine be connected to hot water?

Bosch does not allow this for its machines: they are connected only to cold drinking water. For other makers, see the instructions.

Can the old inlet hose be used?

No. Bosch requires using only the new inlet hose that comes with the machine.

When pumping out, the machine draws water back in by itself. Why?

One reason can be a hose end submerged in water or a wrong drain height. Bosch states the height at which the drain must be and forbids submerging the hose in the pumped-out water.

Do you also connect the electricity?

No. A socket with residual-current protection is an electrician's job.
