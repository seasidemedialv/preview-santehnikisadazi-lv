Connecting a house drain to a septic tank in Ādaži

Connecting a house drain to a septic tank in Ādaži and the Riga region is the house-side job: the outlet through the foundations, the line in the yard with the right fall and depth, and the connection to the septic tank inlet.

Choosing, installing and emptying the septic tank is a different job. The price of the yard line depends on the job: the length, depth, digging and soil decide it. A drain outlet inside the house up to the fixture costs from €35 per outlet.

What is included on the house side

A building's internal drains run from the fixtures to the first inspection chamber outside the building. Where there is no external network, wastewater is dealt with under LBN 223, and in a house that is usually a septic tank, a holding tank or a biological treatment unit. The plumber's work is everything up to the septic tank inlet: the horizontal line in the house, the outlet through the foundations, the line in the yard and the chambers on it.

What happens after the septic tank is part of the septic system. Wastewater may be released into the environment from a septic tank only through an infiltration system, for example a filtration field or filtering drains, and the septic tank installer builds it.

The outlet through the foundations

Where the outlet crosses the foundations or a basement wall, a 0.2 m gap is left around the pipe and filled with a flexible material that is watertight and gastight, and in wet ground a sealing sleeve is needed. The outlet diameter must not be smaller than the largest connected riser, and an outlet from the basement needs a fall of at least 20 ‰ (2 cm per metre).

The length of the outlet from the riser or inspection point to the axis of the chamber must not exceed 8 m for a 50 mm pipe, 12 m for a 100 mm pipe and 15 m for a 150 mm pipe. So a septic tank placed far from the house needs a chamber in the yard.

The yard line: depth, frost and chambers

An external gravity sewer pipe is laid 0.3 m shallower than the standard soil frost depth that is possible once in 100 years, but no shallower than 0.7 m to the top of the pipe. In clay soil in Riga this depth is 120 cm, and for sandy soil the value is multiplied by 1.2. Pipes lying at 0.7 m depth or shallower must be protected from freezing and from traffic loads.

Chambers in an external sewer are placed at connection points where the direction, fall or diameter changes, and on straight sections every 35 m. In a green area the chamber cover is set 20–70 mm above the ground so that it can be found and opened when the line has to be cleaned. If the septic tank is lower than the house, the fall is easy to make; if it is higher, a sewage pump may be needed.

Cabinet Regulation No. 327, LBN 223-15 “Sewerage Structures”, clauses 67, 73 and 80; Cabinet Regulation No. 432, LBN 003-19 “Building Climatology”, Table 15. Checked 13.11.2026.

How the connection works

  1. Levels The heights of the house outlet and the septic tank inlet and the distance between them are determined, to see whether an even fall is possible.
  2. Route We choose the straightest path, the places for chambers at the bends and the pipe depth according to the soil and frost.
  3. Outlet A hole is made in the foundations or the existing one is used, and the pipe is fitted with a seal.
  4. Line and chambers The pipe is laid in the trench on an even base with a fall, and insulated in shallow places.
  5. Connection and check The line is connected to the septic tank inlet and water is run from the house before the trench is backfilled.

Connecting to the central sewer instead of a septic tank

If a new network is built in the street, the house-side work is similar: switching the old line from the septic tank to the new connection. In Ādaži Municipality, in areas planned for the central system, the owner must connect within one year after new main networks are put into operation. The operator issues the technical conditions free of charge within 20 working days, and the applicant builds the connection at their own expense under the operator's supervision.

In Ropaži Municipality the municipality co-finances 50% of a residential house's sewer connection, up to €3,000 and no more than €60 per metre, but internal networks are not financed. In Ādaži Municipality the co-financing was abolished in 2022. In Riga co-financing for a private house's sewer connection is from €1,000 to €2,000 depending on the length. Binding regulations No. 72/2022 of Ādaži Municipality (amended by No. 11/2026) and binding regulations No. 24/2022; binding regulations No. 34/23 of Ropaži Municipality; Riga regulations No. RD-23-207-sn. Checked 13.11.2026.

Registration after a rebuild

A septic tank is a decentralised sewerage system and must be entered in the municipality's register. In Ādaži Municipality it is registered with the agency “Carnikavas komunālserviss”, and changes, including a rebuild, must be reported within one month. In Ropaži Municipality the confirmation is submitted to SIA “Garkalnes inženiertīkli”, and for new builds the system must be registered before the building is put into operation. In Riga a newly built system is registered within a month.

Binding regulations No. 3/2025 of Ādaži Municipality, binding regulations No. 45/22 of Ropaži Municipality, binding regulations No. 66 of Riga City Council; Cabinet Regulation No. 384, clause 15. Checked 13.11.2026.

How much does connecting a drain to a septic tank cost?

The price of the line in the yard up to the septic tank depends on the job. It is decided by the length, depth and insulation of the line, the digging, the soil, the number of chambers and whether the hole in the foundations already exists. Digging and materials are not included in the price of labour; on the call you talk through the job and the price. If the internal outlets in the house also have to be redone, each drain outlet up to the fixture costs from €35 per outlet.

Frequently asked questions

Do you also install the septic tank itself?

No. We build the house side: the outlet, the line and the chambers up to the septic tank inlet. Installing the septic tank, the infiltration system and emptying are the work of other specialists.

How deep must the line to the septic tank lie?

LBN 223-15 sets 0.3 m shallower than the frost depth possible once in 100 years, but no shallower than 0.7 m to the top of the pipe. A pipe buried shallower is insulated.

Is switching the old line to the central network co-financed in Ropaži Municipality?

Ropaži Municipality regulations No. 34/23 co-finance the connection at 50%, but internal networks are not financed. See the conditions in the regulations or ask the municipality.

What should I write in the request?

The distance from the house to the septic tank, whether the septic tank is already buried, whether the house has a basement and where the existing pipe exits. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes.
