We replace one section or the whole horizontal line with the riser, and the new lines follow the falls, diameters and inspection points of LBN 221-15. The work costs from €10/m, and building one outlet from €35.

A drain pipe is leaking: what to do now

  1. Stop using fixtures above the leak Water leaks from a drain line only when the line is used. Do not run water in the sinks, bath and washing machine connected to this line.
  2. Collect the water Put a container under the leak and wipe the floor. Wastewater is not clean water, so work in gloves.
  3. Look at the spot Note whether a joint, a crack in the pipe or a socket is leaking, and from which floor and fixture the water comes. This helps to tell whether one section is enough.
  4. Request the work Describe in the form where the leak is and what material the pipe is made of. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes.

If the leak is only at a joint or in a short accessible section, it is a leak-fixing job from €40. If the pipe cracks in several places, it is worth replacing the whole section.

How pipe replacement differs from cleaning

Cleaning frees the pipe but does not repair it. If the line has a sagging section with a reverse fall, a crack or roots in a joint, the blockage comes back. In replacement the old section is taken out and the new one is laid with the correct fall and inspection points, through which the line can be cleaned in future without opening the floor.

In a house a line built in a summer house is also often replaced when the house is rebuilt for year-round living: a line meant for one sink is not designed for a bathroom and a washing machine.

Cast-iron drain replacement

Cast-iron pipes are heavy and are joined with sockets. When only part is replaced, the new plastic pipe must be joined to the old cast iron with an adapter, and the old pipe must be secured so that it does not slip down. If the riser is replaced, it is done from the bottom up or all at once, because the riser carries the wastewater of every floor.

Before the work, find out where the house outlet runs and whether the basement has an inspection point. LBN 221-15 requires inspection points at the bottom and top of risers and in riser sections in the basement, so replacement is a good moment to fit them.

Requirements for new lines

  • Fall: 30 ‰ (3 cm per metre) for pipes up to 50 mm, 20 ‰ (2 cm per metre) for 75–100 mm pipes; the maximum permitted fall is 150 ‰, and the fall must not change between connections.
  • Diameter: at least 100 mm for a toilet bowl, 40 mm for a bath, shower, kitchen sink, washing machine or dishwasher, 32 mm for a hand basin.
  • Inspection points: on horizontal sections every 12 m for pipes up to 100 mm, every 15 m for 100–150 mm pipes.
  • Ventilation: the end of the riser at least 0.5 m above a pitched roof.
  • Frost: drain pipes are built so that they do not freeze.

Cabinet Regulation No. 332, LBN 221-15 “Internal water supply and sewerage of buildings”, clauses 232, 249, 253, 255, 269 and 272 and Annex 3. Checked 13.11.2026.

How drain replacement works

  1. Defining the section We find out where the line starts and ends, which fixtures are connected to it and whether the pipe runs in a wall, a floor or the basement.
  2. Fixtures out of use Fixtures whose water goes down this line are not used during the work. If the riser is replaced, water cannot be used on the upper floors either.
  3. Removal The old pipe is taken out, and the remaining ends are prepared for connection.
  4. Fitting The new pipe is laid with an even fall, fixings and inspection points, and the fixture outlets are connected.
  5. Checking Water is run from each fixture to see whether the joints leak and the water flows away freely.

Frequently asked questions

Can I replace only part of a cast-iron drain?

Yes. The new plastic section is joined to the old cast iron with an adapter. If the old pipe cracks in several places, consider replacing a longer section on the call.

Why does the new drain block?

Often the cause is a wrong fall or a change of fall between connections, which LBN 221-15 does not allow. Then water and sediment stay in the pipe. Also check that the traps are in place.

Does the floor have to be opened for drain replacement?

If the pipe runs in a concrete floor, yes. If it is in a basement or under a wooden floor, access is usually easier. Restoring floors and tiles is not included in the price of pipe replacement.

Do you also replace the line in the yard?

Yes, the yard line up to the chamber or septic tank can be replaced in sections. For the fall, depth and chambers of the yard line, see the page on connecting a house drain to a septic tank.
