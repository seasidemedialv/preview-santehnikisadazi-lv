A high-pressure water jet removes the grease layer, silt and sand from the whole pipe wall, not just drilling a way through the blockage. Hydrodynamic jetting costs from €60 an hour, minimum one hour.

How jetting differs from cleaning with a snake

A rotating snake breaks up grease deposits, cuts roots and opens a way for the water. After it, a layer of grease and silt stays on the pipe walls, and the next blockage forms on it faster. High-pressure hydrodynamic jetting removes this layer from the pipe walls, and a summary by the US Environmental Protection Agency says that it is effective especially for small-diameter pipes.

So the snake is the job to start with if the water is simply standing, while jetting is for when the capacity of the line has shrunk along its whole length. Often the two are combined: the snake opens the way, then the line is jetted.

When jetting is the right choice

  • The kitchen line blocks every few months: there is a grease layer in the pipe.
  • Sand and silt have built up in the yard line to the chamber or septic tank.
  • After cleaning with a snake the water drains away, but slowly.
  • Before connecting a new kitchen or bathroom to an old line, to see whether it is usable.
  • A summer house being rebuilt for year-round living, where the old line has been little used for years.

If only one trap is blocked, jetting is not needed. Ordinary drain cleaning from €50 is enough then.

How hydrodynamic drain cleaning works

  1. Access We find a place where the hose can be fed into the line: an inspection hatch in the house, the inspection point of the house outlet or a yard chamber. The closer to the blockage, the shorter the job.
  2. Jetting against the flow A nozzle with jets pushes the hose back along the pipe, and the water carries the loosened sediment back to the access point, where it is collected or led to a chamber.
  3. Repeating in sections A long line is jetted in sections between chambers. In an external sewer, under LBN 223-15 chambers are placed where the direction, fall or diameter changes, and on straight sections every 35 m.
  4. Checking with water At the end water is run from the fixtures in the house to see whether it flows freely to the chamber.

Drain jetting in a house and in the yard

In the house the horizontal line and the outlets from the kitchen and bathroom are jetted. In the yard the line between the house, the chambers and the septic tank or the connection to the central sewer is jetted. The line on the property is maintained by the owner: the Water Management Services Law sets the ownership boundary between the operator's and the owner's networks. If the blockage is beyond this boundary, in the street network, it is the operator's job.

Water Management Services Law, Section 1, clause 8: the ownership boundary between the service provider's and the owner's networks. Checked 13.11.2026.

When jetting does not help

Jetting does not repair a pipe with a reverse fall. LBN 221-15 requires a fall of 20 ‰ (2 cm per metre) for 75–100 mm pipes, and at least 20 ‰ also for a house outlet from the basement. If a section sags, water stands in it, and sediment builds up again. The same goes for a cracked joint or one pushed out by roots.

If after jetting the blockages return in the same place, the solution is replacing the section. On that: sewer pipe replacement.

How much does drain jetting cost?

Hydrodynamic drain jetting costs from €60 an hour, minimum one hour. If the line first has to be opened with a motorised snake, cleaning a yard line costs from €50 for the first hour. That is the price of labour; materials are extra.

Frequently asked questions

How often does a drain need jetting?

There is no set interval. Jetting is needed when the line blocks repeatedly or the water drains slowly. If that happens often, it is worth checking the fall of the pipe and the joints.

Can jetting damage old pipes?

Jetting cleans the pipe with water, but it does not repair a cracked or badly corroded section. If the line is very old, say on the call what material it is made of, so that the work can be chosen accordingly.

Can the line up to the septic tank be jetted?

Yes, the line between the house and the septic tank can be jetted from a chamber or the inspection point of the house outlet. Jetting does not empty the septic tank itself: a tanker operator does that.

What should I write in the request?
