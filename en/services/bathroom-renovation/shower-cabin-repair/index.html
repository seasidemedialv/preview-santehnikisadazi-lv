Shower cabin leaking: where to look for the cause

Where the water appears Possible cause What is done
On the floor by the door while showering worn or slipped door seals, magnetic strips, door does not close fully replace the seals, adjust the door and rollers
In the cabin corners or along the wall silicone joint between the profiles, tray and wall has come unstuck or turned black remove the old silicone and reseal
Under the tray when water runs to the drain loose or damaged trap connection, worn seal open the tray panel, tighten or replace trap parts
Under the tray even when the shower is not used a water supply pipe, flexible hose or mixer tap connection is leaking shut off the water and look for the leak in the supply pipes
Through a crack in the tray cracked tray the tray is usually replaced; you talk it through on the call

If water leaks even when nobody is showering, shut off the water and do not put it off: that is a leak under pressure. More on the page water leak.

Water not draining from the tray

Most often the cause is hair in the trap. The maker Geberit states that a shower trap cannot easily be cleaned without removing the tray, and advises pulling the hair out with long tweezers. If that does not help, or the water does not drain in the sink and bath either, the blockage is further along the drain. Clearing a blockage in the internal drains costs from €50: see drain cleaning.

If there is a smell from the trap but the cabin is rarely used, the water in the trap may have evaporated. Running the water for a few seconds is enough.

Weak flow or jumping temperature

If too little water comes from the shower, the maker hansgrohe advises checking the supply pressure and the pump, if there is one, and cleaning the filters at the mixer tap inlets and in the shower hose seal. In a house with its own well or borehole the cause can be the hydrophore (a pump with a pressure tank): about it, hydrophores and water pressure.

For a thermostatic mixer tap the hot and cold water pressure must be equal (hansgrohe). If the temperature jumps, the cause can be the pressure difference, not the tap. If a mixer tap drips even when closed, the fault is usually a damaged or limescale-clogged ceramic cartridge, which can be replaced. Mixer tap work is described on the page mixer tap repair and replacement.

Doors, rollers and seals

The rollers and seals of sliding doors wear out, and the door starts to sag or does not close. They can be replaced if spare parts are available for the specific model. That is why, in the form, write the make and model of the cabin, if known. If parts for an old model are not available or the glass is cracked, it is often better to fit a new cabin: shower cabin installation.

How shower cabin repair works

  1. Description in the form Write where the water appears, when it happens and what the cabin model is.

Frequently asked questions

Can a cracked tray be repaired?

A crack in the tray usually means replacing the tray, because water gets under the tray through it. If the new tray fits the old cabin, the rest can stay.

Do I need to know the cabin model?

It helps, because the seals, rollers and mixer tap parts are specific to a model. If the model is not known, describe the cabin in the form: shape, size, sliding or hinged door.

When is it better to replace the whole cabin?

When there are no spare parts, when the glass or the tray is cracked and when it leaks in several places at once. On the call you talk through the cost of repair against a new cabin.

Do you also repair hydromassage cabins?

The plumbing parts, yes: the drain, trap, mixer tap, jet supply pipes and sealant joints. The electrical parts are repaired by an electrician.
