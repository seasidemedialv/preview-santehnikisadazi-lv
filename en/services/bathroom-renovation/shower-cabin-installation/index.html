A prepared space means finished walls and floor and the outlets where the cabin instructions require them. Write in the form the cabin model and whether the space is already ready.

What must be ready before installation

  • The walls and floor are tiled and dry, if the cabin stands against tiles.
  • The drain outlet is where the tray instructions place the trap. For a shower the minimum outlet diameter under LBN 221-15 is 40 mm.
  • The cold and hot water supply pipes are where the cabin panel needs them.
  • For a cabin with electrical features (lighting, fan, radio), an electrician provides the socket.
  • All cabin parts and the instructions are in the box; check this before the work, while the box has not yet been thrown out.

If the outlets still have to be moved, for example in place of a bath, start with the page bath to shower conversion.

How shower cabin installation works

  1. Tray The tray is set on its feet or frame and levelled, so that the water runs to the drain and does not stay in the corners.
  2. Trap The tray trap is connected to the drain outlet and tested with water before the tray panel is closed.
  3. Walls and door The walls, door and profiles are assembled following the maker's instructions, and the door is adjusted so that it closes without gaps.
  4. Water The cabin mixer tap or panel is connected to the water supply pipes with flexible hoses, and the connections are checked.
  5. Sealing The joints between the tray, profiles and wall are sealed with sanitary silicone.

Trap under the tray: why access is needed

The maker Geberit states that a shower trap cannot easily be cleaned without removing the tray. If the tray panel is closed permanently or tiled in, a blockage or leak means taking the cabin apart. That is why the panel must stay removable. For example, UK guidance (Approved Document H) sets a 50 mm water seal for a shower trap, so that sewer air does not enter the room.

Shower drain installation

If you do not want a tray, the shower is made level with the floor, with a drain. A linear drain is long and narrow along the wall, and a point drain sits in the middle or corner of the shower area. Both need space under the floor and a fall: LBN 221-15 states that pipes up to 50 mm are laid with a 30 ‰ fall (3 cm per metre). The minimum outlet diameter for a drain is 50 or 100 mm.

Shower drains under EN 1253 usually have a 50 mm water seal, but the flow capacity depends on the model. For the Dallmer DallFlex DN 50 drain it is 0.83 l/s at a 20 mm water depth, and others may have less. For a rain shower it is worth comparing it with the flow of the shower head. The drain is built in before waterproofing and tiling, which another tradesperson does.

Outlet diameters and fall: Cabinet Regulation No. 332, LBN 221-15 (likumi.lv), checked 13.11.2026.

Shower glass panel and tray replacement

A glass panel without a cabin roof is fixed to the tiled wall and to the tray or floor with profiles, which must be levelled so that the door does not swing open by itself. Replacing the tray in an old cabin means taking the walls apart, removing the old silicone, replacing the tray and trap and assembling the cabin again, if the new tray matches its size.

How much does shower cabin installation cost?

Installing a shower cabin in a prepared space costs from €125. That includes assembling the cabin, connecting the tray and trap, connecting the water and sealing. If an outlet must be built or moved, each outlet costs from €35, chasing and materials extra.

For shower drain installation the price depends on the type of drain (linear or point), on whether the drain outlet must be lowered or moved and on opening the floor and forming the fall (screed). Waterproofing and tiling are usually separate. The price also depends on whether an old cabin must be removed. This is the labour price; materials are extra. All prices: plumbing prices.

Frequently asked questions

Can you assemble a cabin of any make?

If there is a full set of parts and the maker's instructions, the cabin can be assembled. In the form, write the make and model, and then on the call we can tell you what to keep in mind.

Do you also connect the cabin's electrics?

No. Lighting, fan or radio is connected by an electrician. We connect the water and drains.

Why does the water drain slowly from the tray?

Often there is hair in the trap, or the tray is not levelled. If the water does not drain even after the trap is cleaned, the drain further along may be blocked: then see drain cleaning.

Can a shower cabin be installed in a summer house?

Yes, but in an unheated house in winter the water must also be drained from the cabin mixer tap and trap. There is a page about this, summer house water system winterising.
