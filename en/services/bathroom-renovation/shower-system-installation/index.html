Shower system installation in Ādaži - rain shower and thermostat

Shower system installation in Ādaži and Riga Region means fitting a rain shower with a mixer tap or thermostat: either a surface-mounted rail on the existing outlets, or a concealed system whose body is set in the wall before tiling.

If the bathroom is not tiled yet, the concealed system must be decided before the tiler starts. If the tiles are already there and you do not want to break them, a surface-mounted rail is usually chosen.

Thermostat: pressure and hot water in a house

The maker hansgrohe states a recommended operating pressure of 1–5 bar and a maximum of 10 bar for mixer taps, and for a thermostat the hot and cold water pressure must be equal. In a house the hot water comes from the water heater and the cold from the mains or the hydrophore (a pump with a pressure tank), and the pressure on the two sides can differ. Then the thermostat does not hold the temperature.

LBN 221-15 states that the pressure in the domestic water supply at the lowest draw-off point must not exceed 6 bar. If it is higher, a pressure reducer is fitted, which keeps the outlet pressure steady even when the inlet pressure fluctuates.

A thermostat also helps against burns. At the draw-off point the hot water must be 55–70 °C under LBN 221-15, and the maker Ariston warns that water hotter than 50 °C can cause serious burns at once, especially to children and older people.

Maximum pressure 6 bar and hot water temperature 55–70 °C: Cabinet Regulation No. 332, LBN 221-15 (likumi.lv), checked 13.11.2026.

Rain shower, flow and drainage

A large rain shower head uses more water than a hand shower. If the house has its own well or borehole with a hydrophore, check that it supplies enough flow when another tap is open at the same time. If too little water comes out, hansgrohe advises checking the supply pressure and the pump and cleaning the filters at the mixer tap inlets and in the shower hose seal. About the pump: hydrophores and water pressure.

The water must also drain away. The flow capacity of a shower drain depends on the model, and for a rain shower it is worth comparing it with the flow of the shower head. For drains and trays, read the page shower cabin installation.

How shower system installation works

  1. Shutting off the water The water is shut off and the old shower mixer tap is removed.
  2. Checking the outlets For a surface-mounted system, we check that the outlet spacing and height suit the rail and that the outlets are not crooked. For a concealed system, the supply pipes are run to the body position.
  3. Fitting The mixer tap or thermostat is connected, and the rail or the built-in body is fixed following the maker's instructions.
  4. Testing The connections are checked under pressure before the wall is closed and tiled, and afterwards the temperature and flow are checked in each mode.

Frequently asked questions

Can a rain shower be fitted on the old outlets?

Often it can, if it is a surface-mounted system and the outlet spacing suits the rail. Check the outlet spacing in the system's instructions and compare it with yours.

Why does the thermostat not hold the temperature?

Most often because the hot and cold water pressure differ, or because the filters in the supply pipes are clogged. A thermostat needs equal pressure on both sides.

Can a concealed system be fitted in a bathroom that is already tiled?

Only by opening the wall, because the body and supply pipes are inside it. Restoring the tiles afterwards is another tradesperson's work.

Can I buy the system myself?

Yes. Write the make and model in the form, and then on the call we can tell you whether the existing outlets fit.
