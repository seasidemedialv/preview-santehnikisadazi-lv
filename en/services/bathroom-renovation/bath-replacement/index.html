How bath replacement differs from other bathroom work

A bath stands tight against tiled walls, and it is often built into a tiled panel. That is why the hardest part is not connecting the new bath but taking out the old one. If the panel is tiled, it must be taken apart, and the tiles above the bath edge can crack. Repairing tiles is another trade, so it is worth thinking about before the work.

The second matter is the drain outlet. In old houses there is often an old cast iron or plastic outlet with a worn seal under the bath. Once the bath is out, it can be inspected and, if needed, a short section replaced while it is still accessible.

If the whole bathroom must be rebuilt, see the page bathroom plumbing. If you want a shower in place of the bath, that is a different job: bath to shower conversion.

How bath replacement works

  1. Water and drain The water is shut off, the bath mixer tap is removed or disconnected if it is in the way, and the waste and overflow are disconnected.
  2. Removing the old bath The silicone seam is removed, the panel is taken apart, if there is one, and the bath is taken out. A cast iron bath is heavy, and usually several people are needed to carry it out.
  3. Checking the outlet The drain outlet and water supply pipes are inspected. If the outlet is damaged or is not in the right place for the new bath, it is replaced or moved.
  4. Installing the new bath The bath is set on its feet or frame, levelled and connected to a new waste and overflow set with a trap.
  5. Testing and the seam The bath is filled and emptied to see whether the drain leaks. After that the seam between the bath and the wall is sealed.

Mixer tap and height

The new bath may be longer or shorter than the old one, and then the mixer tap or the drain position no longer matches. Compare the bath dimensions with the old one before you buy. LBN 221-15 specifies a wall-mounted bath mixer tap at a height of 800 mm from the floor, tolerance ±20 mm. If you are also replacing the tap with the bath, see mixer tap repair and replacement.

Bath mixer tap height 800 mm from the floor, tolerance ±20 mm: Cabinet Regulation No. 332, LBN 221-15, Annex 14 (likumi.lv), checked 13.11.2026.

Connecting a whirlpool bath

For a whirlpool bath the plumbing part is the same as for an ordinary one: water, drain and overflow. The electrics differ, and they are connected by an electrician. Sadales tīkls (the electricity distribution network operator) states that because of damp the bathroom is one of the least safe rooms as far as electricity is concerned, and advises testing the residual current device with the “T” button at least once a year.

How much does bath replacement cost?

Installing a steel or acrylic bath on feet or a frame, with the waste and overflow connected, costs from €60. Removing the old bath, a cast iron bath and a panel are extra. If a mixer tap is replaced at the same time, it costs from €25, and a new drain outlet from €35 per outlet. This is the labour price; materials are extra.

The price depends on the type of the old bath, a tiled panel, the condition of the outlet and how easy it is to carry the bath in and out, for example to the second floor up a narrow staircase. All prices: plumbing prices.

Frequently asked questions

Do the tiles have to be replaced too?

Not always. If the bath stands free or the panel is not tiled, the wall tiles usually stay. If the panel is tiled, the tiles above the bath edge can crack, and a tiler repairs them.

Can I buy the new bath myself?

Yes. Before buying, measure the space and compare it with the old bath, so that the drain position and mixer tap match. In the form, write the type and size of the bath.

What happens to the old bath?

You talk that through on the call: whether the old bath must be carried out and where it goes. For a cast iron bath it is a separate job.

Why does water appear under the bath after replacement?

Most often the waste or overflow connection is leaking, or the seam at the wall has not cured and water runs behind the bath. That is why the bath is tested with water after installation, before the panel is closed.
