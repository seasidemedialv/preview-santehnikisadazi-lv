Three ways to put a shower in place of a bath

Solution What is needed What to keep in mind
Shower cabin with a tray An outlet to connect the tray trap to, and cold and hot water supply pipes The simplest option; the trap is under the tray and must stay accessible.
Tray with a glass panel The same as for a cabin, but the walls are tiled and the glass panel is fixed to the wall A low tray needs a lower outlet, or a tray on feet with a panel.
Level-access shower with a drain Opening the floor, a drain, a fall to the drain, waterproofing and tiles The biggest job; waterproofing and tiling are done by another tradesperson.

Why the drain outlet decides everything

A bath drain outlet is usually low by the floor. The trap of a shower tray, and especially a drain, needs room below floor level and a fall to the sewer. LBN 221-15 states that pipes up to 50 mm are laid with a 30 ‰ fall, that is, 3 cm for every metre, and that the fall must not change between connections. The farther the shower is from the riser, the higher the drain must sit or the deeper the outlet must go.

For a shower the minimum outlet diameter under LBN 221-15 is 40 mm, for a drain 50 or 100 mm. Shower drains under EN 1253 usually have a 50 mm water seal. The flow capacity depends on the model: for example, for the Dallmer DallFlex DN 50 drain it is 0.83 l/s at a 20 mm water depth, and other makers may have less. For a large rain shower it is worth comparing the drain's flow capacity with the flow of the shower head.

Fall and outlet diameters: Cabinet Regulation No. 332, LBN 221-15 (likumi.lv), checked 13.11.2026. Drain data: the Dallmer DallFlex DN 50 product sheet.

Water supply pipes for a shower

A bath mixer tap is low; LBN 221-15 places it 800 mm from the floor. A shower mixer tap is fitted higher, so the supply pipes usually have to be moved inside the wall, which means chases before tiling. If you want a thermostat or a rain shower, decide it before the work: for a concealed system the body must be built into the wall. About that on the page shower system installation.

How the work goes

  1. Removing the bath The water is shut off, the drain and mixer tap are disconnected, and the bath and panel are taken out.
  2. Outlets The drain outlet is lowered or moved to the position of the new trap or drain, and the water supply pipes are moved to shower height. The connections are tested with water before they are covered.
  3. Floor and walls If there is a drain, a fall is formed in the floor, and another tradesperson does the waterproofing and tiling.
  4. The shower The cabin, or the tray with a glass panel, is installed, the trap and mixer tap are connected, the drainage is tested and the seams are sealed.

Cabin installation is described in more detail on the page shower cabin installation.

How much does a bath to shower conversion cost?

Installing a shower cabin in a prepared space costs from €125: assembling the cabin, connecting the tray and trap, connecting the water and sealing. Each new or moved water or drain outlet costs from €35 per outlet, chasing and materials extra.

The price of installing a shower drain depends on whether the drain is linear or point, whether the drain outlet must be lowered or moved and whether the floor must be opened and a fall formed (screed). Waterproofing and tiling are usually separate. Removing the bath and its price you talk through on the call. This is the labour price; materials are extra.

Frequently asked questions

Can the old bath outlet stay?

Sometimes it can, if the shower tray is high enough and the trap reaches the outlet. For a level-access shower the old outlet is usually too high or not in the right place.

Will you do the tiling after the bath is removed?

No. We do the outlets, supply pipes and shower installation. Waterproofing and tiles are done by another tradesperson, between our two stages of work.

Which to choose: a cabin or a shower with a drain?

A cabin needs less work and suits you if you do not want to open the floor. A shower with a drain has no threshold, but it needs the floor opened, a fall and room for the outlet under the floor.

Can a drain be installed in an old house?

It depends on the floor construction and on how far away the riser or outlet is. Write in the form what the floor is like and which floor the bathroom is on, and you talk it through on the call.
