Plumbing winterising in Ādaži - house water supply and frost

In a house lived in all year, the places where pipes run through the cold must be protected from frost: the supply from the well, the cellar, the outdoor tap, unheated rooms. A house that is left over winter is drained completely, including the water heater and the hydrophore (a pump with a pressure tank). The price depends on the system, and on the call you talk through the job and the price.

Why pipes burst in winter

When water freezes it expands: the density of ice is about 9% lower than that of water, so ice takes up a larger volume. A pipe, tap or pump body full of water can burst because of this. A burst is often noticed only when the ice melts and the water starts to run.

Plastic pipes are not fully protected either. The maker Uponor states that a flexible PEX pipe will usually withstand freezing, but repeated freezing weakens it, and a PEX pipe cast in concrete without a protective sleeve can be punctured when it freezes.

A house you live in and a house you leave

In an occupied house the most important thing is the pipes in unheated places: in the cellar, under the floor, in the loft, in the garage, by an outside wall and in the supply. Water industry advice is to insulate pipes with no gaps at bends, valves and joints, and Uponor advises insulated pipes where there is a risk of freezing. Where warmth is not enough, a heating cable helps.

If a house will stand empty for a long time, the advice is to close the inlet valve and drain the system so that no water is left in the pipes. This applies both to summer houses and to houses not lived in over winter. The working order for a summer house, from the pump to the traps, is on the page winterising a summer house water system.

When you need a plumber

  • The system has no drain taps at the lowest points, and water stays in the pipes.
  • The house has a water heater, a hydrophore, a heating circuit and several floors, and it is not clear what must be drained and in what order.
  • The outdoor tap freezes every winter or leaks in spring.
  • In spring after start-up something drips or the pump does not build up pressure.
  • The pipe has already frozen: then see the page frozen pipes.

Outdoor tap: frost-proof tap or ordinary tap with shut-off

With an ordinary outdoor tap the water stands right up to the tap outside, so it freezes in winter. It needs a shut-off valve inside the house and a way to drain the pipe section to the outdoor tap in autumn. In a frost-proof tap the valve is inside the wall, on the warm side, and when the tap is closed the water runs out of the outer part.

A frost-proof tap works only if it is fitted with a fall outwards and if the hose is disconnected from it in autumn, because a hose keeps water inside the tap. If a tap leaks or drips in spring, it has often frozen full and has a crack. Then the tap is replaced, and the pipe in the wall is checked together with it.

Draining the water heater and hydrophore

In an unheated house with a risk of frost the water heater is drained before the cold and disconnected from the power on all poles. The maker Dražice states that you first close the cold water supply and open the hot tap so that no vacuum forms in the tank, and advises draining the water pipes too, down to the point where they do not freeze.

The hydrophore and its tank are drained if the pump is not used in winter. The maker Grundfos states that a pump not in use during the frost period must have all the water drained from the pump chambers, otherwise frozen water can damage the internal parts of the pump. The drain screw is usually at the bottom of the pump body. For more on the pump, see the page hydrophores and water pressure.

Spring start-up

After winter the system is filled slowly, with the drain taps closed, and it is checked at once that nothing drips. Water industry advice, after a long standstill, is to remove the tap aerators and shower hoses, flush all the cold water first, then the hot, from the tap nearest the inlet to the farthest.

The water heater is switched on only when it is full. The maker Dražice advises testing the safety valve after every standstill longer than 5 days, and SPKC advises running the hot and cold water for a few minutes after a longer absence.

What to write in the form

  • Whether the house is used in winter or left, and whether it is heated.
  • Water source: a well or borehole with a hydrophore, or the central water supply.
  • What is in the house: a water heater, a heating circuit, a sauna, an outdoor tap, an outdoor shower.
  • Where the main valve is and whether there are drain taps.
  • Whether you need draining in autumn, start-up in spring or both. You cannot attach a photo to the form, so describe in words.

What most often goes wrong

  • The water supply is drained, but the water heater or the hydrophore is forgotten, and they freeze full.
  • A hose is left connected to the outdoor tap, and the frost-proof tap freezes.
  • Antifreeze is poured into drinking water pipes. The UK water regulations classify a heating fluid with ethylene glycol as a fluid posing a slight health hazard, so it is used only in a closed heating circuit.
  • A frozen pipe is heated with an open flame or a blowtorch. Water industry guidance warns against this.

Frequently asked questions

When should the water system be drained in a house not lived in over winter?

Before the first frosts, while the pipes have not yet frozen. If the house will stand empty for a long time, the advice is to close the inlet valve and drain the whole system, including the water heater and the hydrophore.

Does a frost-proof outdoor tap also have to be shut off for winter?

A frost-proof tap does not need to be drained separately if it is fitted with a fall outwards and the hose is disconnected from it. If the house has a shut-off valve on the outdoor tap supply, it is better to close it in winter.

Is it enough to leave a little heating on in the house?

In a heated house the internal pipes usually do not freeze, but the supply, the outdoor tap, the cellar and pipes by an outside wall can freeze. If the heating switches off, for example because of a power cut, a full system is at risk. That is why a house not lived in for a long time is better drained.

Can winterising and spring start-up be requested together?

Yes. In the form write that you need both, and on the call you talk through when each job is needed and what it costs.
