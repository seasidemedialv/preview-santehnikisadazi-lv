Summer house water system winterising - draining and spring start-up

Winterising a summer house water system means that in autumn all the water is let out of the pump, water heater, pipes and traps so that nothing bursts in an unheated house over winter, and in spring the system is filled, flushed and checked.

In Ādaži Municipality and the Riga region these are often garden houses with water from their own well. The price depends on what is in the house, and on the call you talk through the job and the price.

Why in a summer house it is a different job

A summer house stands unheated for months in winter, so partial draining is not enough: freezing can happen anywhere water is left in a pipe. In Ādaži Municipality and nearby villages there are several summer house areas. In Kadaga there is Upmalas, a summer house village between Lake Kadaga and the Gauja, Carnikava became a summer house place for Riga residents in the early 20th century, and the central part of Bukulti was allotted in 1986 to 368 garden plots.

Some of them have over time been rebuilt into houses lived in all year, but the water supply has often stayed the old one: pipes along a wall or under a floor with no insulation, a pump in an outbuilding. If someone lives in such a house in winter, it is no longer winterising but protection against frost; see the page winterising plumbing.

The order in which a summer house is drained

  1. Water and power The pump is switched off, the inlet valve is closed and the water heater is disconnected from the power on all poles.
  2. Water heater The cold water supply to the water heater is closed, the hot tap is opened so that no vacuum forms in the tank, and the water heater is drained through the drain tap or through the safety valve in its test position.
  3. Hydrophore The pump chambers and the tank are drained. The maker Grundfos states that a pump not in use during the frost period must have all its water drained, otherwise frozen water damages the internal parts of the pump.
  4. Pipes All taps are opened, including the outdoor tap and the shower, and the drain taps at the lowest points. If the pipes have no fall or have loops where water stays, the system is blown through with a compressor.
  5. Appliances The toilet cistern is drained, flexible hoses are disconnected and the water is drained from the washing machine according to its instructions.
  6. Traps They hold water that blocks the smell from the drains, and it can freeze and crack the plastic. It is poured out or sucked out, or the trap is removed.

The maker Dražice advises draining the water pipes down to the point where they do not freeze. If the supply from the well runs underground below the frost depth, water may stay in it, but everything above ground and in the house is drained.

Heating system: drain or antifreeze

If the summer house has water heating with radiators, it also freezes in winter. There are two options: the heating circuit is drained or filled with a heating fluid that does not freeze. The latter suits houses heated only at weekends or occasionally, because then it does not have to be refilled every time.

Antifreeze is used only in a closed heating circuit. The UK water regulations classify a heating fluid with ethylene glycol as a fluid posing a slight health hazard, so it is not put into drinking water pipes. The water supply is drained, not filled with antifreeze.

Sauna and outdoor shower

A sauna is often a separate building with its own supply, water heater or water tank by the stove. It is drained together with the house, otherwise the first frost splits exactly the sauna tap or shower. The supply to an outdoor shower is shut off indoors and drained completely. If it runs above ground, the hose or pipe is taken off for winter.

Starting up a summer house water system in spring

  1. Close the drain taps All drain taps are closed, the traps are put back and the hoses are reconnected.
  2. Fill slowly The system is filled gradually, and meanwhile the whole house is walked through: if a pipe has burst over winter, it can be seen at once.
  3. Flush Water industry advice: remove the tap aerators and shower hoses, flush all the cold water first, then the hot, from the tap nearest the inlet to the farthest, until there is fresh water in the pipes.
  4. Water heater The water heater is filled until water runs from the hot tap without air, and only then is it switched on. The maker Dražice advises testing the safety valve after every standstill longer than 5 days.
  5. Pump The hydrophore is filled, started and checked to see that it switches off at the set pressure.

SPKC advises cleaning and disinfecting water heaters at least once a year. Spring, when the water heater is empty anyway, is the time to do it: see the page water heater cleaning.

How much summer house winterising costs

The price of winterising a summer house water system and opening it in spring depends on whether only the water supply is drained or also the heating and the drain traps, and on whether the system is blown through with a compressor. It also depends on whether the water heater and the hydrophore have to be drained, and in spring on the filling and the leak check. Frequently asked questions

Is it enough to close the main valve?

No. Closing the valve leaves water in the pipes, the water heater, the pump and the traps, and in an unheated house it freezes. The water industry advice for an empty house is to close the inlet and drain the whole system.

Can the water heater in a summer house be left full?

If the house freezes in winter, no. The maker Dražice states that if there is a risk of frost, the water heater must be drained and disconnected from the power on all poles.

When should a summer house be drained in autumn?

Before the first frosts. If the summer house is also used in the autumn holidays, it can be drained after the last visit, but until then the house must be heated or at least the most vulnerable places drained after every visit.

Can I request both the autumn draining and the spring start-up?
