Frozen pipes in Ādaži - thawing a water supply in a house

In Ādaži and the Riga region, pipes most often freeze where the water supply passes through cold places: the supply from the well under the yard, the cellar, pipes by an outside wall, an outdoor tap.

If no water comes from the tap, close the inlet valve and open the tap, but do not heat the pipe with a flame. The price of thawing depends on the place and the material, and replacing a burst section in an accessible place costs from €40.

What to do now

  1. Close the inlet valve In a house with a well, also switch off the hydrophore (a pump with a pressure tank) so that it does not run without water.
  2. Open the tap Open the tap that has stopped running, so that the melting water can run out and the pressure in the pipe does not build up.
  3. Warmth slowly, from the tap side If you can reach the frozen spot, heat it with hot water bottles or a warmed cloth, starting from the tap side. The UK's WaterSafe and CCW also allow a hairdryer on a warm setting, but Affinity Water does not recommend it.
  4. No open flame Do not use a blowtorch, a gas burner or any other open flame. The UK water industry guidance (WaterSafe, CCW) forbids it.
  5. Look for cracks If something leaks after thawing, close the valve again and fill in the form. If water runs onto electrical equipment, switch off the power at the fuse board only if you can do so safely, without standing in water. Do not approach a flooded fuse board.

What makes this job different from an ordinary leak

A frozen pipe first has to be found. Water does not come from the tap, but the ice can be anywhere between the well pump and the tap, and it is often where the pipe passes through a wall, under a floor or through a shallower section of ground. The way of thawing depends on the material: a metal pipe and a plastic pipe are not heated the same way, and plastic deforms if overheated.

The other difference is that a burst shows up only after thawing. That is why, after thawing, the system is filled slowly and the whole section that was frozen is checked.

Frozen water supply underground

If the supply from the well or from the street has frozen, the problem is usually the depth or the insulation. The Latvian Building Code LBN 222-15 provides that external water pipes are laid at least 0.5 m deeper than the depth at which a 0 °C temperature is possible in the ground. For Riga, LBN 003-19 gives a frost depth in clay soil of 91 cm once in 10 years and 120 cm once in 100 years, and in sandy soil these values are multiplied by 1.2, because sand freezes deeper.

In practice the ground freezes shallower: observations show (LSM, 2025) that over the last 20 years it usually freezes to a depth of 40–60 cm and that a snow cover reduces the freezing depth by 20–40%. That is why on a driveway or path cleared of snow the ground freezes deeper, and it is exactly there that a supply laid shallower freezes first.

A pipe frozen in the ground is harder to thaw, and sometimes access has to be dug out. If the supply freezes every winter, in spring you consider whether to relay it deeper, insulate it or fit a heating cable.

LBN 222-15 “Water supply structures” (Cabinet Regulation No. 326), clause 164, applies to external networks. LBN 003-19 “Building Climatology” (Cabinet Regulation No. 432), Table 15 of the annex and its note 2. Checked 13.11.2026.

Pipe burst from frost

When water freezes, its density decreases by about 9%, the ice takes up more space, and a full pipe or tap can burst. The maker Uponor states that a PEX pipe usually withstands freezing, but repeated freezing weakens it.

A burst section is cut out and replaced, or the damaged joint is replaced. If the burst is in a wall or floor or the pipe is worn in many places, that is already pipe replacement work: see the page pipe repair.

Drains frozen

A drain outlet can also freeze, most often in the yard section between the house and the inspection chamber or septic tank, if it is shallow or if water stands in the pipe because the fall is wrong. Then water does not drain from the sink or shower even though there is no blockage. Such a line is thawed from the inspection point or the chamber, and afterwards we check that the pipe is not cracked and that it has a fall.

Heating cable for a water pipe

Where a pipe cannot be buried deeper or insulated enough, a self-regulating heating cable is fitted. For example, for the DEVIpipeheat 10 V3 cable the maker Danfoss DEVI states about 10 W/m at 10 °C and requires a thermostat and a residual-current device with a trip current of no more than 30 mA. This model may also be fitted inside the pipe (water up to 23 °C, pressure up to 10 bar). The earlier V2 model is not approved for that, so check the exact cable chosen.

The maker nVent Raychem also requires 30 mA residual-current protection for its frost protection cables. The cable is laid along the pipe by the plumber, and the connection to the electrical supply is made by an electrician.

How much thawing pipes costs

The price of thawing frozen pipes depends on where the pipe has frozen (indoors, in the cellar or underground), on the pipe material (metal or plastic) and on whether digging or opening a wall is needed. Fixing a leak in an accessible place costs from €40, replacing pipe along a wall or floor from €10/m, and a call-out with an inspection from €40. Replacing a supply that has frozen or burst underground depends on the job: it is set by the length, the depth, the digging and the soil.

Frequently asked questions

Can a frozen pipe be heated with a hairdryer?

The sources differ here: the UK's WaterSafe and CCW allow a hairdryer on a warm setting, while Affinity Water does not recommend it. All these guidelines forbid an open flame. The gentlest heat is hot water bottles or a warmed cloth, starting from the tap side, with the tap open.

How deep should a water supply from a well be buried?

For external water pipes LBN 222-15 requires them to lie at least 0.5 m below the depth at which the ground can reach 0 °C. For Riga the standard frost depth in clay soil is 91–120 cm, deeper in sand. The exact depth for a specific place is set by the designer.

The water is not running, but I cannot see anything burst. What should I do?

Close the inlet, open the tap and do not leave the pump running without water. A burst usually shows up only when the ice melts, so after thawing check the whole section.
