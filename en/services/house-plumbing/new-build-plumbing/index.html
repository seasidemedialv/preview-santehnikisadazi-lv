If a house or flat was handed over at shell finish, the outlets are already there, and you need to check that they are where the fixtures will be. If you are building yourself, the outlets have to be built and pressure tested before the walls are closed. Internal plumbing for a new house costs from €55 per outlet; labour only, materials are extra.

Shell finish: what to check before fixtures are fitted

  • Whether every outlet has a fixture opposite it on the plan and whether the heights work. In residential buildings the Latvian Building Code LBN 221-15 usually places the top edge of a hand basin at 800 mm, a wall-mounted mixer tap 200 mm above the basin edge and a bath tap 800 mm from the floor. The tolerance is ±20 mm.
  • Whether the outlets have the right diameters: 100 mm for a toilet, 40 mm for a shower and a kitchen sink, 32 mm for a hand basin.
  • Whether the water outlets are capped and were not damaged during plastering.
  • Whether there is room for a water heater and a manifold and access to them.

If an outlet has to be moved, it is a new outlet: building a water or drain outlet costs from €35 per outlet.

A house you build yourself: outlets before the walls are closed

The water supply and the drains are built while the walls and floors are still open. LBN 221-15 then provides for a pressure test of the installed water supply, flushing with drinking-quality water and, if needed, disinfection; the test pressure and method are stated in the construction design, and LVS EN 806-4 can be used.

For a water test the maker Uponor gives 11 bar for 30 minutes with no pressure drop and then 5.5 bar for 120 minutes, after disconnecting expansion vessels and safety valves that do not withstand the test pressure. Internal drains can be tested with air: the British Approved Document H gives a water-gauge pressure of 38 mm for at least 3 minutes, and at least 25 mm of water must remain in every trap.

Documents a new build needs

The General Building Regulations provide that the responsible construction manager organises the acceptance of completed work and, if needed, photographs the work before it is covered. The Building Regulations for buildings provide that the client and the contractor agree before the work starts on which jobs, for example covered utilities, an acceptance certificate is drawn up. When a building is accepted into service, a construction log, utility readiness statements and equipment test records are also submitted.

If a new build has a septic tank or a treatment plant, in Ropaži Municipality it has to be registered before the building is put into service, in Riga within a month after it is built. In Ādaži Municipality systems are registered with the agency “Carnikavas komunālserviss”.

Cabinet Regulation No. 500 (sub-points 100.6 and 100.7), Cabinet Regulation No. 529 (point 125.1, point 167), Ropaži Municipality binding regulations No. 45/22, Riga City Council binding regulations No. 66, Ādaži Municipality binding regulations No. 3/2025. Checked 13.11.2026.

First start-up

  1. Filling and bleeding The system is filled slowly, opening the taps one at a time so that the air can escape.
  2. Water heater The water heater is filled before the electricity is switched on. The cold water supply must have a safety valve, and its outlet must be open and point down.
  3. Hydrophore If the water comes from a well, the pre-charge pressure of the tank (the hydrophore is a pump with a pressure tank) is set while it is empty. The maker Grundfos advises 0.2 bar below the pump cut-in pressure.
  4. Hot water check LBN 221-15 states that hot water at the outlet must be 55–70 °C and must flow within 20 seconds after the tap is opened.

Frequently asked questions

The developer has already built the outlets. Can I move them?

Usually you can, if there is enough room for the fall of the drain. Each moved outlet is a new outlet from €35.

When should the pressure test be done?

Before the pipes are covered in a wall, a floor or behind cladding. After that, finding and repairing a fault is much harder.

Does the water supply in a new build have to be flushed?

Yes. LBN 221-15 provides for flushing the installed water supply with drinking-quality water and, if needed, disinfecting it.

Do you also install underfloor heating?

Yes, water underfloor heating: water underfloor heating installation. Electric underfloor heating is an electrician's job.
