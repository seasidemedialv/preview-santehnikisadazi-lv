Where your house plumbing starts and ends

The Law on Water Management Services sets the ownership boundary between the water utility's network and the owner's. In Riga, for example, under regulations No. 17 the central water supply ends at the outer wall of the meter assembly chamber and the sewer at the outer wall of the control chamber, and beyond that the owner maintains the networks. Under LBN 221-15 the internal drains of a building run from the fixtures to the first inspection chamber outside the building.

If the water comes from your own well or borehole, the whole system is yours: the pump, the pipeline to the house, the hydrophore, the filters and everything after. That is exactly why in a house a plumber works with more parts than in a flat.

What sets the houses around Ādaži apart

In many villages the central networks cover only part of the territory. In Atari the water supply and sewer networks cover only part of the village, so residents mostly use their own water supply systems and septic tanks. In Divezeri there is no central water supply or sewer, and houses use borehole wells and local systems. That means hydrophores, filters and pipes from the well to the house, which have to be protected from frost.

Elsewhere the networks are coming closer. In Carnikava phase III a water supply and sewer have been built in several streets. In the southern part of Berģi more than 400 properties can connect under the Rīgas ūdens project, and in Bukulti a pressure sewer is planned in which each user pumps wastewater with a small pump station of their own. Former summer houses, such as Upmalas near Kadaga, are now often homes for the whole year, and their old pipes were not laid for winter.

When you need a whole-house job and when you do not

If one tap leaks or one sink is blocked, the whole house does not need looking at: choose the specific service from the list of plumbing services. A whole-house job is needed if you are building or rebuilding, if the house becomes a home for the whole year, if you are buying or selling a house, or if you want to check the parts once a year before they fail.

A plumber for a house: connecting to the central network

If a new network has been built on your street, the plumber's part is the house side: from the meter assembly or control chamber to the house and inside it. In Ādaži Municipality the applicant builds the connection and the commercial metering assembly at their own expense under the supervision of SIA “Ādažu ūdens”. Under the municipality's binding regulations No. 72/2022 the operator issues the technical conditions free of charge within 20 working days, and after new main networks are handed over, in areas planned for the central system you have to connect within one year.

In Ropaži Municipality regulations No. 34/23 provide co-funding of 50% for a connection of a residential house: up to €2,000 for the water supply and up to €3,000 for the sewer, no more than €60 per metre. Internal networks are not funded. The co-funding of the former Carnikava Municipality was cancelled from 10 May 2022. In Riga, under regulations No. RD-23-207-sn, the co-funding for a private house sewer connection is €1,000, 1,500 or 2,000, depending on the length.

What to write in the form

  • which of the four jobs interests you;
  • the type and area of the house, the number of floors, whether there is a basement;
  • the water source: central water supply or your own well, borehole;
  • the drains: central network, septic tank, holding tank or treatment plant;
  • how many fixtures there are or will be: sinks, toilets, showers, a bath, a washing machine, a water heater.

You cannot send a photo or a plan through the form, so describe the house in text. If you have a design, write that you have one.

What often goes wrong in a house

  • The internal drains do not have the right fall. For pipes up to 50 mm LBN 221-15 requires 3 cm per metre, for 75–100 mm pipes 2 cm per metre, and the fall must not change between connections.
  • Fixtures in a basement that are lower than the cover of the nearest inspection chamber are connected to the common outlet. They need a separate outlet and a device that prevents the basement from flooding from the external sewer.
  • The drains have no ventilation, and the traps are sucked empty. The ventilation riser above a pitched roof ends at least 0.5 m high and must not be connected to room ventilation.
  • Pipes from the well to the house are laid shallow, and they freeze in winters when there is little snow on the ground.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with houses that have their own well?

Yes. The house side is our work: the pipeline from the well or borehole to the house, the hydrophore, the filters and the internal plumbing. Drilling a well or borehole is a different job.

Do you also build a septic tank?

No. We build the internal drains and the outlet from the house. Installing and emptying a septic tank is the work of other specialists.

Where do I start if a summer house becomes a home for the whole year?

With an inspection: what the pipes are, whether they are in the warm zone, how the water comes from the well and where the drains run. Then you can decide what to change: plumbing installation in a house.

Does Ropaži Municipality co-funding also cover internal work?

No. Regulations No. 34/23 co-fund the connection, but internal networks are not funded. The conditions also include a declared place of residence in the municipality for at least 6 months and a main network at the property.
