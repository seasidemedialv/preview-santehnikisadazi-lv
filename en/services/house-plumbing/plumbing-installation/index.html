Plumbing installation in a house - from the incoming supply to the fixtures

Plumbing installation in a house in Ādaži and the Riga Region is the whole water and drain route of the house in one job.

It covers the water supply from a well or the central network, the incoming supply assembly, pipes to every fixture and the internal drains to the outlet from the house. This job is done when rebuilding an old house, turning a summer house into a home for the whole year or connecting to a new network. Each outlet costs from €35; labour only, materials are extra.

What plumbing installation includes

  • The incoming water supply: the pipe from the well, borehole or meter assembly to the house and its entry through the foundations.
  • The incoming supply assembly: a shut-off valve, a filter, a pressure reducer where needed and, with your own well, also a hydrophore (a pump with a pressure tank).
  • Internal water pipes: cold and hot water to every fixture, with or without a manifold.
  • A water heater connection with a safety valve.
  • Internal drains: from the fixtures to the first inspection chamber outside the building, with ventilation and cleanouts.

Drilling a well or borehole, a septic tank and the electrical installation are not part of this job. Connecting the outlet of the house to a septic tank is described in connecting house drains to a septic tank.

The water pipe from the well to the house

A pipe in the ground has to be below the frost line. The Latvian Building Code LBN 222-15 requires an outdoor water pipe to be buried at least 0.5 m deeper than the depth at which the ground can reach 0 °C. For Riga, LBN 003-19 gives a standard frost depth for clay ground of 59 cm once in 2 years, 91 cm once in 10 years and 120 cm once in 100 years; for sandy ground these values are multiplied by 1.2. Adding 0.5 m, in the Riga area this comes to about 1.1–1.7 m in clay and up to about 1.9 m in sand. This is a calculation, not a quotation from the standard.

At the pump, LBN 221-15 requires a non-return valve, shut-off fittings and a pressure gauge on the discharge line, and shut-off fittings and a pressure gauge on the suction line. A compact pump unit with a hydrophore of up to 50 l may be placed right at the point of use. You talk through the digging scope separately, because it is not in the per-metre price. About pumps: hydrophores.

Connecting to the central water supply: the house side

In Ādaži Municipality the applicant builds the connection and the commercial metering assembly at their own expense under the supervision of SIA “Ādažu ūdens”. Under the municipality's binding regulations No. 72/2022 the operator issues the technical conditions free of charge within 20 working days, and they are valid for at least a year. If the meter cannot be placed in the standard spot, it can be placed in a heated room no further than 2 m from the outer wall. Rainwater may be discharged into the sewer only with the operator's permission.

Ādaži Municipality binding regulations No. 72/2022 “Procedure for Public Water Management Services in Ādaži Municipality” (amended by binding regulations No. 11/2026). Checked 13.11.2026.

Inside the house, after switching from a well to the network, the pressure often has to be checked. LBN 221-15 states that at the lowest outlet it must not exceed 6 bar, and a pressure regulator on the incoming supply is placed after the meter assembly and the shut-off fittings.

Building the internal drains

Under LBN 221-15 the internal drains have to be built with a set fall: 3 cm per metre for pipes up to 50 mm, 2 cm per metre for 75–100 mm pipes. The minimum connection diameter is 100 mm for a toilet, 40 mm for a bath, shower, kitchen sink and washing machine or dishwasher, and 32 mm for a hand basin. Cleanouts are needed at the bottom and top of risers, at the start of branches if three or more fixtures are connected, and at turns.

An outlet from a basement needs a fall of at least 2 cm per metre. Where it crosses the foundations, a gap of 0.2 m is left around the pipe and filled with an elastic material that is impermeable to water and gases, and in wet ground a sealing sleeve. The drains are ventilated through a riser above the roof.

How the installation is done

  1. The list of outlets Where each fixture will be, where the incoming supply, the water heater, the hydrophore, the manifold and the outlet from the house go.
  2. Building the pipes The water supply and drains before the walls and floors are closed.
  3. Pressure test The maker KAN-therm advises a preliminary test at 1–4 bar, then 30 minutes at the test pressure and 30 minutes at half that with no drop, with the pressure gauge at the lowest point.
  4. Flushing and fixtures The water supply is flushed with drinking-quality water, then the fixtures and the water heater are connected.

How much does plumbing installation in a house cost?

Each water (cold or hot) or drain outlet to a fixture costs from €35 per outlet, pipe installation from €10/m. In a new house, where the outlet consists of cold and hot water and a drain from the manifold or riser, an outlet costs from €55. Chasing, digging, fixture fitting and materials are extra. The incoming supply line in the yard from the well or the network depends on the job: it is set by the length, depth, digging and the ground. All prices: prices.

Frequently asked questions

Do you also build the water pipe from the well to the house?

Yes: the pipeline, its entry into the house and the pump connection. Drilling a well or borehole and digging machinery are a separate matter that you talk through on the call.

Do you also build drains outside?

The internal drains reach the first inspection chamber outside the building. The yard line to the septic tank or the network can be discussed, but building a septic tank is not our work.

Is a design needed?

That depends on the building and the connection. A connection to the central network needs the technical conditions of the water utility. If you have a design, write it in the form.

What should I do with the old plumbing in a summer house?

First find out which pipes are in the warm zone and which are not. Rooms with cold water pipes must be warmer than 2 °C, otherwise the pipes must be protected from frost or moved.
