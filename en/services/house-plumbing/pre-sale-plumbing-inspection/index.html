Plumbing inspection before selling or buying

A plumbing inspection before a sale in Ādaži and the Riga Region shows what works in the house and what will soon need repair: pipes, signs of leaks, the water heater, the hydrophore (a pump with a pressure tank), drains and heating.

For the seller it helps to fix small things before viewings, for the buyer to understand what work awaits after the purchase. The call-out costs from €40, the hourly rate from €25/h.

What this inspection is and is not

It is a plumber's visit with simple checks: taps are opened, the pressure and pipes are looked at, water is run into the drains. It is not a structural survey, a valuation of the house or a legal check, and it does not replace servicing a septic tank or treatment plant. What exactly to check, you talk through on the call, because it differs for a summer house and a new house.

What is checked on the water side

  • The water source and incoming supply: central network or your own well, borehole, where the main stopcock is and whether it closes.
  • The pipe material. Old galvanised steel pipes rust from the inside, and their signs are low pressure and brownish water.
  • Signs of leaks: bubbling paint, stains, mould, a musty smell, the sound of running water; in the yard damp patches, a sunken lawn, unusually green grass in dry weather.
  • Pressure drop: all taps are closed and the pressure gauge is watched. If the pressure drops within a few hours, a leak is possible.
  • The water heater: if the safety valve drips when the water heater is not heating and nobody is using water, the pressure is too high or the valve is faulty.
  • The hydrophore: whether the pump starts by itself without water being used and whether the tank has a pre-charge pressure.

What is checked in the drains and the heating

  • Water is run into all fixtures at the same time: it should drain without gurgling and smell.
  • Traps in rarely used rooms: if they have dried out, the smell is not a fault, but you need to know about it.
  • The yard inspection chamber: whether it can be reached and whether water is standing in it.
  • Heating: the pressure when cold is usually 1–2 bar; a radiator that is colder at the top than at the bottom usually has air in it, but one that is warm at the top and cold at the bottom points to sludge.

The septic tank and documents when selling

If the house has a septic tank, a holding tank or a treatment plant, a sale is a change that has to be reported to the municipal register. In Ādaži Municipality changes have to be reported to the agency “Carnikavas komunālserviss” within a month at the latest, in Ropaži Municipality to SIA “Garkalnes inženiertīkli” within a month, in Riga to the Housing and Environment Department, also within a month.

It is worth a buyer asking for the septic tank emptying records: the owner has to keep them for at least 2 years, and they show the address, the quantity removed, the date and the details of the contractor. In Ādaži Municipality a septic tank has to be emptied at least once a year, pumping out at least 70% of its volume.

Ādaži Municipality binding regulations No. 3/2025, Ropaži Municipality binding regulations No. 45/22, Riga City Council binding regulations No. 66, Cabinet Regulation No. 384. Checked 13.11.2026.

A house left empty in winter

If the house has been drained for the winter, the system has to be filled for the inspection. After standing for a long time it is flushed: remove the tap aerators and shower hoses, flush all the cold water first, then the hot, starting from the tap nearest the incoming supply and moving to the furthest. If water has been left in the pipes in an unheated house, after filling check whether any pipe has burst.

Frequently asked questions

Is the inspection also suitable before buying a flat?

Yes, with one difference: the risers in a block of flats belong to the whole building, so ask the property manager about them. In a flat the pipes from the riser, the fixtures, the water heater and signs of leaks are checked.

Does the inspection replace a building surveyor's report?

No. It covers only the plumbing. Structures, the roof, the electrics and the value of the house are assessed by other specialists.

What is worth fixing before viewings?

Dripping taps, leaking cisterns, leaks at joints and dried-out traps. The buyer sees them first, and they are cheap jobs.

Can the drains be checked with a camera?

In this inspection the drains are judged by how they drain and by the inspection chamber. If a damaged line is suspected, it is worth talking about that separately.
