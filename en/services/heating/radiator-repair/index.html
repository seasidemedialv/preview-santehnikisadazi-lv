Radiator repair in Ādaži - when a radiator leaks or stays cold

Radiator repair in Ādaži and the Riga region usually means one of three things: a valve or joint leaks, the radiator heats only partly or not at all, or a thermostatic valve or head has to be replaced.

What to do if a radiator leaks

  1. Catch the water Put a container or a towel under the leak and move anything the water could damage off the floor.
  2. Close the valves Close the radiator valve and, if there is one, the shut-off valve on the other side. Turn the thermostatic head to zero or the star symbol if the valve handle is not accessible.
  3. Look at the pressure gauge If the pressure drops sharply, water is being lost faster than drip by drip. Write down what the gauge shows.
  4. Fill in the form Write where it leaks: at the valve, at the bleed valve, at a joint or from the radiator body itself.

What goes wrong in a radiator and what is done about it

Sign Most common cause What is done
Dripping at the valve stem or nut a worn seal or a loose joint the seal is tightened or replaced, the valve too if needed
A damp patch on the radiator surface corrosion in the radiator body a hole in the body is usually not patched, the radiator is replaced
Top colder than the bottom air in the radiator bleeding and topping up the pressure
Bottom cold, top warm sludge flushing the radiator or the whole system
The whole radiator is cold a closed or seized valve, the pin of the thermostatic valve checking the valve, replacing the thermostatic head or the valve
Several radiators farther along are cold the circulation pump or an unbalanced system checking the pump, adjusting the valves

If the radiator body has rusted through, patching is usually not durable, and then radiator replacement is the more sensible option.

Fitting thermostatic heads and valves

A thermostatic valve consists of two parts: the valve body at the radiator connection, and the head that senses the room temperature and presses the valve pin. The head can be replaced without draining the system. To replace the valve body, the radiator is shut off or part of the system is drained.

If the old radiator has an ordinary manual valve, a thermostatic head cannot be fitted: the valve has to be replaced with a thermostatic one first. If a radiator does not heat after summer although the head has been turned back on, the valve pin has often seized. You can try to free it by removing the head, but a rusted valve is better replaced.

Bleeding radiators

If a radiator is colder at the top than at the bottom, it usually has air in it. The maker Worcester Bosch advises switching the heating off, letting the radiators cool, opening the bleed valve with a key until the hissing stops and water starts to flow steadily, and then closing it. After bleeding, the system pressure usually drops, so the pressure gauge should be checked and, if needed, the system topped up.

If air comes back every few weeks, bleeding treats the symptom. Air can get in through a leak, through a faulty automatic air vent, or with fresh water topped up too often. Then the cause is looked for, and the page heating system repair covers it.

Frequently asked questions

Can a leaking radiator be fixed without taking it off?

If a valve, seal or joint leaks, usually yes: the radiator is shut off and the faulty part is replaced. If the body itself leaks, the radiator is taken off and usually replaced.

Why is my radiator warm only at the top?

The maker Worcester Bosch states that a warm top and a cold bottom usually mean sludge, and then the system needs cleaning, not bleeding.

Can a thermostatic head be fitted to any radiator?

Only if the radiator has a thermostatic valve. If it has an ordinary manual valve, the valve is replaced first.
