If one room stays cold, the cause is usually the loop valve, an actuator or air; if all rooms stay cold, the mixing unit or its pump. Write in the form which rooms stay cold and since when, and on the call you talk through the job and the price.

Underfloor heating not working: where to start

Sign Most common cause What is checked
One room is cold a closed or seized loop valve, a faulty actuator, the room thermostat the flow meter on the manifold, the actuator, the thermostat
A loop heats only partly air in the loop or too little flow bleeding at the manifold, checking the pre-setting
No room is warm the mixing unit pump, the mixing valve or the heat source whether the pump turns, what the flow temperature is
Uneven heat across the house unbalanced loops, deposits in the meters balancing the loops, cleaning the meters
Pressure drops, a damp patch on the floor a leak in a loop or a joint pressure testing loop by loop, finding the leak

Underfloor heating manifold repair

The manifold is the place where almost everything can be seen and changed. Each loop has a flow meter that shows whether water is flowing, and a valve that often carries an actuator. The actuator opens the valve when the room thermostat calls for heat. If the motor has failed or the valve pin has seized after summer, the loop stays cold although the others are warm.

Over time the glass of a flow meter turns dark or collects deposits, and then it reads wrongly or blocks the flow. The meters can be replaced separately, but if the deposits are throughout the system, heating system flushing helps.

Underfloor heating mixing unit replacement

The mixing unit blends hot water from the heat source with cooled return water, so that only as warm a flow as the floor needs enters it. It has its own circulation pump and a mixing valve or thermostat. If the pump does not run or the mixing valve has seized, the whole floor stays cold while the radiators in the same house work.

Not every pump noise means replacement. For the ALPHA2 pump the maker Grundfos states that it bleeds itself, noise caused by air goes away after a few minutes of running, but the pump must not run dry. If the pump does not run even after that, it or the whole mixing unit is replaced.

Underfloor heating adjustment and balancing

If the floor heats but some rooms are hot and others cool, the loops are not balanced. The maker Danfoss states that each manifold valve is preset from a diagram, by turning the red ring to the required mark. Without the design diagram the setting is adjusted according to the loop lengths and how the rooms warm up.

A floor that is too hot is not wanted either. The standard EN 1264 limits the floor surface temperature to 29 °C in the occupied zone and 35 °C in the edge zone, and usually to 33 °C in bathrooms (Uponor technical materials).

A leak in the floor and frost damage

If the system pressure drops and a damp or dark patch appears on the floor, a loop may be leaking. First, at the manifold the loops are shut off one by one, watching after which one the pressure stops dropping. That identifies the damaged loop, and only then is the floor opened at the specific spot, not across the whole room.

In an unheated summer house the water in the loops can freeze. The maker Uponor states that PEX pipes must be protected from frost: repeated freezing weakens the pipe, and PEX pipe embedded in concrete without a protective sleeve can be pierced when it freezes. How to prepare a house for winter is on the page plumbing winterising.

How much underfloor heating repair costs

The price of adjusting or repairing an underfloor heating manifold depends on the number of circuits, on whether flow meters, actuators or a pump have to be replaced, on whether a pressure test is needed. It also depends on whether the fault is in the floor, where a leak has to be found. A plumber call-out with inspection and diagnosis is from €40, and the hourly rate is from €25/h. Frequently asked questions

Do you also repair electric underfloor heating?

No, electric underfloor heating is an electrician's job. We repair water underfloor heating: the loops, the manifold and the mixing unit.

Can I bleed the underfloor heating loops myself?

If the manifold has bleed valves, it can be done much like a radiator. Afterwards check the pressure gauge, because the pressure usually drops. If the air comes back, the cause is looked for.

Can a damaged loop be fixed without opening the whole floor?

Usually the floor is opened only at the leak, if it can be located precisely. That is why you first find out loop by loop which one leaks.

Why does the floor not heat after summer?

Often the valve pins or actuators that have not been moved all summer seize, or the mixing unit pump does not start.
