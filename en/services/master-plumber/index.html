How to choose a plumber

  • The prices are public, and next to each it is clear what it includes and what is extra: materials, chasing, removing the old fixture, the travel charge.
  • Before the work you are told how the job will be done and what will be done if the first method does not help. For example, a blockage is cleared with a drain snake; if that does not help, the line is cleaned with high-pressure jetting, and that is a different price item.
  • You know whether the call-out charge is included in the labour price if the work is done right away.
  • If the work involves a connection to the central network, the plumber knows that a connection needs the technical conditions of the water utility. In Ādaži Municipality they are issued by SIA “Ādažu ūdens”.
  • You are not offered work that is not needed. For a dripping tap, replacing the cartridge is usually enough, not a new mixer tap.

A good plumber asks a lot before setting out. If a price is named without asking anything about the house, the water source and where the damage is, it is not yet a price but a guess.

What to say before the call-out

  • What is happening and since when.
  • The type of home: a house, a summer house, a semi-detached house or a flat.
  • The water source: central water supply or your own well, borehole with a hydrophore.
  • The drains: central network, septic tank or holding tank.
  • Where the problem is and whether it can be reached without opening a wall or floor.
  • Whether there are other jobs to do at the same time.

You cannot attach a photo to the form, so describe it in words. Several jobs in one call-out are usually better value than each one separately.

What a master plumber does not do

Emptying and installing septic tanks, drilling boreholes and wells, gas equipment, water meters, electrical installation and tiling are the work of other specialists. For example, we install and connect water heaters with a safety valve, but the electrical connection is a separate job. On the drains side the plumber works from the fixtures to the outlet from the house and the yard line to the chamber or septic tank, not on the septic tank itself.

How to check that the work was done properly

  • After the work, turn on the water and look at the joints: they must not drip even after an hour.
  • If pipes were replaced or built, the water supply must be pressure tested before it is covered in a wall.
  • After drain work, run water into all the fixtures at the same time: it should drain without gurgling and smell.
  • After water heater work, check that the safety valve outlet is open and points down.

If something does not match what was agreed, say so right away, while the master plumber is still with you.

How much does a master plumber cost?

A plumber call-out costs from €40: the master plumber's arrival, inspection and diagnosis. If the work is done right away, the call-out is usually included in the labour price. The hourly rate is from €25/h, and the minimum call-out charge is usually one hour.

Many jobs are priced per item: installing or replacing a mixer tap from €25, installing or replacing a toilet from €50, installing or replacing a water heater from €70. All prices are labour only; materials are extra. All prices: plumbing prices.

Frequently asked questions

Do I pay for the call-out separately if the work is done right away?

Usually not: if the work is done right away, the call-out is usually included in the labour price. If the master plumber only inspects and diagnoses the problem, the call-out costs from €40.

Can I call a master plumber for several jobs at once?

Yes. Write all the jobs in the form, including small ones, for example a dripping tap and a leaking cistern. Then on the call you can talk through the whole job and the price.

Do you also work in flats?

Yes, also in flats. In a block of flats, shutting off the riser goes through the building manager, and it is separate from the plumber's work.
